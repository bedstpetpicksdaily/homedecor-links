Frequently Asked Questions

Does the linen shade yellow over time?

Not in my experience over three months, though I keep it out of direct afternoon sun exposure. Linen naturally ages with character, but this particular shade maintains its neutral tone. If it's in a south-facing window getting 6+ hours of direct sun daily, you'll see some gradual warming. That's linen being linen, not a manufacturing defect.

What's the actual light output compared to a standard 60-watt equivalent bulb?

The shade diffuses light warmly, so it won't brighten a dark corner like an exposed bulb would. This is ideal for bedside or accent lighting, less ideal as your only light source in a dim home office. I use it alongside a larger overhead fixture when I need true task lighting. For ambient evening lighting or reading in bed, it's perfect.

Is the ceramic base actually stable, or does it feel tippy?

It's genuinely stable. The weight distribution is intentional—the base is wide enough relative to the height that even a 30-pound toddler couldn't tip it over with reasonable contact. If you have cats that enjoy batting things, that's your actual risk factor, not the design itself.

How difficult is it to replace the bulb?

Simple. The shade lifts off without tools, and standard bulb installation takes literal seconds. No fussy fixtures or confusing design here—it's refreshingly straightforward, which matters when you need to swap bulbs at 10 PM.
