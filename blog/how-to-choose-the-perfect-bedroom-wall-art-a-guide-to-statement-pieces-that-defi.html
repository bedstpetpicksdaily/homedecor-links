Understand Your Space and Dimensions

Before you start shopping for bedroom wall art, take time to assess your room's layout and wall space. Measure the wall where you want to hang your piece—larger bedrooms can accommodate oversized canvas prints, while smaller spaces may benefit from medium-sized framed art. Consider the wall's prominence: is it the first thing you see when entering the room? Is it positioned above your bed or beside a window?

Big Wall Decor offers a range of sizes in their collection, from substantial statement pieces to more proportionate options, making it easy to find large wall art that fits your specific space perfectly. The key is choosing artwork that doesn't overwhelm the room or get lost against expansive wall space.

Select a Color Palette That Complements Your Bedroom

The colors in your bedroom wall decor should harmonize with your existing furniture, bedding, and overall color scheme. If your room features neutral tones, consider bold abstract art or vibrant oversized canvas prints as a focal point. For bedrooms with colorful schemes, you might choose artwork that echoes or complements those hues without competing for attention.

Big Wall Decor's diverse selection includes everything from serene, muted tones to striking, energetic abstract art. Whether you're looking for statement wall pieces that pop or subtle designs that create ambiance, their curated collection of big wall decor options provides plenty of choices to match your aesthetic.

Consider the Style and Theme of Your Bedroom

Your wall art should align with your bedroom's overall design theme. Modern bedrooms pair beautifully with contemporary abstract art and minimalist framed art. Traditional spaces benefit from classic landscapes or vintage-inspired pieces. Eclectic rooms can showcase bold, artistic statement pieces that reflect multiple influences.

When browsing bedroom wall decor options, think about what inspires you and what you want to feel when you're in your space. Large wall art has the power to set the mood—whether that's calming, energizing, or creative. Explore Big Wall Decor's living room wall art and bedroom wall decor collections, as many statement pieces work beautifully in either space and can inspire your design choices.

Balance Scale and Proportion

One of the biggest mistakes people make is choosing wall art that's too small for the space. Oversized canvas prints and big wall decor create impact and make a statement. As a general rule, your artwork should cover 50-75% of the wall space above furniture or in your chosen area. Large wall art instantly elevates a room and prevents it from feeling sparse.

Frequently Asked Questions

What size wall art is best for a bedroom?

For most bedrooms, oversized canvas prints or large wall art measuring 40x60 inches or larger work best, especially as statement pieces. Big Wall Decor specializes in these proportions to ensure your artwork makes a bold impact without overwhelming the space.

How do I choose between abstract art and other styles?

Consider your bedroom's existing décor and your personal preferences. Abstract art works well as a statement piece in modern or eclectic spaces, while landscapes and traditional framed art suit classic or transitional bedrooms. Browse Big Wall Decor's collections to see what resonates with your style.

Can I use the same wall art in different rooms?

Absolutely! Many statement pieces from Big Wall Decor's living room wall art collection work beautifully in bedrooms, and vice versa. The key is choosing artwork that complements each space's color palette and design theme.

How do I properly hang large wall art?

Center oversized canvas prints at eye level, typically 57-60 inches from the floor to the center of the piece. For pieces above beds, hang them 12-18 inches above the headboard. Consider using a stud finder or wall anchors rated for the weight of large wall decor.

Where can I find affordable big wall decor?

Big Wall Decor offers a wide selection of large wall art, framed art, and abstract art at competitive prices. They regularly feature statement wall pieces across different price points to accommodate various budgets.
