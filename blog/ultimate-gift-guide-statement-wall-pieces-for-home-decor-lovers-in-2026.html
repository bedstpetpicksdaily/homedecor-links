Frequently Asked Questions

What size statement wall piece should I choose?

Measure your wall space and consider the room's proportions. For large living rooms, oversized canvas prints (48x60 inches or larger) work well, while smaller bedrooms benefit from medium-sized pieces (24x36 to 36x48 inches). Big Wall Decor offers a range of sizes to fit any space.

Are Big Wall Decor's canvas prints fade-resistant?

Yes, Big Wall Decor uses high-quality printing techniques and materials designed to resist fading. Their oversized canvas prints maintain vibrant colors for years, making them a worthwhile investment for any home decor enthusiast.

Can I return a statement wall piece if my recipient doesn't like it?

Most retailers, including Big Wall Decor, offer return policies for wall art. Check their specific return guidelines before purchasing to ensure you have options if the piece doesn't meet expectations.

What's the difference between canvas prints and framed art from Big Wall Decor?

Canvas prints offer a modern, gallery-style look and are stretched over wooden frames. Framed art pieces feature traditional framing with glass or acrylic, providing a more classic, formal aesthetic. Both are excellent statement wall pieces depending on décor preference.
