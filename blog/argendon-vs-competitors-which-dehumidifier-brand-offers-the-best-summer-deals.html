Why Dehumidifier Selection Matters for Your Home

Excess humidity isn't just uncomfortable—it can damage your property, promote mold growth, and negatively impact indoor air quality. The ideal indoor humidity level ranges from 30-50%, and maintaining this balance is crucial for protecting your home's structural integrity and your family's health. A quality dehumidifier is an essential investment, but choosing the right one means considering factors like capacity, noise level, energy efficiency, and warranty support.

Frequently Asked Questions

What size dehumidifier do I need for my basement?

Dehumidifier capacity is measured in pints per day (PPD). For basements up to 500 sq ft with moderate moisture, a 30-50 PPD unit suffices. Larger or very humid basements need 70+ PPD. Argendon offers sizing guides for their basement dehumidifiers to help you choose correctly.

How much can I save with Argendon's summer deals?

Current Argendon summer promotions typically offer 15-25% off select models, plus additional rebates for commercial purchases. Bundle deals combining basement and crawl space units can yield even greater savings. Check their official site for current promotion details.

Are Argendon dehumidifiers energy efficient?

Yes. Argendon models feature Energy Star certification and advanced compressor technology that reduces power consumption by 20-30% compared to standard units. This translates to lower electricity bills, especially during humid summer months when dehumidifiers run frequently.

What warranty does Argendon offer?

Argendon provides comprehensive warranties ranging from 2-5 years depending on the model, covering parts and labor. Their customer support team is responsive and helpful, making warranty claims and service straightforward.

Can I use an Argendon dehumidifier in crawl spaces?

Absolutely. Argendon specifically manufactures crawl space dehumidifiers designed to operate in tight, humid spaces. These units feature corrosion-resistant construction and are engineered to handle the unique challenges of crawl space environments.
