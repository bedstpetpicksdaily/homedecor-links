Design Tips for Displaying Custom Photo Home Decor

When incorporating custom photo home decor into your summer refresh, consider these design principles: balance negative space to avoid overcrowding, choose a consistent color palette that complements your existing décor, and ensure proper lighting to showcase your pieces.
