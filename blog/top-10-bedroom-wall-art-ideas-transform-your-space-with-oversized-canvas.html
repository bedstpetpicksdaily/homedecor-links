Frequently Asked Questions

What size oversized canvas should I choose for my bedroom?

The ideal size depends on your wall space and bedroom dimensions. For walls above a bed, consider 40x60 inches or larger. For accent walls, 48x72 inches or bigger creates dramatic impact. Big Wall Decor offers sizing guides to help you select the perfect dimensions for your space.

How do I hang heavy oversized canvas prints?

Use heavy-duty wall anchors, studs, or professional-grade hanging hardware. Most Big Wall Decor pieces come with hanging instructions and hardware. For very large wall art, consider using two anchor points or consulting a professional installer.

Can abstract art work in traditional bedroom designs?

Yes! Abstract art with neutral tones, earth colors, or muted palettes can complement traditional bedrooms beautifully. Big Wall Decor's abstract collection includes pieces suitable for various design styles, not just modern interiors.

Are framed or canvas oversized prints better for bedrooms?

Both work well—it depends on your preference. Canvas prints offer a modern, gallery-like appearance, while framed art adds sophistication and protection. Big Wall Decor specializes in both formats, allowing you to choose based on your bedroom aesthetic.

How do I prevent oversized wall art from damaging my walls?

Use proper hanging hardware rated for your canvas weight, avoid drilling into valuable wall sections, and use protective wall anchors. Big Wall Decor provides detailed installation guidance to ensure safe, damage-free mounting of large wall art.
