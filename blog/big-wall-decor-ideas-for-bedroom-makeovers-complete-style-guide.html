Frequently Asked Questions

What size big wall decor should I choose for my bedroom?

For most bedrooms, oversized canvas prints between 36-48 inches work well above the bed. Consider your wall size and ceiling height—the piece should cover about 60-75% of the wall space above furniture. Big Wall Decor offers detailed sizing guides to help you choose the perfect dimensions.

Can abstract art work in a traditional bedroom?

Absolutely! Big Wall Decor offers abstract art in various color palettes and styles. Choose pieces with muted tones and traditional framing options to integrate modern wall art into classic bedroom designs seamlessly.

How do I arrange multiple pieces of big wall decor in my bedroom?

Create a gallery wall with consistent spacing of 2-3 inches between frames, or use a symmetrical arrangement with a larger statement piece centered among smaller artwork. Big Wall Decor's framed art options coordinate beautifully for cohesive displays.

Is it difficult to install oversized canvas prints from Big Wall Decor?

Most Big Wall Decor pieces come ready to hang with hardware included. For large statement wall pieces, use wall anchors rated for the weight, or locate studs for permanent installation. Professional installation is recommended for extra-large pieces.
