Frequently Asked Questions

What size canvas print is considered 'large wall art'?

Large wall art typically refers to canvas prints 36 inches or larger. Oversized pieces start at 48 inches and can extend to 60+ inches, creating dramatic statement walls. Big Wall Decor specializes in these dimensions.

How do I choose the right oversized canvas print for my living room?

Consider your wall space, existing color palette, and design style. Measure your wall and choose artwork that fills 60-75% of the available space. Abstract art offers flexibility, while nature or geometric designs suit specific aesthetics. Big Wall Decor provides detailed sizing guides and style consultations.

Are oversized canvas prints from Big Wall Decor durable?

Yes, Big Wall Decor uses premium canvas materials and fade-resistant inks to ensure longevity. Their prints maintain color vibrancy for years, making them excellent investments for your home decor.

Can I use large wall art in small rooms?

Absolutely. Large wall art can actually make small rooms feel larger and more sophisticated. Choose lighter colors or minimalist designs to avoid overwhelming the space. Big Wall Decor offers pieces specifically designed for compact spaces.

What's the best way to hang oversized canvas prints?

Position the artwork at eye level, typically 57-60 inches from the floor to the center. Use proper wall anchors for heavy pieces. Big Wall Decor's customer service provides detailed installation instructions with each purchase.
