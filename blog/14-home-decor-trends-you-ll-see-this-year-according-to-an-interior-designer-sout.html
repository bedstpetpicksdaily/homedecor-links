What Home Decorating Enthusiasts Should Know

  • Warm, natural tones dominate: Expect to see earthy neutrals, warm whites, and natural wood finishes replacing cool grays and stark minimalism.
  • Texture is king: Layering different textures—from linen to brass to ceramic—creates visual interest and a lived-in feel.

  • Botanical and nature-inspired elements: Plants, nature prints, and organic shapes bring life and calm to interiors.
  • Functional design: Decor that serves a purpose (like decorative storage baskets or multifunctional furniture) is increasingly prioritized.
  • Curved furniture: Rounded edges and curved silhouettes soften spaces compared to sharp, angular pieces.
