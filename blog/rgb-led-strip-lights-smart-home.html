Frequently Asked Questions

Can RGB LED strip lights work with Alexa and Google Home?

Yes, most smart RGB LED strip lights are compatible with both Alexa and Google Home through WiFi or Bluetooth connectivity. You can control brightness, color, and scheduling through voice commands or the respective apps, though some brands require a separate hub for full functionality.

How long do RGB LED strip lights last and are they energy efficient?

Quality RGB LED strips typically last 25,000-50,000 hours (3-5+ years with daily use) and consume significantly less energy than traditional lighting, using about 80% less power. This makes them both cost-effective long-term and better for reducing your home's energy consumption.

What's the difference between adhesive-back and clip-mount RGB LED strips?

Adhesive-back strips stick directly to surfaces and are better for clean installations but can be difficult to remove, while clip-mount strips attach to aluminum channels and are easier to reposition and replace. Adhesive strips work best on clean, smooth surfaces like drywall or cabinets, whereas clips suit edges and corners where repositioning might be needed.

How many meters of RGB LED strips do I need for a room?

For ambient lighting, measure the perimeter or specific areas you want to highlight (shelves, behind TV, under cabinets) and plan for 1-2 meters per accent zone; for full room coverage, multiply your room's perimeter by 1.5x to account for corners. Most strips come in 5-10 meter rolls, and you can connect multiple rolls together if needed.
