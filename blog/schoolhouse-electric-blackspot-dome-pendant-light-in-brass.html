Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Schoolhouse Electric Blackspot compare to similar brass dome pendants on Amazon?

The Schoolhouse Electric version has thicker materials and more refined brass finishing, but functionally similar pendants from Rivet, Brightech, and Schoolhouse's own budget line perform nearly identically. What size room or space is this pendant best for?

The standard Blackspot Dome works best over islands, dining areas, or as a single statement piece in entryways and bedrooms. It's too small for vaulted ceilings and too bright for direct reading without adjustment. Measure your space: this pendant shines (literally) in 8-12 foot ceiling heights with islands or tables 24-36 inches wide beneath it.

Does the brass finish tarnish or require special maintenance?

The brass will naturally patina over time, which most people love. If you prefer keeping it shiny, a simple dry cloth wipe weekly prevents buildup. Don't use brass polish—it strips the finish. The dome interior is usually powder-coated steel, so it won't tarnish there. This fixture is genuinely low-maintenance compared to unlacquered brass alternatives.
