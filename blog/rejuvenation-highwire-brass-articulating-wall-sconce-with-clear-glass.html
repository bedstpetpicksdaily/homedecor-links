Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Rejuvenation Highwire compare to cheaper brass articulating sconces on Amazon?

The primary difference is design pedigree and manufacturing precision. Rejuvenation sources quality brass and controls the finish; cheaper alternatives ($80-120) often have thinner brass, less consistent finishes, and weaker articulation mechanisms that loosen over time. You're looking at 3-5 years of reliable movement versus 10+ years with Rejuvenation. That said, if you're not certain you want an articulating sconce, the cheaper option lets you experiment without betting $250+ on an untested aesthetic.

Does this sconce work in modern farmhouse and contemporary spaces, or just mid-century decor?

The clear glass shade and brass finish work across multiple styles—modern farmhouse, contemporary, industrial, and transitional all accommodate this fixture well. The key is the room's overall color palette. In warm neutral spaces (creams, taupes, warm grays), brass feels cohesive. In cool-toned modern spaces with chrome fixtures, it clashes. Check your existing hardware finish before committing.

What bulb type should I use, and does it come with one?

The sconce typically arrives without a bulb. You'll want a quality LED or incandescent bulb rated for exposed/visible bulb applications—avoid bottom-market LED bulbs that flicker or have poor color rendering. A warm white (2700K) bulb in 40-60W equivalent gives you the soft mood lighting that makes brass fixtures shine. Budget $15-25 for a good bulb; it's not included in the price you'll see quoted.

How much wall space does the articulating arm actually take up when extended?

The arm extends roughly 12-16 inches from the wall depending on how far you pull it. In tight hallways or small bathrooms, this matters. Measure your wall before ordering, especially if you're placing it near a doorframe or mirror. The fixture works best when it has at least 18 inches of unobstructed wall space.

Is installation difficult if I don't have an existing wall sconce connection?

If you have an existing light fixture location with wiring already in place, installation is straightforward—30 minutes with basic tools. If you need new wiring run, hire an electrician; DIY electrical work isn't worth the risk. Budget $100-200 for electrical work if needed. This cost should factor into your total investment calculation.
