Frequently Asked Questions

Does this pendant work with standard light bulbs, or do I need something special?

It works with standard E26 base bulbs, which means you have flexibility. A 5–7W warm-white LED bulb (2700K) is the sweet spot—it mimics the color temperature that polished brass was designed around and avoids the harsh blue cast of daylight bulbs. If you go vintage Edison filament style, the warm glow is stunning, though technically less efficient. Just avoid anything over 8W unless you want a very bright focal point.

How does polished brass compare to matte or brushed brass for durability?

Polished brass shows fingerprints and dust more visibly, period. Brushed or matte finishes forgive neglect better cosmetically. However, polished brass develops a subtle patina over years if you leave it unlacquered, which many people find beautiful. Schoolhouse Electric typically applies a protective lacquer, so you're protecting shine at the cost of maintenance. If you hate polishing anything, choose brushed finishes instead—they're lower maintenance and still sophisticated.

Is this fixture bright enough for task lighting over a kitchen island or dining table?

Yes, with the right bulb. The dome's interior reflector surface is designed to direct light downward efficiently. A single 7W LED bulb provides adequate task lighting for most people over a 2–3 foot diameter work surface. If you need brighter light or have a very large island, pair it with under-cabinet lighting or add a second pendant. For dining tables where ambiance matters more than task work, one pendant is typically perfect.
