Frequently Asked Questions

What size dehumidifier do I need for my basement?

Argendon offers sizing guides based on square footage and moisture severity. For most basements (1,000-2,000 sq ft), a 70-pint Argendon dehumidifier works well. Consult their product specifications or customer support for personalized recommendations.

How often do I need to empty an Argendon dehumidifier?

Most Argendon models feature continuous drain options via built-in pumps, eliminating manual emptying. In manual mode, tank capacity ranges from 15-22 pints depending on the model and humidity levels in your home.

Are Argendon dehumidifiers noisy?

Argendon dehumidifiers operate at 45-55 decibels on average, comparable to a quiet conversation. Their whisper-quiet design makes them suitable for bedrooms and living areas without disruption.

Can Argendon dehumidifiers handle crawl spaces?

Yes, Argendon specializes in crawl space dehumidifiers with compact designs that fit tight spaces while delivering powerful humidity control, preventing mold and structural damage.

What is Argendon's warranty coverage?

Residential Argendon dehumidifiers typically come with 2-3 year warranties covering defects in materials and workmanship, with optional extended coverage available.
