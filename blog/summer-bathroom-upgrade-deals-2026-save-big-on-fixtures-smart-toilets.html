Frequently Asked Questions

What is a macerating toilet and why would I need one?

A macerating toilet grinds waste and paper into small particles before pumping them through small-diameter pipes to your main sewage line. They're perfect for basement bathrooms, guest houses, and spaces where traditional plumbing isn't feasible. Simple Project offers reliable models that operate quietly and efficiently.
