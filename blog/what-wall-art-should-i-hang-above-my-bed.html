What Wall Art Should I Hang Above My Bed?

The best wall art for above your bed depends on your bedroom style, but generally you want something that's visually balanced, complements your color scheme, and creates a calming focal point. Popular choices include large canvas prints, abstract art, landscape photography, botanical prints, and minimalist line drawings—all of which work well in bedroom settings.

The Short Answer

Hang wall art above your bed that matches your bedroom's color palette and design aesthetic while remaining visually calm and not overly stimulating. The ideal piece should be large enough to anchor the wall (typically 50-70% of your headboard width), positioned 8-12 inches above your headboard, and reflect your personal style whether that's modern, bohemian, traditional, or eclectic. Consider the mood you want to create—serene landscapes promote relaxation, while abstract art can add sophistication and personality. Avoid overly busy or chaotic designs that might interfere with restful sleep.

The Full Explanation

Size and Proportion Matter

The most critical factor in choosing bedroom wall art is getting the size right. Your artwork should be substantial enough to make an impact without overwhelming the space. For a standard queen or king bed, aim for a piece that's at least 40-60 inches wide, or use a gallery wall of multiple smaller pieces arranged cohesively. A small, undersized print will look lost above a bed and fail to create the focal point you're aiming for.

Color Palette Considerations

Your wall art should either complement or intentionally contrast with your existing bedroom colors. If your room features neutral walls and bedding, consider art with soft tones—blues, greens, grays, and earth tones naturally promote calm and relaxation. For boldly colored bedrooms, you might choose artwork that echoes one accent color or introduces a complementary shade. The key is ensuring your art feels intentional rather than random or clashing.

Popular Art Styles for Bedrooms

  • Landscape and Nature Photography: Mountains, forests, beaches, and natural scenes create peaceful atmospheres ideal for sleep spaces. These evoke tranquility and connection to nature.
  • Abstract Art: Modern abstract pieces add visual interest and sophistication without the specificity that might be mentally stimulating. Soft, flowing abstracts work better than aggressive, angular designs.
  • Botanical and Floral Prints: Plants and flowers bring life and freshness to bedroom walls while maintaining a calm aesthetic. These work particularly well with bohemian or farmhouse styles.
  • Minimalist and Line Art: Simple line drawings, geometric patterns, and minimalist designs create clean, sophisticated spaces that feel uncluttered and restful.
  • Personal Photography: Family photos or travel memories can create emotional connections, though consider black-and-white or muted versions to maintain a calming environment.

Positioning and Installation

Proper placement makes a significant difference in how your art impacts the room. Mount wall art 8-12 inches above the headboard of your bed. If you don't have a headboard, position it approximately 60-72 inches from the floor. This height keeps the art at eye level when you're sitting up in bed and creates proper visual balance with the furniture below.

Frequently Asked Questions

What size wall art should I hang above my bed?

For a standard queen or full bed, choose artwork that's 50-60% of the bed's width—typically 24-36 inches wide for a single piece. If using multiple pieces, ensure the entire grouping spans 60-75% of the bed width to create proper visual balance without overwhelming the space.

How high should I hang art above a bed?

Mount the center of your artwork 8-12 inches above the headboard, or 24-30 inches from the top of the mattress if there's no headboard. This distance allows the artwork to feel connected to the bed without appearing too close or crowded.

What type of wall art works best above a bed?

Calming, serene imagery like landscapes, botanical prints, abstract art in soft colors, or family photography works best in bedrooms. Avoid overly busy patterns, dark/aggressive themes, or anything that might create visual stimulation that disrupts sleep.

Should I hang one large picture or multiple smaller ones above my bed?

One large statement piece creates a clean, modern look and is easier to balance, while a gallery wall of 3-5 smaller pieces adds interest and personality. Choose based on your style preference, but ensure any multi-piece arrangement is cohesive in color, frame style, or theme.
