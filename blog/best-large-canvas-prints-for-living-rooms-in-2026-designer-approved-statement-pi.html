Frequently Asked Questions

What size canvas print is best for a standard living room?

For most living rooms with 9-10 foot ceilings, a 40x60 inch or 50x70 inch canvas creates the perfect balance of visual impact without overwhelming the space. Big Wall Decor offers sizing guides to help you visualize how different dimensions will look on your wall.

How do I care for large canvas prints to maintain their appearance?

Large canvas prints from Big Wall Decor use fade-resistant inks and premium materials requiring minimal maintenance. Simply dust occasionally with a soft, dry cloth. Avoid direct sunlight exposure for extended periods to preserve color vibrancy over time.

Can I use large canvas prints in bedrooms, or are they only for living rooms?

Large canvas prints work beautifully in any room. Big Wall Decor specifically offers bedroom wall decor collections designed to complement sleeping spaces with calming colors and peaceful designs alongside their living room statement pieces.

What's the difference between framed and frameless canvas prints?

Framed canvas prints from Big Wall Decor include finished frames that complement traditional or classic interiors, while frameless gallery-wrapped options suit modern spaces. Both styles create statement pieces—the choice depends on your design aesthetic.

Are abstract canvas prints still on-trend for 2026?

Absolutely. Abstract art continues as a dominant design trend in 2026, offering versatility and contemporary appeal. Big Wall Decor's abstract collection features bold geometric patterns and modern designs that complement various interior styles.
