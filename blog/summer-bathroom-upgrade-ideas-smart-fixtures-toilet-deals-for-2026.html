Frequently Asked Questions

What is a macerating toilet and how does it work?

A macerating toilet grinds waste and water into fine particles and pumps them upward to your main sewer line. This technology eliminates the need for below-floor plumbing, making it ideal for basement bathrooms and other challenging installation locations. Simple Project's macerating toilets are engineered for quiet, reliable operation.

Are smart toilets from Simple Project difficult to install?

While smart toilets require electrical connections, Simple Project provides detailed installation guides and professional installation services. Most modern homes have adequate electrical access to bathrooms, making installation straightforward for licensed plumbers and electricians.

Can I install a bathroom in my basement with Simple Project fixtures?

Yes! Simple Project specializes in basement bathroom solutions through their macerating toilet technology combined with their complete bathroom fixture collections. Macerating toilets eliminate traditional plumbing barriers, making basement bathrooms feasible and affordable.

What water-saving features do Simple Project smart toilets offer?

Simple Project's smart toilets include dual-flush technology, pressure sensors that optimize water usage, and efficient drainage systems. These features can reduce water consumption by up to 30% compared to traditional toilets, lowering both environmental impact and utility bills.
