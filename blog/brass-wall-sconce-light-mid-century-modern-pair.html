Frequently Asked Questions

Do these brass sconces require hardwired installation or can they plug in?

These are hardwired fixtures, meaning you'll need to connect them directly to your electrical wiring. If your walls aren't set up for this, you'll need an electrician—no plug-and-play option here. This is actually a pro because it means the fixture is permanent and professional-looking, not a temporary solution.

Will the brass finish tarnish or lose its color over time?

The brass finish is lacquered, so it resists tarnishing much better than raw brass would. In normal indoor conditions, you won't see significant color shift for years. Bathroom moisture and direct sunlight are the main culprits if tarnishing happens—but these sconces are rated for interior use and hold up well in bedrooms, hallways, and living rooms.

What's the actual light output, and will they illuminate a room properly?

These are accent lights first, ambient lights second. A single pair can comfortably light a hallway or frame a bathroom mirror, but they shouldn't be your only light source in a larger room. Most buyers pair them with ceiling fixtures or use them alongside table lamps. The frosted glass diffuses light warmly without the brightness of bare bulbs.
