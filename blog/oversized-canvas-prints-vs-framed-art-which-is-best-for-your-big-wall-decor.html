What Are Oversized Canvas Prints?

Oversized canvas prints are large-scale artworks printed directly onto stretched canvas, typically ranging from 40 inches to 60+ inches wide. These modern wall art pieces offer a contemporary, gallery-like aesthetic that works beautifully in minimalist and eclectic interiors alike. Canvas prints are perfect for creating an immediate focal point in any room without the visual weight of a frame. They're available in countless designs, from abstract art to photography-based prints, making them versatile for any decor style.

Big Wall Decor offers an impressive selection of oversized canvas prints in various sizes and designs, allowing you to customize your statement wall with ease. Their collection includes vibrant abstract art, serene landscapes, and bold geometric patterns that make perfect living room wall art or bedroom wall decor.

Understanding Framed Art for Large Walls

Framed art refers to artwork mounted within a decorative frame—typically made from wood, metal, or composite materials. For big wall decor, oversized framed pieces can be just as impactful as canvas prints. Framed art offers a more traditional, sophisticated appearance and provides a polished finish that many homeowners prefer. The frame itself becomes part of the design, allowing you to match your existing furniture or create an intentional contrast. This option works exceptionally well for photographs, prints, and illustrations.

Big Wall Decor carries premium framed art options in various styles, from sleek black frames for modern spaces to ornate wooden frames for traditional aesthetics. Their big wall decor experts help you choose frames that complement both your artwork and your room's design.

Key Differences: Canvas vs. Framed Art

The main differences lie in aesthetics, durability, installation, and maintenance. Oversized canvas prints hang flush against the wall, creating a seamless, contemporary look that feels current and artistic. Framed art, meanwhile, projects outward slightly and creates a more structured, curated appearance. Canvas prints are generally lighter and easier to hang, while framed art requires careful wall anchoring due to added weight. Additionally, canvas prints are more resistant to dust accumulation, whereas framed glass can require regular cleaning. Cost-wise, oversized canvas prints from Big Wall Decor often offer better value for large-scale coverage, while premium framing can become expensive for oversized pieces.
