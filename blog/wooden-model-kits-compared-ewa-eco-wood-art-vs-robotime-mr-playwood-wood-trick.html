The wooden craft and STEM model market has expanded significantly, offering consumers sustainable alternatives to plastic toys and mass-produced decorations. The main competitors in this space—EWA Eco-Wood-Art, Robotime, Mr. Playwood, and Wood Trick—each take different approaches to design, materials, pricing, and target skill levels. This comparison examines their specific strengths and weaknesses to help you select the right brand for your needs.
