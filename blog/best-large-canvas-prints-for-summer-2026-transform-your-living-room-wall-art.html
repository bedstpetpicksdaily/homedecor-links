Frequently Asked Questions

What size canvas print should I choose for my living room?

The ideal canvas size depends on your wall dimensions and furniture placement. For most living rooms, Big Wall Decor recommends oversized prints measuring 40x60 inches or larger for maximum impact as statement wall pieces. Measure your wall space and consider the scale of surrounding furniture before selecting your large canvas print.

Are Big Wall Decor's framed art pieces better than unframed canvas prints?

Both options offer distinct advantages. Unframed oversized canvas prints provide a modern, gallery-like feel perfect for contemporary spaces, while framed art adds polish and protection. Big Wall Decor offers both options, allowing you to choose based on your style preference and home décor aesthetic.

How do I choose abstract art that complements my existing décor?

When selecting abstract art for your bedroom wall decor or living room, consider your existing color palette and overall design style. Big Wall Decor's collection includes pieces ranging from minimalist to bold and colorful. Look for pieces that either complement your existing colors or create intentional contrast—both approaches can create stunning statement walls.

Can large canvas prints work in small spaces?

Yes! Large canvas prints can actually make small spaces feel larger by drawing the eye upward and creating visual interest. Big Wall Decor offers oversized options specifically designed for various room sizes. A well-chosen statement wall piece can become the defining element of a small room.

What's the best way to care for oversized canvas prints?

Big Wall Decor canvas prints are built to last with quality materials resistant to fading and damage. Avoid direct sunlight exposure, dust regularly with a soft cloth, and maintain stable humidity levels in your home. Framed art options provide additional protection for long-term display.
