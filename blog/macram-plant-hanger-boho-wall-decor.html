What to Look For When Buying Macramé Plant Hangers

1. Material Quality and Durability

Look for hangers made from 100% natural cotton or jute—these are the most authentic boho options and hold up best over time. Synthetic materials may look similar initially but deteriorate faster, especially with moisture from watering plants. Check product descriptions for cord thickness; thicker cords (at least 4-5mm) indicate better weight capacity and longevity. Avoid anything that feels flimsy or has loose, unraveling edges.

2. Weight Capacity for Your Plants

This is crucial and often overlooked. A standard macramé hanger typically holds 2-5 pounds, but larger plants in ceramic pots can easily exceed this. Check the product specifications carefully—some premium hangers support up to 10 pounds. Measure your plant pot's weight before purchasing, and always choose a hanger rated for more than you think you'll need as a safety margin.

3. Knot Pattern and Aesthetic Appeal

Macramé hangers come in various knot patterns—square knots, spiral knots, and more complex designs. Square knot patterns are most versatile and pair well with any decor style. Look at product photos from multiple angles to ensure the pattern looks good from all sides, not just the front. The aesthetic consistency across the hanger matters since you'll be viewing it from different positions in your room.

4. Adjustability and Hardware

The best macramé hangers have adjustable lengths so you can customize how low your plants hang. Look for hangers with wooden or metal beads that allow you to tie knots at different heights. Check that the top ring or hook is sturdy—typically wooden or reinforced metal—and that the hanging hardware can safely mount to your wall type (drywall anchors, hooks, or ceiling mounts depending on your setup).
