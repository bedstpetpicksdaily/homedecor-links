Frequently Asked Questions

Do the adhesive hooks actually stay, or do they peel off after a few months?

If applied to properly cleaned, smooth drywall, the adhesive holds firm for years—reviewers report no issues with bags or light coats pulling them down. The catch: they work best on flat surfaces. Textured or freshly painted walls sometimes struggle. Wall anchors (included) are the safer permanent option if you doubt your wall prep or need guaranteed staying power.

Can you repaint your walls if the hooks are already up?

Technically yes, but it's awkward. You'll need to remove the hooks first, which means sacrificing whatever adhesive tabs you used and potentially touching up the wall paint underneath. They're not designed for frequent repositioning, so plan their placement before installation.

How much weight can each hook realistically hold?

Each hook safely handles 2-3 pounds of weight—think a lightweight jacket, a canvas crossbody bag, or 2-3 folded scarves. Don't hang heavy coats or winter parkas on these. If you need serious weight capacity, you need different hardware entirely.

Are they worth the price compared to basic plastic hooks?

If you care about how your walls look beyond pure functionality, yes. Plastic hooks visually disappear into the wall or look cheap. These metal dots are intentional design pieces that elevate an otherwise ordinary wall. For someone spending money on throw pillows and wall art anyway, these complete the look instead of detracting from it. If you need invisible hooks and don't care about design, save your money.
