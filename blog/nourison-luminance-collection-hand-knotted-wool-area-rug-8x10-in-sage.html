Frequently Asked Questions

How much shedding should I actually expect with hand-knotted wool rugs?

During the first month, expect noticeable fibers in your vacuum—this is normal and not a defect. Daily vacuuming helps speed up the process and reduce the visible loose fibers on the rug surface. By week 6-8, shedding drops dramatically. This happens because hand-knotted construction leaves individual fiber ends that gradually anchor themselves into the pile structure. Machine-made rugs shed less initially because they're tufted into backing, but that backing eventually degrades. Hand-knotted pieces actually improve with age as the pile becomes more compact and durable.

Will the sage color fade in sunlight, and is it too light for a high-traffic family room?

Wool's natural lanolin content provides inherent UV protection that synthetic fibers lack, so fading happens gradually over years rather than months. The sage colorway is darker than pale gray-green alternatives and tends to hide foot traffic patterns reasonably well—I've observed that walnut furniture and darker wood tones actually make the sage read even richer. In family rooms with kids and pets, the color choice becomes an advantage because minor soiling is less visible than on lighter neutrals. That said, this rug absolutely shows footprints more than charcoal or deep taupe would, so if you have light-colored furniture and pristine aesthetics as priorities, consider deeper sage or charcoal variants instead.

Is the 8x10 size right for my living room, and what should I budget for professional cleaning?

The 8x10 dimensions work best when at least the front legs of your sofa sit on the rug—if your living room furniture floats completely off the rug, you need 9x12. Measure your seating area footprint and ensure the rug extends at least 18-24 inches beyond the furniture edges for visual balance. Professional wool rug cleaning typically costs $2-4 per square foot depending on your region, so budget around $160-320 annually if you have pets or high traffic. However, routine vacuuming and spot-cleaning extend the time between professional cleanings significantly. Many owners get 2-3 years between full professional cleanings with proper maintenance, which spreads the cost considerably.
