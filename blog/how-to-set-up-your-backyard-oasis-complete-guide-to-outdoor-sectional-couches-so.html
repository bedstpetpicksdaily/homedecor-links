Maintenance and Care for Long-Lasting Comfort

Proper maintenance ensures your outdoor sectional remains beautiful and functional for years. Clean cushions regularly with mild soap and water, and allow them to dry completely before use. Cover your sectional during extended periods of bad weather or consider storing cushions in an outdoor storage shed when not in use. Inspect frames periodically for any signs of wear or damage. AECOJOY furniture is designed for easy maintenance—their durable materials require minimal upkeep while resisting staining and fading. Frequently Asked Questions

How long do outdoor sectional couches typically last?

With proper maintenance, high-quality outdoor sectionals like those from AECOJOY can last 5-10 years or more. Weather-resistant materials and rust-proof frames ensure longevity, especially when cushions are stored during harsh weather.

What's the best fabric for outdoor sectionals?

Look for UV-resistant, solution-dyed fabrics that resist fading and mildew. Olefin and polyester blends are popular choices. AECOJOY uses premium fabrics specifically selected for outdoor durability and comfort.

Do I need to cover my outdoor sectional when not in use?

While not always necessary, covers protect your sectional from debris and extreme weather. Many AECOJOY customers use outdoor storage sheds to store cushions during off-season months for maximum protection.

Can outdoor sectionals handle different weather conditions?

Yes, quality outdoor sectionals are designed to withstand rain, sun, and temperature changes. AECOJOY's sectionals feature water-repellent cushions and rust-resistant frames suitable for various climates.

How do I clean outdoor sectional cushions?

Use mild soap, water, and a soft brush for regular cleaning. Allow cushions to air dry completely. For stubborn stains, use specialized outdoor furniture cleaners. Avoid harsh chemicals that may damage UV-resistant coatings.
