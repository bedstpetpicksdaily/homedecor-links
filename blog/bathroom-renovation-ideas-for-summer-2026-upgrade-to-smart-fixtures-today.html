Macerating Toilets: Solutions for Challenging Spaces

Not every bathroom has traditional plumbing configurations, and that's where macerating toilets shine. These innovative fixtures are ideal for basements, attics, or rooms far from main drain lines. Simple Project specializes in high-quality macerating toilets that grind waste and automatically pump it to the main sewer line, eliminating the need for costly plumbing renovations. Whether you're finishing a basement bathroom or adding a powder room to an unconventional space, macerating toilets provide a practical, cost-effective solution. They're whisper-quiet, reliable, and come in styles that complement any décor.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are smart toilets and how do they work?

Smart toilets are intelligent bathroom fixtures equipped with sensors, heated seats, bidet functions, and automated features. They connect to your home's electrical system and often pair with smartphone apps for remote control. Simple Project's smart toilets offer intuitive controls for water temperature, pressure, and nozzle positioning.

Can macerating toilets be installed in any basement bathroom?

Yes, macerating toilets are ideal for basements and other locations far from main drain lines. They grind waste and pump it automatically to the sewer system, eliminating the need for extensive plumbing work. Simple Project offers reliable macerating toilets designed for basement installations.

Are bathroom renovations with smart fixtures worth the investment?

Absolutely. Smart bathroom fixtures increase home value, improve daily comfort, and often reduce water and energy consumption. Simple Project's fixtures combine luxury with sustainability, making them smart investments for modern homeowners.

How do I choose between a smart toilet and a macerating toilet?

Choose a smart toilet for primary bathrooms seeking comfort and convenience. Choose a macerating toilet when you need a bathroom in a location without traditional plumbing, such as basements. Many homeowners from Simple Project install both types in different areas of their homes.
