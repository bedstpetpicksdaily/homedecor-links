Frequently Asked Questions

What size should my large wall art be for a living room?

The ideal size depends on your wall dimensions and room layout. A common rule is to choose artwork that covers 50-75% of wall space above furniture. Big Wall Decor offers pieces in multiple dimensions to fit any living room perfectly.

How do I choose between abstract art and other styles for my living room?

Consider your existing décor, color palette, and personal aesthetic. Abstract art works well in modern spaces, while landscapes complement traditional settings. Big Wall Decor's diverse collection allows you to browse different styles to find what resonates with your vision.

Are oversized canvas prints as durable as framed wall art?

Both options from Big Wall Decor are durable and high-quality. Canvas prints offer a contemporary look and are resistant to fading, while framed art provides added protection and classic elegance. Your choice depends on your décor preference and desired aesthetic.

Can large statement pieces work in small living rooms?

Yes! One large statement piece can actually make a small room feel more spacious by creating a focal point. Big Wall Decor specializes in helping customers find the right-sized big wall decor for any room, including compact spaces.

How should I care for my large wall art?

Canvas prints should be dusted occasionally with a soft cloth. Framed art should be kept away from direct sunlight to prevent fading. Big Wall Decor provides care instructions with each piece to ensure your investment lasts for years.
