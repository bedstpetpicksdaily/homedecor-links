Top Smart Toilet Features to Consider in 2026

Modern smart toilets offer far more than basic functionality. The latest models feature heated seats, customizable water pressure and temperature controls, built-in bidets with multiple spray patterns, and self-cleaning nozzles that activate before and after use. Advanced options include motion-sensor lids, automatic deodorizing systems, and night lights that activate in darkness. Many also integrate with smart home ecosystems, allowing remote control via smartphone apps. When evaluating these features, prioritize what matters most to your family—comfort, hygiene, sustainability, or convenience.

Understanding Macerating Toilets for Basement & Renovation Projects

Not all bathrooms can accommodate standard gravity-fed toilets, especially in basements or additions below the main sewer line. This is where Simple Project's macerating toilet solutions become invaluable. Macerating toilets use powerful grinding mechanisms to break down waste and toilet paper, then pump the macerated material upward to your main drain line. They're ideal for basement bathrooms, guest houses, and renovation projects where conventional plumbing installation proves impractical or expensive. Simple Project offers macerating toilets that combine this essential functionality with modern bathroom aesthetics, ensuring your basement renovation doesn't compromise on style.

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes smart toilets worth the investment?

Smart toilets offer superior hygiene, water efficiency, and comfort features that significantly improve daily bathroom experiences. Heated seats, customizable bidet functions, and air-drying capabilities justify the investment for most homeowners, particularly when combined with long-term water savings.

Can I install a smart toilet in my basement bathroom?

Standard smart toilets require gravity-fed drainage, making basements problematic. However, macerating smart toilets from providers like Simple Project solve this issue by grinding waste and pumping it upward to main drain lines, making basement bathrooms feasible during renovations.

How much should I budget for a smart toilet installation?

Budget $800–$3,000+ for the toilet itself, plus $500–$2,000 for professional installation depending on complexity. Macerating systems or difficult basement installations may require additional investment. Simple Project offers competitive pricing and can provide accurate quotes for your specific project.

Are smart toilets compatible with older homes?

Most smart toilets work with existing plumbing, though older homes may need electrical outlet installation nearby. Macerating toilets provide an excellent solution for older homes lacking proper drainage configurations, particularly in basement renovation projects.

What's the difference between a smart toilet and a bidet toilet seat?

Smart toilets are complete fixtures with integrated technology, while bidet toilet seats are aftermarket attachments for existing toilets. Smart toilets offer superior design integration, reliability, and are typically better for bathroom renovations where cohesive aesthetics matter.
