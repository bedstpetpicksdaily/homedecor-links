Frequently Asked Questions

Is this rug actually Moroccan or just Moroccan-inspired?

Most Beni Ourain rugs sold on Amazon are authentic Moroccan hand-loomed pieces, though quality varies by seller. The genuine versions come from the Atlas Mountains region and are made using traditional techniques. Check seller reviews and ask about origin before purchasing—legitimate sellers will specify if it's authentic Moroccan or a reproduction. The authenticity affects both longevity and that subtle quality feel you're probably paying for.

How do I prevent excessive shedding after I bring it home?

Vacuum thoroughly before placing furniture on it, then vacuum 2-3 times per week for the first month. Avoid heavy traffic directly on the rug during this initial period if possible. Some people use a lint roller on high-traffic areas. The shedding is normal and expected with hand-loomed wool—it's not a defect, just part of breaking in natural fiber. After week 4, shedding drops to minimal levels.

Does the white color stain easily, or is it actually livable with kids and pets?

Honest answer: white shows dirt more than darker neutrals, period. However, wool naturally resists stains better than many alternatives because fibers have a protective outer layer. Spills bead up slightly if you blot immediately. Regular vacuuming and annual professional cleaning keep it looking fresh. If you have young kids or pets that frequently have accidents, consider a gray or cream alternative instead. This isn't a "show rug"—it's meant to be lived on—but realistic expectations matter.

What's the actual difference between the cheaper and more expensive options on Amazon?

Price variation typically comes from seller markup rather than quality differences in authentic rugs. Sometimes you're paying more for expedited shipping, seller reputation, or simply higher profit margins. Compare product descriptions carefully—they should look virtually identical for authentic pieces. Check the review dates and verified purchase badges, then choose the reputable seller at the lower price. You're not sacrificing quality by shopping strategically.

How long do these rugs actually last with normal use?

Authentic hand-loomed Beni Ourain rugs last 10-15+ years with normal living room use. They're built to age beautifully, developing character and slight color shifts over time that most owners find charming rather than degrading. Heavy commercial use wears them faster, but home use in a living or bedroom area means you're looking at genuine investment pieces. Professional cleaning every 1-2 years extends lifespan significantly.
