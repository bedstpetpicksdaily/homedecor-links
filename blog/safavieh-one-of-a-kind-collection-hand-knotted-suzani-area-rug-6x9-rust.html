Frequently Asked Questions

How much does the Safavieh One of a Kind Suzani 6x9 actually shed, and how long does it last?

Initial shedding peaks in weeks 2-6, then drops dramatically by week 8. This is normal for hand-knotted wool and indicates the pile is settling. A quality rug pad underneath accelerates this process and minimizes loose fibers reaching your furniture. Life expectancy for this rug in normal home use is 12-15 years, with proper vacuuming and occasional professional cleaning extending that significantly. Unlike flat-weaves that degrade after 5-7 years, hand-knotted pieces actually develop patina.

Is the rust color accurate to the product photos, and will it fade in sunlight?

The rust shade is fairly true to listing photos, though monitor calibration matters. What matters more: natural light exposure will gradually soften the rust tone over 3-5 years, shifting toward a more muted terracotta. This is actually desirable for many people because it makes the rug look less 'new' and more intentional. If you're in a bright south-facing room, expect noticeable fading within 18 months, which is why area rugs in high-light zones typically benefit from a sheer curtain to diffuse direct sun.

What's the difference between this hand-knotted Suzani and the machine-made versions at similar price points?

Hand-knotted means each knot is tied individually, creating structural integrity and allowing the wool fibers to move independently. Machine-made rugs have glued-together layers that separate when backing fails—usually 5-7 years in. You're essentially paying $300-400 more for a rug that won't need replacing, which actually makes it cheaper per year of use. The Safavieh sits at the accessible end of hand-knotted pricing; Indian and Turkish pieces cost 2-3x more but use nearly identical construction methods.

Does this rug need a pad underneath, and does it work on hardwood or carpet?

A quality rug pad is non-negotiable. Hand-knotted rugs are heavy enough to slide on hardwood without one, creating tripping hazards and uneven wear patterns. On carpet, a pad prevents the rug from bunching and wrinkling. Spend $40-60 on a dense rubber pad—it's the best investment you'll make for rug longevity. The rug performs identically on both hardwood and carpet, though hardwood does slightly highlight the rug's texture visually.
