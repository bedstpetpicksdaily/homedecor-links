    Frequently Asked Questions

    What size throw pillows should I buy for my couch?

    Standard throw pillow sizes are 16x16", 18x18", and 20x20", with 18x18" being the most versatile for average couches. For sectionals or larger sofas, 20x20" or 22x22" pillows work better, while small accent chairs pair well with 16x16" pillows. A good rule of thumb is choosing pillows that are about one-third to one-half the width of your seating surface.

    What fabric is best for throw pillows on a high-traffic couch?

    Performance fabrics like microfiber, solution-dyed polyester, and outdoor-grade materials are ideal for heavily-used couches because they resist staining and wear better than natural fibers. If you prefer natural materials, tightly-woven linen or cotton blends with a performance finish offer durability while maintaining breathability. Avoid delicate fabrics like silk or velvet unless you're willing to frequently dry-clean them.

    How many throw pillows should I put on my couch?

    Most designers recommend 2-4 pillows depending on couch length: 2-3 for a loveseat or apartment-sized sofa, and 4-5 for larger sectionals or L-shaped couches. Odd numbers (3 or 5) typically create a more balanced, designed look than even numbers. Leave enough space for actual seating and comfort—the pillows should enhance the couch, not overwhelm it.

    Should throw pillow colors match or contrast with my couch?

    For a cohesive look, choose pillow colors that complement your couch's color palette—either matching the main color or selecting accent colors from your existing décor. A general strategy is using 60% of one coordinating color and 40% contrasting accents to create visual interest without clashing. If your couch is a bold color, neutral or muted pillow tones ground the space; if neutral, patterned or jewel-tone pillows add personality.
