Who Should Buy This

Whether you're moving into a new place, updating a tired room, or simply want to maximize your summer decorating budget, these July sales offer the best pricing of the year on quality pieces that will last for years to come.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Buying items based solely on price without considering how they'll work with your existing décor—a bargain pillow or rug that clashes with your style will never get used.
  • Neglecting to measure your space before purchasing larger items like curtains, rugs, or statement lamps, leading to pieces that don't fit properly or overwhelm the room.
  • Overlooking quality and material composition in favor of a lower price; cheaper fabrics fade faster and synthetic rugs show wear more quickly, making them a false economy.

Expert Tips for Best Results

  • Layer your décor strategically by combining items from different categories—pair a quality rug with coordinating throw pillows and a complementary lamp to create a cohesive, intentional look rather than a scattered approach.
  • Invest your July savings on anchor pieces (like a durable area rug or quality curtains) that will anchor your room's design, then fill in with more budget-friendly accents that can be swapped seasonally.
  • Test colors and textures in your actual space before committing, especially with wall art and pillows; natural lighting changes throughout the day, so verify that your selections look right in morning, afternoon, and evening light.
