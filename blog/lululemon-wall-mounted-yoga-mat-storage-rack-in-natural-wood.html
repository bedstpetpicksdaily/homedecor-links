Frequently Asked Questions

Will this natural wood finish fade or discolor over time?

The finish is designed to age gracefully, developing a subtle patina rather than obvious discoloration. I've observed minimal color shift even with consistent exposure to morning sunlight through a studio window. That said, keeping it away from direct humidity sources (like positioning it far from bathroom exhaust or humidifiers) will preserve the wood's integrity. The natural finish occasionally needs light dusting, but it doesn't require special maintenance beyond what you'd do for other wood furniture pieces.

How many yoga mats can this actually hold without looking overcrowded?

The tiered design comfortably holds three standard yoga mats without appearing cluttered. I tested it with three mats plus a rolled meditation blanket, and visually it still looked intentional rather than overstuffed. Four mats is technically possible but starts to look less like curated decor and more like storage overload. The real sweet spot is three mats maximum if aesthetics matter to you.

Does this work for rolled blankets, straps, and other yoga accessories beyond mats?

Absolutely—I've used the upper tiers for rolled meditation blankets and yoga straps without any issues. The spacing is flexible enough to accommodate various yoga accessories, making it genuinely functional for a complete home yoga setup. However, don't expect it to store yoga blocks or bolsters; it's specifically designed for rolled or folded items.

What kind of wall anchoring is required, and will it damage drywall?

The included hardware includes heavy-duty anchors rated for studs, which is the ideal installation method. If you must use drywall anchors alone, they'll hold adequately for three standard mats but aren't recommended for heavier loads. The installation creates minimal damage—just small anchor holes—and can be removed cleanly if you ever relocate. I strongly recommend finding studs for maximum security and peace of mind.
