The frame makes or breaks the look. Metal frames (brass, black steel, or brushed nickel) are currently leading the trend. Curved arch mirrors are heavier than standard mirrors due to the arched frame structure. Verify weight (most quality pieces run 15-35 pounds) and confirm your walls can handle it.
