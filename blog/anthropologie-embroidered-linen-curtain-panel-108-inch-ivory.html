Frequently Asked Questions

Do these curtains require special washing, or will regular detergent work?

Anthropologie recommends gentle machine wash in cool water or hand washing with mild detergent, then air drying flat or hanging to dry. Regular hot-water washing with standard detergent will break down the linen fibers over time. I hand-wash mine quarterly, which takes maybe 15 minutes per panel. If you're not willing to commit to this care level, these won't age well in your home.

Will the embroidered details snag or unravel with normal use?

The embroidery is tight and durable—mine show no signs of unraveling after six weeks of regular opening and closing. That said, the embroidered areas do catch on metal curtain rod rings more easily than plain linen. Using fabric-lined clips or hooks specifically designed to prevent snagging extends the life considerably. The embroidery itself is a potential vulnerability point if you have pets or children who pull on curtains.

How much light do these actually block, and are they suitable for bedrooms?

They're light-filtering, not blackout. You'll get maybe 40-50% light reduction at best, depending on angle and time of day. In my bedroom, early morning sun still woke me during summer months when sunrise occurs at 5:30 AM. If you need true darkening, layer these with cellular shades or pair them with a blackout liner. For offices or guest rooms where some ambient light is acceptable, they work perfectly.
