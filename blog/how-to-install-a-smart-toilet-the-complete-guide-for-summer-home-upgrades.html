Preparing Your Bathroom for Smart Toilet Installation

Before installing your new smart toilet, proper preparation is essential. Start by turning off the water supply to your existing toilet and flushing to empty the tank completely. Remove any items around the toilet area to create a clean, accessible workspace. Measure your current toilet's rough-in distance (typically 12 inches from the wall to the center of the drain) to ensure your new smart toilet will fit properly. If you're upgrading an older bathroom or working with limited space, consider options like basement toilets or macerating toilets from Simple Project, which offer flexibility in placement and installation requirements.

Removing Your Old Toilet and Preparing the Flange

Disconnect the water supply line and unbolt the old toilet from the floor. Carefully lift and remove it, then inspect the flange—the ring that connects the toilet to the drain pipe. If the flange is damaged or deteriorated, now is the time to replace it. Clean the area thoroughly and remove the old wax ring. For homes with plumbing challenges or unconventional layouts, Simple Project's macerating toilet options provide alternative solutions that don't require direct gravity-fed drainage, making them perfect for basement bathrooms or difficult installations.

Installing Your Smart Toilet and Making Connections

Position your new smart toilet over the flange and secure it with bolts, ensuring it sits evenly and doesn't rock. Connect the water supply line, typically using a flexible hose with a shutoff valve. Many smart toilets from Simple Project come with integrated bidet features and heated seats, so take time to review the manufacturer's electrical requirements. Most smart toilets need a dedicated outlet within 3 feet of the fixture—plan your electrical setup accordingly before finalizing placement. Once everything is connected, turn on the water supply gradually and check for leaks around the base and connections.

Testing Features and Final Adjustments

After installation, test all smart features including the heated seat, bidet spray pressure, and automatic lid functions. Configure water pressure settings to your preference and ensure the deodorizer system (if included) is functioning properly. Simple Project bathroom fixtures are designed for user-friendly setup, with intuitive controls and detailed manuals. Make any necessary adjustments to ensure everything operates smoothly. Run multiple flush cycles to confirm water flow is adequate and drainage works correctly, then enjoy your upgraded bathroom fixture.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a plumber to install a smart toilet?

While basic installation can be DIY if you're comfortable with plumbing, hiring a professional plumber is recommended for electrical connections and ensuring proper alignment. Simple Project provides detailed installation guides, but professional installation ensures warranty coverage.

What's the difference between a smart toilet and a macerating toilet?

Smart toilets feature electronic controls like heated seats and bidet functions. Macerating toilets grind and pump waste, allowing installation in basements or remote locations without gravity-fed drainage. Simple Project offers both options to suit different home layouts.

How much does a smart toilet installation cost?

Smart toilet prices range from $800-$3,500 depending on features. Installation costs vary by location and complexity, typically $200-$500. Simple Project offers various price points to fit different budgets.

Are smart toilets water-efficient?

Yes, most smart toilets use 1.28 gallons per flush or less, meeting EPA WaterSense standards. Simple Project's bathroom fixtures prioritize water conservation without compromising performance.
