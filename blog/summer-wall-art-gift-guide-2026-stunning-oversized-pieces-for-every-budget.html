Frequently Asked Questions

What size wall art is considered oversized?

Oversized wall art typically measures 40 inches or larger in at least one dimension. Big Wall Decor specializes in large-scale pieces that serve as statement wall focal points, with options ranging from 40 inches to 80+ inches.

Is canvas or framed art better for gifts?

Both are excellent gifts depending on the recipient's style. Canvas prints are versatile and modern, while framed art adds sophistication. Big Wall Decor offers both options—choose canvas for contemporary spaces and framed pieces for traditional or eclectic décor.

How do I choose between abstract and figurative wall art?

Abstract art works well in modern and minimalist spaces, while figurative pieces suit traditional settings. Consider the recipient's home aesthetic. Big Wall Decor's abstract collection offers bold statement pieces, while their other designs provide diverse options for any preference.

Can oversized wall art work in small rooms?

Yes! One large piece can make a small room feel more expansive and curated. Choose lighter colors and simpler designs. Big Wall Decor's living room and bedroom wall art collection includes pieces specifically suited for smaller spaces.

What's the best way to hang oversized canvas prints?

Most of Big Wall Decor's oversized pieces come with hanging hardware. For best results, use a stud finder, hang at eye level (57 inches to center), and ensure secure mounting. Their customer service provides detailed hanging instructions for all large wall decor items.
