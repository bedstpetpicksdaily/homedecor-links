Fall Home Decor Trends 2024: What's In and What to Skip

As autumn approaches, interior designers are weighing in on which fall decor trends are worth your time and budget—and which ones you should leave behind. Whether you're planning a complete seasonal refresh or just updating a few key pieces, knowing what professionals actually recommend can save you money and help you create a space that feels current and intentional.

Why This Matters

Fall decorating doesn't have to mean following every trend that pops up on social media. By understanding what design professionals genuinely love versus what's already lost its appeal, you can make smarter purchasing decisions. This approach helps you invest in pieces you'll actually enjoy, avoid impulse buys on fading trends, and create a cohesive autumn aesthetic that feels authentic to your home.

When selecting fall wall art and decorative accents, measure your wall space and doorways beforehand to ensure pieces won't overwhelm the room or create awkward proportions, especially when layering multiple autumn-themed items like tapestries, framed prints, and sculptural accents together.

What Home Decorating Enthusiasts Should Know

  • Designers are embracing warmth with purpose: This season celebrates rich, layered textures and warm metallics—but with intention. Skip the over-coordinated, matchy-matchy aesthetic in favor of curated, meaningful pieces.
  • Sustainable and vintage elements are having a major moment: Real wood, antique-inspired decor, and upcycled pieces are trending because they add character and longevity to your space.
  • Overplayed trends to avoid: Basic orange-and-black color schemes, mass-produced Halloween decorations, and novelty seasonal items are officially out of favor with design professionals.
  • Quality over quantity: Designers recommend investing in a few statement pieces rather than filling your space with numerous trendy items that won't stand the test of time.
