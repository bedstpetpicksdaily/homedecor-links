Macerating Toilets: Perfect for Basement Bathrooms

If you've been considering adding a bathroom to your basement, macerating toilets from Simple Project offer an elegant solution. Unlike traditional toilets that require extensive plumbing work, macerating toilets can be installed in tight spaces without costly renovations. These innovative fixtures grind waste into a fine slurry and pump it to your main sewer line, giving you complete bathroom flexibility. This is a game-changer for homeowners looking to maximize usable space and increase property value without breaking the bank.
