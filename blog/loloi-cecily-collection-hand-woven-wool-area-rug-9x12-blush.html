Frequently Asked Questions

How does hand-woven construction actually affect long-term wear compared to power-loomed rugs?

Hand-woven wool rugs have denser fiber connections and more uniform tension throughout, which means they resist wear patterns and maintain structural integrity far longer. Power-loomed rugs, even quality ones, develop visible traffic patterns within 5-7 years because the weaving density isn't consistent. With the Cecily specifically, the hand-woven construction means the 9x12 size maintains its shape under furniture weight and foot traffic—it won't cup at edges or create hollow spots like lower-tier pieces do.

Is the blush color too light for a family home with kids or pets?

Blush wool is light but not impossible to maintain. Wool's natural lanolin content actually repels liquids initially, giving you time to blot spills before they set. Pet hair is visible but manageable with weekly vacuuming and a good rubber broom. The real factor: are you willing to do that maintenance? If yes, this works. If you're seeking a 'hide everything' rug, charcoal or gray neutrals are more forgiving.

How does Loloi's pricing compare to other hand-woven wool options in the 9x12 range?

Loloi sits in the mid-to-premium tier. You'll find cheaper hand-woven options from lesser-known brands, but they often use lower-grade wool or thinner weaves. Higher-end brands like Restoration Hardware or Parachute charge 30-50% more for similar construction. The Cecily represents solid value—quality that justifies the investment without luxury pricing. Check current pricing across retailers since wool costs fluctuate seasonally; July sometimes sees better stock clearance on spring collections.
