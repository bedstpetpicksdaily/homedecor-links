Frequently Asked Questions

How much does an authentic hand-knotted Turkish kilim 6x9 actually cost?

Prices range dramatically—anywhere from $400 to $1,200+ depending on knot density, wool quality, and where you buy. Direct Turkish artisan sales tend to cost less than Amazon reseller markups. Don't assume two kilims at different price points are different quality; often it's just the intermediary taking a cut. Check the specific listing for knot count per square inch if available.

Will this rug shed all over my house?

Yes, for the first year especially. Hand-knotted wool naturally sheds loose fibers during the breaking-in period—this is normal, not a defect. Vacuum 2-3 times weekly for the first few months, then it stabilizes. If you have dark furniture or light-colored flooring, prepare for visible fibers during this phase. Some people vacuum-seal the rug for a week to speed up initial shedding.

Is this actually better than a machine-made kilim or Persian rug?

Hand-knotted means individual knots are tied by hand, which creates denser weave and longer lifespan—we're talking 20-30 years of regular use versus 5-8 years for most machine-made versions. The trade-off is price and that initial shedding phase. Hand-knotted also develops patina (lighter spots from foot traffic) that adds character; machine-made versions just look worn out. If you're keeping the rug for under five years, save your money on a quality machine-made alternative.
