Frequently Asked Questions

How does this compare to budget wool rugs from big-box stores?

Budget wool rugs ($150-$300) use lower-quality fibers and looser weaves that crush more easily under furniture. The Aubrey's hand-woven construction creates denser fibers that resist matting. Real difference emerges after 18 months—budget rugs show visible wear patterns; the Aubrey maintains its pile. You're essentially choosing between replacing it twice in seven years or once.

Is this rug safe for homes with pets and young kids?

Yes, and it's actually ideal for them. Wool naturally resists staining better than synthetics—spills sit on top longer, giving you time to blot before they set. The charcoal color camouflages pet hair and crumbs effectively. Parents specifically praise it for households with both kids and dogs, which is your actual use case if you're asking this question.

Will the charcoal color fade or look dingy in bright sunlight?

Charcoal holds color better than lighter shades, but any dark fabric will eventually fade if positioned directly in southern or western windows (8+ hours daily sun). If your room gets afternoon sun, placing the rug where furniture blocks peak light hours helps. Realistic timeline: noticeable fading after 3-4 years of direct sun exposure, not a defect but normal wear.
