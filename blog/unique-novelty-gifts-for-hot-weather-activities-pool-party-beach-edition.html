Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to receive personalized gifts from GiftLab?

GiftLab typically processes custom orders within 3-5 business days, with shipping times varying by location. For time-sensitive pool parties or beach events, we recommend ordering at least 1-2 weeks in advance to ensure timely delivery.

Can I order multiple personalized gifts with different photos?

Absolutely! GiftLab allows you to create multiple custom items with different photos, making it easy to personalize gifts for different guests. You can upload various photos and create unique novelty gifts tailored to each recipient.

What file formats work best for custom photo gifts?

GiftLab accepts most standard image formats including JPEG, PNG, and PDF. High-resolution photos (300 DPI) produce the best quality results on personalized mugs, photo flipbooks, and home decor items. Lower resolution images may appear blurry on final products.

Are GiftLab's personalized gifts suitable for outdoor use?

Yes! GiftLab's custom mugs and outdoor home decor are designed with weather-resistant materials. However, delicate items like photo flipbooks are best kept indoors. Check product descriptions to confirm durability for your specific poolside or beach environment.
