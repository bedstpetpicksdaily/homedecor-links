Frequently Asked Questions

How tall is this lamp, and will it fit in a low-ceiling apartment?

The West Elm model stands approximately 63 inches tall from floor to top of shade, making it suitable for standard 8–9-foot ceilings. If your ceiling is below 8 feet, test the proportions by measuring the vertical space above nearby furniture. Many reviewers in apartments report it works well without overwhelming their rooms, but ceiling height is the primary limiting factor.

Does the walnut finish scratch easily, or is it sealed well?

The walnut is treated with a protective urethane coat that resists light scratches. However, direct contact with sharp objects or rough fabric will mark it. You won't babysit the lamp, but don't expect it to stay pristine if you have kids or pets. Touch-up stain pens designed for walnut furniture can mask minor marks if needed.

What bulb type does it use, and are LED options available?

The lamp accepts standard E26 base bulbs. LED bulbs work perfectly and are recommended because they reduce heat on the wooden frame and lower electricity costs. Look for warm-white (2700K) LEDs to match the mid-century aesthetic. The dimming feature works only with dimmable LED bulbs, not all LED options, so verify the bulb packaging before purchasing.

Is the shade removable for cleaning, and can you replace it separately?

Yes, the linen shade is removable and can be vacuumed gently or hand-cleaned with a dry cloth. West Elm sells replacement shades in matching natural linen, which costs $40-60. If the original shade stains or ages, replacing it is a practical option rather than replacing the entire lamp.
