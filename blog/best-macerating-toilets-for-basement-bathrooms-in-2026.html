What Are Macerating Toilets and Why Choose Them for Basements?

Macerating toilets are self-contained units that use a powerful grinding mechanism to break down waste and paper before automatically pumping it upward to your existing drainage system. Unlike traditional gravity-fed toilets, these fixtures can be installed virtually anywhere in your basement, regardless of proximity to main drainage lines. This flexibility makes them ideal for finished basements, home offices, and secondary bathrooms where running new plumbing would be prohibitively expensive.

The technology also means you can install a full basement bathroom without major structural modifications. Simple Project's macerating toilet systems feature advanced impeller technology and quiet operation, ensuring your basement bathroom experience rivals upstairs facilities.
