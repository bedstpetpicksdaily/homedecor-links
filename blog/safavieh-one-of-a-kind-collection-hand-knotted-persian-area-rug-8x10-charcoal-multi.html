Frequently Asked Questions

How much shedding should I expect in the first year?

Expect visible shedding for the first 3-6 months, with gradual reduction afterward. You're not looking at losing the entire rug, but rather loose fibers from the knots settling. Vacuum 2-3 times weekly during this period. This is normal for hand-knotted rugs and doesn't indicate a defect—it's part of the authenticity. Many reviews mention this, so it's not a surprise if you go in expecting it.

Will the charcoal colorway hide dirt effectively?

Charcoal hides foot traffic, dust, and most debris far better than cream or light beige alternatives. However, it can show pet hair if you have light-colored animals, and spills are still visible—you just don't see daily dust as quickly. It's the practical choice for high-traffic areas, which is why it remains popular despite being less 'Instagram-friendly' than lighter tones.

Is 8x10 actually large enough for a living room?

An 8x10 rug works best when all major furniture pieces sit partially on it—think front legs of the sofa and chairs touching the rug. If your living room is over 20x15 feet, this size might feel small and isolate your furniture arrangement. Measure your space and plan furniture placement before ordering. It's generous for bedrooms but potentially undersize for expansive living rooms.

How does this compare to newer area rugs on Amazon?

Most competing 8x10 rugs in this price range are machine-made or power-loomed, which means they're uniform, less durable long-term, and shed less initially but wear faster. The Safavieh's hand-knotted construction costs more upfront but holds texture and durability for 10+ years with proper care. You're paying for longevity, not just initial appearance.
