Frequently Asked Questions

How does matte black compare to brushed nickel or polished brass for pendant lights?

Matte black reads more contemporary and pairs better with mixed metals, while brushed nickel leans formal and brass leans warm-vintage. The Blackspot's matte finish is more forgiving with dust than polished options but requires occasional wiping. If you have existing hardware in brushed nickel or stainless steel in your kitchen, the Blackspot will feel cohesive without blending into invisibility.

Is this fixture bright enough for task lighting over a kitchen island?

The dome shape focuses light downward effectively, but brightness depends entirely on the bulb you install—the fixture itself is just the housing. A standard A19 LED bulb (around 800-1000 lumens) works for ambient-task lighting. For serious prep work, pair it with a higher-lumen bulb or use multiple pendants spaced 24-30 inches apart. Check your bulb wattage limits printed inside the fixture.

Will the matte black finish match my existing Schoolhouse Electric or vintage-style fixtures?

Yes, Schoolhouse Electric maintains consistent matte black finishes across their line, so this will coordinate seamlessly with other pieces from the brand. However, 'vintage matte black' from other manufacturers sometimes reads slightly different—one might be flat while another has subtle texture. If you're mixing brands, order samples or see in person first.
