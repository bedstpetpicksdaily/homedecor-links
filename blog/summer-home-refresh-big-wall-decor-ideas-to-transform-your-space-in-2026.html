Frequently Asked Questions

What size wall art should I choose for my living room?

For most living rooms, aim for oversized canvas prints or statement wall pieces that are at least 40-60% of your wall width. Big Wall Decor offers pieces in various dimensions, so measure your space first and browse their collection for options that match your room's proportions.

How do I choose abstract art that matches my home décor?

Consider your existing color palette, furniture style, and the mood you want to create. Big Wall Decor features abstract art in every color combination imaginable. Look for pieces that either complement your current décor or intentionally contrast for a bold statement.

Are Big Wall Decor's oversized canvas prints easy to hang?

Yes, Big Wall Decor's large wall art pieces come with hanging hardware and instructions. Most oversized canvas prints are lightweight and can be hung on standard drywall with proper wall anchors. For framed art, ensure your wall can support the weight.

Can I use large wall art in small bedrooms?

Absolutely. Oversized canvas prints and statement wall pieces actually work well in smaller spaces as they create an illusion of larger walls. Choose lighter colors or minimal designs from Big Wall Decor's collection to avoid overwhelming the room.

What's the difference between canvas prints and framed art from Big Wall Decor?

Canvas prints offer a modern, gallery-like look and work well for abstract art and contemporary designs. Framed art provides a more traditional, polished appearance. Both are available as oversized options from Big Wall Decor, so choose based on your style preference.
