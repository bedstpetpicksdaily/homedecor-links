Frequently Asked Questions

Will this rug shed? Should I expect fuzzing for months?

Hand-knotted rugs naturally shed loose fibers during the first few weeks—this is normal and unavoidable, not a defect. You'll notice it most in the first 2-4 weeks with regular vacuuming. The 500+ reviews mention this occasionally, but if shedding is still happening significantly after two months, that's worth investigating. Use a vacuum without a beater bar to minimize further damage.

How does the rust color look in different lighting? Is it closer to burnt orange or deep burgundy?

Based on reviewer feedback, this rust leans warm—more burnt orange undertone than burgundy. Natural daylight shows the true color best; incandescent lighting can push it slightly warmer. If you're matching to existing décor, order a swatch first or check return policies, because photos online don't always capture Suzani's natural dye variations accurately.

Is hand-knotted worth the price over a power-loomed Suzani rug?

Hand-knotted offers authentic artisan construction and typically lasts 15-20+ years with proper care; power-loomed versions cost 40-60% less but usually show wear within 5-7 years in high-traffic areas. If you're placing this in a formal living room with moderate foot traffic, hand-knotted justifies the investment. If it's heading to a hallway, bedroom, or kids' play area, a quality power-loomed alternative performs almost identically at lower financial risk.
