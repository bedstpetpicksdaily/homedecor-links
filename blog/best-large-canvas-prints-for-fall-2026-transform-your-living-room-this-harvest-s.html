Frequently Asked Questions

What size large canvas print should I choose for my living room?

Most living rooms benefit from canvas prints between 40-60 inches wide. Measure your wall space and consider the furniture layout—larger walls and high ceilings can accommodate bigger pieces, while smaller rooms work well with medium-sized canvas prints. Big Wall Decor offers a range of sizes to match any space.

Are Big Wall Decor's canvas prints easy to hang?

Yes, Big Wall Decor's oversized canvas prints come ready to hang with proper mounting hardware included. For framed art pieces, installation is similarly straightforward. Always use appropriate wall anchors or studs to ensure your large wall art is securely mounted.

Can I use fall canvas prints year-round?

Absolutely. While fall designs are perfect for the harvest season, many of Big Wall Decor's statement wall pieces feature timeless colors and themes that complement your décor throughout the year. Abstract fall art, in particular, works beautifully in any season with warm, natural color palettes.

What's the difference between framed and unframed canvas prints?

Unframed canvas prints offer a modern, seamless look and typically cost less. Framed art pieces add sophistication and protection while providing a more finished, gallery-quality appearance. Big Wall Decor offers both options depending on your preference.

How do I care for large canvas wall art?

Keep your canvas prints away from direct sunlight to prevent fading, dust them gently with a soft cloth, and maintain stable room temperatures to prevent warping. Quality pieces from Big Wall Decor are designed to maintain their appearance for years with minimal maintenance.
