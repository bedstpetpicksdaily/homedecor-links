Frequently Asked Questions

What's the actual height and will it work on a nightstand next to an average bed?

This lamp sits at approximately 24-26 inches tall (depending on shade), making it perfect for nightstand placement where you need to see over it while lying down. The proportions feel balanced on standard furniture—not so tall it creates awkward shadows, not so short it disappears. Test it against your bedside table height before ordering; if your nightstand is unusually high, this might feel undersized.

Does the aged brass finish tarnish or require special maintenance?

The finish is coated to resist tarnishing, so you won't need special brass polish or protective treatments. Light dusting with a dry microfiber cloth keeps it looking intentional. Unlike raw brass that develops patina rapidly, this aged finish is somewhat stable, though it will naturally evolve slightly over years of use—which actually adds character.

Is the ceramic base prone to chipping if you move it around frequently?

The ceramic is fired solid and reasonably durable for a decorative object, but it's not indestructible. If you're moving this lamp multiple times per year as part of seasonal décor rotations (common for summer furniture rearranging), wrap it carefully or use a lamp moving box. Shelf life is excellent if you're placing it permanently in a bedroom or living room.
