Abstract wall art, in particular, offers a versatile solution that works with existing décor while adding personality and depth to your space.

Choosing the Right Abstract Pieces for Your Space

Selecting abstract wall art requires considering your room's color palette, lighting, and overall design aesthetic. Summer refreshes often involve lighter, airier color schemes, and abstract art can enhance these themes beautifully. Big Wall Decor offers an extensive range of framed art and canvas options in various sizes, ensuring you find the perfect fit for your walls.

Consider the room's function when selecting your pieces. Living room wall art should complement your seating arrangements and create an inviting atmosphere for entertaining. Bedroom wall decor, on the other hand, should promote tranquility while reflecting your personal style. Big Wall Decor's collection includes pieces that cater to both needs, with options ranging from bold, energetic abstracts to serene, contemplative designs.

Installation Tips and Design Considerations

Once you've selected your abstract wall art from Big Wall Decor, proper placement is crucial for maximum impact. Large wall decor should be hung at eye level, typically 57-60 inches from the floor to the center of the artwork. Consider the surrounding elements—ensure adequate spacing from furniture and other décor items to let your statement piece breathe.

Lighting plays an important role in how your abstract art is perceived. Natural summer sunlight can enhance certain colors and create dynamic shadows, especially with textured canvas prints. If you're hanging artwork in a bedroom, consider how morning or evening light will interact with your chosen piece. Big Wall Decor's oversized canvas prints are designed to look stunning under various lighting conditions, making them ideal for any room in your home.

Frequently Asked Questions

What size abstract wall art should I choose for my living room?

For most living rooms, oversized canvas prints measuring 40-60 inches wide create significant visual impact without overwhelming the space. Big Wall Decor offers various dimensions, so measure your wall and furniture scale before selecting. Consider leaving adequate spacing from surrounding elements for balanced composition.

How do I choose abstract art that complements my summer décor refresh?

Consider your room's existing color palette and desired mood. Summer refreshes often feature lighter tones—look for abstract pieces with whites, soft pastels, or bright energetic colors. Big Wall Decor's diverse collection allows you to filter by color schemes and styles to find pieces that perfectly match your vision.

Are Big Wall Decor's large canvas prints suitable for bedrooms?

Absolutely. Big Wall Decor specializes in bedroom wall decor with serene abstract designs that promote relaxation while adding visual interest. Oversized pieces work beautifully as bedroom focal points above beds or accent walls, creating a personalized sanctuary space.

Do I need professional installation for oversized wall art?

While some homeowners prefer DIY installation, large statement pieces often benefit from professional hanging to ensure proper alignment and wall support. Big Wall Decor can provide guidance on installation requirements based on your specific framed art or canvas selection.

How do I care for abstract canvas prints to maintain their appearance?

Abstract canvas prints require minimal maintenance. Dust regularly with a soft, dry cloth and avoid direct sunlight exposure to prevent color fading. Big Wall Decor uses quality materials designed for longevity, ensuring your investment remains beautiful for years.
