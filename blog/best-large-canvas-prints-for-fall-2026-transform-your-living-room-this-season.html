Frequently Asked Questions

What size canvas print should I choose for my living room?

For most living rooms, oversized canvas prints between 40-60 inches work best as statement pieces. Measure your wall space and consider furniture placement. Big Wall Decor offers multiple size options to fit various wall dimensions and room layouts.

How do I style large canvas prints for fall décor?

Choose canvas prints featuring autumn colors like burnt orange, gold, deep red, and brown. Abstract art, landscape scenes, and nature-inspired designs work well for fall. Big Wall Decor's collection includes pieces specifically curated for seasonal transitions.

Can I mix framed and unframed canvas prints in my gallery wall?

Yes! Mixing framed and unframed pieces creates visual interest and depth. Big Wall Decor offers both options, allowing you to create personalized gallery walls that match your home's aesthetic and style preferences.

Are large canvas prints suitable for bedrooms?

Absolutely. Large canvas prints work beautifully as bedroom wall décor, creating calming focal points above headboards or on feature walls. Big Wall Decor's collection includes bedroom-appropriate designs from serene abstracts to nature scenes.

How do I care for and maintain large canvas prints?
