Frequently Asked Questions

What's the actual light output, and will this work as a primary reading light?

The lamp works best as ambient or task lighting on a nightstand or side table, not as your primary reading light for an entire room. If you're using it at a desk or need bright, focused light, pair it with a second light source or consider a lamp with a higher wattage bulb. The linen shade diffuses light softly—that's a feature for ambiance, not a bug for productivity.

Is the putty color actually neutral, or does it lean gray or beige?

Putty is firmly in the warm gray territory—it has a slight beige undertone that keeps it from feeling cool or stark. It pairs beautifully with warm whites, creams, and soft grays. If your space runs very cool (lots of pure whites and blacks), it might feel slightly warm by comparison, but it's neutral enough to work in most settings. See it in person if possible before ordering, as lighting can shift how neutrals read in photos.

Does this lamp actually justify the price compared to cheaper ceramic table lamps on Amazon?

Honestly? You're paying for construction quality and design restraint. Cheaper ceramic lamps often have hollow bases that feel hollow, flimsy switches, and poorly made shades. This lamp feels solid, the switch is responsive, and the linen shade isn't thin or see-through in a bad way. If you buy one lamp every two years versus one lamp every five years, the math favors investing upfront. But if you're renting or space-hopping frequently, the cheaper option might make sense despite the shorter lifespan.
