Frequently Asked Questions

What size oversized canvas print should I choose for my living room?

The ideal size depends on your wall dimensions and furniture layout. Generally, your statement wall piece should be 50-75% the width of your sofa or main furniture piece. Big Wall Decor offers multiple size options so you can find the perfect fit for your living room wall art.

How do I decide between abstract art and other design styles?

Consider your existing décor and personal preferences. Abstract art works well in modern and contemporary spaces, while Big Wall Decor also offers landscapes, botanical designs, and geometric patterns. Browse their collection to see what resonates with your style.

Are oversized canvas prints durable for long-term use?

Yes, quality canvas prints from Big Wall Decor are built to last. They use premium materials and professional printing techniques, ensuring your bedroom wall decor or living room wall art maintains its appearance and color vibrancy for years.

Can I use oversized canvas prints in rental apartments?

Absolutely! Oversized canvas prints can be hung using non-damaging wall hooks. For a more temporary option, Big Wall Decor's framed art can also be displayed on easels or leaned against walls, making them perfect for renters.

How should I care for my canvas prints?

Keep canvas prints away from direct sunlight to prevent fading, and dust them regularly with a soft cloth. Avoid hanging them in high-moisture areas like bathrooms. Big Wall Decor's canvas prints are made with fade-resistant inks for lasting beauty.
