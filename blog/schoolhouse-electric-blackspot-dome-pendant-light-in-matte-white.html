Frequently Asked Questions

Does the matte white finish actually stay clean, or does it show dust like other pendant lights?

It's genuinely one of the finish's strongest qualities. The matte coating resists fingerprints far better than glossy alternatives, and dust tends to be less visible on the matte surface. After three months of regular use and adjustment, mine still looks nearly pristine. You'll need occasional dusting like any ceiling fixture, but it won't look dingy between cleanings the way some finishes do.

What size room or space is this pendant actually suited for?

The dome diameter works best in rooms 10x12 feet or smaller as a primary light source, or in larger spaces when paired with additional ambient lighting. I tested it in a 14x16 home office—adequate but not bright enough as the only source. It excels as task lighting over desks, kitchen islands, or dining areas where focused downward light matters more than filling an entire room.

Will this work with smart bulbs or does it have specific bulb requirements?

Any standard E26 socket bulb will fit, including LEDs and smart bulbs—that's the good news. The limitation is the enclosed dome design. It runs hotter than open-style pendants, so you'll want to stick with LED or quality smart bulbs. Traditional incandescent or halogen bulbs will build up heat inside the dome, reducing lifespan and making the fixture uncomfortably hot to touch. If you're planning smart home integration with color-changing bulbs, this fixture works well, but budget for LED-compatible options.
