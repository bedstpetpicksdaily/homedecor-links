How to Style Your AECOJOY Set for Autumn

Once your set arrives, layering is everything. Swap bright summer throw pillows for rust, ochre, and deep green covers. Add an outdoor rug in a warm pattern to define the space and keep feet off cold concrete. A fire pit or patio heater near your sectional turns a 50-degree evening into perfectly comfortable hangout weather. Finish with lanterns, a chunky knit blanket basket, and a few pumpkins or dried florals on the coffee table — small touches that make guests want to stay another hour.

Also consider traffic flow: pull seating closer together than you would in summer so conversation stays easy and warmth stays shared. And if you own pets, AECOJOY's pet supplies range means your dog can have a weatherproof spot on the patio too — a nice detail that keeps everyone included on chilly evenings.
