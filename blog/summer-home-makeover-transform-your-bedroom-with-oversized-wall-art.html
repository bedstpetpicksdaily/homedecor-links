Frequently Asked Questions

What size oversized wall art should I choose for my bedroom?

Measure your wall space and aim for artwork that covers 60-75% of the wall. As a general rule, oversized pieces should be proportional to your wall dimensions. Big Wall Decor offers various sizes to help you find the perfect fit for your specific bedroom layout.

How do I hang heavy oversized canvas prints safely?

Use appropriate wall anchors rated for your artwork's weight and wall type. For drywall, use toggle bolts or heavy-duty picture hangers. If you're unsure, consult a professional installer. Big Wall Decor's framed art pieces come with hanging hardware for easy installation.

Can abstract art work in traditional bedroom designs?

Absolutely! Abstract art offers flexibility across design styles. Choose pieces with colors and tones that complement traditional elements like wood furniture and classic fabrics. Big Wall Decor's collection includes abstract pieces suitable for both contemporary and traditional spaces.

What's the best way to style a bedroom with oversized wall art?

Ensure your large canvas print or statement wall piece is visually balanced by coordinating bedding, pillows, and accessories with complementary colors. Keep other wall décor minimal to let your oversized piece shine as the focal point.
