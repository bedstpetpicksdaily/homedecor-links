Frequently Asked Questions

How does the polished copper finish hold up to dust and fingerprints?

It shows both. Polished copper in a kitchen or dining area will develop fingerprints noticeably if you have family members who reach up to adjust it. Many owners embrace the natural patina that develops, which actually looks richer and more authentic than mirror-bright polish. If you want it to stay pristine, you're committing to occasional polishing with copper polish or a microfiber cloth. July heat and humidity can accelerate tarnishing, so be aware if you're installing this during peak summer.

Is the light output bright enough for task lighting over a kitchen island?

Depends on your bulb choice and wattage. The Blackspot accepts standard E26 bulbs up to 60W. With a quality warm-white LED (2700K recommended for residential spaces), you'll get adequate task lighting for food prep and plating. However, if your kitchen relies on this fixture as the primary light source, add additional ambient lighting. It's excellent for focused, supplemental lighting—not designed to be your room's only fixture.

How difficult is installation if I don't have existing wiring?

Installation assumes you have an existing junction box and wiring. If you're running new electrical work, hire an electrician—the fixture itself is straightforward to attach once infrastructure is ready. Schoolhouse Electric includes clear instructions, but the work stops at the ceiling box. Plan for electrical labor if you're starting from scratch; the fixture cost is just part of the total project expense.
