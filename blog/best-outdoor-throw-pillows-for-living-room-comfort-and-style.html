What to Avoid

  • Standard Indoor Pillows: Never repurpose your interior throw pillows for outdoor use. Indoor fabrics lack UV protection, absorb moisture, and develop mildew within weeks. They'll quickly deteriorate and look sad rather than stylish in your outdoor room.
  • Cheap Polyester Blends: Avoid pillows made from non-solution-dyed polyester or unknown fabric blends. These materials fade rapidly in sunlight and often develop a chalky appearance within a season, making your outdoor space look neglected rather than curated.
  • Inadequate Fill Materials: Skip pillows filled with down or traditional fiberfill that absorbs water and becomes heavy and unusable when damp. Poor-quality fill also compresses quickly, leaving you with flat, uncomfortable seating accessories that fail to support your back during relaxation.
