Frequently Asked Questions

Do smart toilets really help keep bathrooms cooler?

Yes, smart toilets reduce water volume per flush, which decreases heat generation. When paired with proper ventilation, they significantly improve bathroom temperature control. Simple Project's smart fixtures are specifically designed with thermal efficiency in mind.

What is a macerating toilet and is it suitable for hot climates?

Macerating toilets grind waste and water into fine particles before pumping them to main sewage lines. They're excellent for basement installations and renovations in any climate, including hot weather regions. Simple Project's macerating toilet models maintain consistent performance regardless of temperature.

Can I install smart bathroom fixtures during a renovation?

Absolutely. Smart fixtures are ideal for bathroom renovations, offering an opportunity to upgrade to modern, efficient technology. Simple Project provides comprehensive bathroom fixture solutions that work seamlessly with renovation projects of any size.

What water-saving features do modern smart toilets include?

Modern smart toilets typically feature dual-flush systems, sensor-activated flushing, and water-efficient bowl designs. Simple Project's fixtures often include adjustable flush settings and conservation modes that reduce water usage by up to 30% compared to traditional toilets.
