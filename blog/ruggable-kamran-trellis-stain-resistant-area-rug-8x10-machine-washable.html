Frequently Asked Questions

How often can you actually wash it before it falls apart?

Owners report washing the top layer every 2-4 weeks for 2+ years without significant wear or color fading. The key is using cold water and mild detergent—hot water or bleach voids the durability claim. The stitching holds up better than budget washable rugs, which is why you see so many positive reviews from long-term owners.

Does the stain-resistant treatment really work, or do you still need to blot immediately?

It's not magic, but it buys you time. Spills sit longer without soaking in compared to untreated rugs—you have maybe 10-15 minutes instead of 2-3 minutes before staining becomes permanent. Blotting immediately is still the move, but the window is wider, which matters when you're juggling multiple kids or dealing with pet accidents.

Is the Kamran Trellis better than other washable rugs in the same price range?

The trellis pattern genuinely hides dirt better than solid-color competitors, and the two-layer construction is more stable than single-layer washable rugs. However, Ruggable's other patterns and competing brands like Safavieh's washable line offer similar performance at slightly lower prices. Choose Kamran Trellis if the pattern fits your décor; don't pay extra for the name alone.
