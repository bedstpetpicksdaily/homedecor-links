Frequently Asked Questions

How much shedding should I expect with a new Beni Ourain rug?

Excessive shedding is normal during the first 3-6 months. This is inherent to hand-knotted wool and doesn't indicate defect. Vacuum regularly during this period. After the initial shedding phase, the rug stabilizes and sheds minimally. This makes new wool rugs problematic for allergy-prone households during the first season.

Will a 9x12 rug actually fit in a standard living room?

A 9x12 is designed for large living rooms and open-concept spaces where it can anchor the entire seating arrangement. In a standard 12x15 living room, the 9x12 works well. In smaller rooms (under 12x14), it may feel overcrowded. The 8x10 is the safer choice if you're uncertain about your room size—it anchors furniture without overwhelming compact spaces.

Is hand-knotted construction actually better than machine-made, or is that marketing?

Hand-knotted construction is genuinely superior for long-term durability. Individual knots won't unravel if pulled, and the rug maintains structural integrity even under heavy foot traffic for 10+ years. Machine-made rugs flatten after 1-2 seasons because the backing fails. The trade-off is cost—hand-knotted rugs cost 2-3x more upfront but last proportionally longer.

Do I need a rug pad, and will this work over hardwood?

Yes, use a rug pad on hardwood or laminate floors. Without padding, the rug will slip and potentially damage both the rug and floor. Rug pads also prevent moisture buildup underneath, extending the rug's lifespan. Over carpet, a pad is optional but still recommended to prevent shifting.

How do the $10 budget options compare to mid-range Beni Ourain rugs?

Ultra-low pricing ($10 for 9x12) raises durability questions that aren't resolved in customer reviews. Mid-range options ($300-$500) have 500+ verified reviews at 4.3 stars, providing genuine evidence of construction quality. Unless you're testing whether the style works in your home, the price difference is minimal compared to the 2-3x durability gap between budget and mid-range hand-knotted wool.

Will the cream color yellow or gray over time?

Genuine wool develops a natural patina over years—slight color shift is normal and adds character rather than indicating damage. The cream and charcoal colorway is specifically chosen because it resists visible dust and staining better than pure white. Light vacuuming maintains appearance; professional cleaning every 18-24 months prevents deep discoloration.
