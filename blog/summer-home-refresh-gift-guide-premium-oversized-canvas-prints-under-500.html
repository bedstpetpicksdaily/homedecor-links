Frequently Asked Questions

What size oversized canvas print should I choose for my living room?

For most living rooms, oversized canvas prints in the 40x60 inch or 36x48 inch range work best as a statement wall above sofas or main furniture pieces. Measure your wall space and furniture proportions—generally, artwork should cover about 50-75% of wall space above a sofa for optimal impact.

Are canvas prints from Big Wall Decor suitable for bedrooms?

Absolutely. Big Wall Decor's bedroom wall decor collection features calming abstract art and landscapes perfect for rest spaces. Slightly smaller sizes (24x36 or 30x40 inches) often work better in bedrooms than living rooms, creating visual interest without overwhelming the space.

How do I maintain and clean oversized canvas prints?

Dust your large wall art regularly with a soft, dry cloth. Avoid direct sunlight exposure to prevent fading, and keep prints away from moisture and humidity. Big Wall Decor uses fade-resistant inks, but proper care extends the life and vibrancy of your statement wall pieces.

Can I use multiple oversized canvas prints together in one room?

Yes! Creating a gallery wall with multiple pieces from Big Wall Decor's collection is a popular design strategy. Mix different sizes, maintain consistent spacing, and coordinate colors or themes to create a cohesive, curated look on your large wall art display.

What's the difference between canvas prints and framed art?

Canvas prints are typically stretched over wooden frames with gallery-wrap edges, creating a modern, seamless look. Framed art includes traditional frames around the artwork. Big Wall Decor offers both options—choose based on your décor style and the aesthetic you want to achieve.
