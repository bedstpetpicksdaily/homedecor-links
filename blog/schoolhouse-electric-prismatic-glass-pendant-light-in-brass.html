Frequently Asked Questions

How does this compare to cheaper brass pendant lights on Amazon?

The primary differences are materials consistency and customer service. Schoolhouse Electric uses higher-grade brass that won't tarnish or discolor unpredictably, and their hand-blown glass is more uniform than budget Chinese-manufactured alternatives. Budget pendants ($40-60 range) do the job functionally, but the glass tends to be thinner, the brass plating flakes after 2-3 years, and you'll likely replace it sooner. For a temporary setup or rental, save the money. For permanence, Schoolhouse justifies the premium.

Will the brass hardware work with mid-century modern or industrial decor?

Absolutely—brass is experiencing a real moment precisely because it bridges styles. The warm tone works beautifully in mid-century spaces, softens industrial aesthetics, and grounds maximalist bohemian rooms. The prismatic glass leans slightly boho-contemporary, so it works best in homes that already embrace some design personality rather than strict minimalism or ultra-modern spaces.

How does the light output compare to standard pendant lights?

Real talk: prismatic glass is beautiful but it diffuses light. You won't get the punch of a bare bulb or clear glass pendant. Plan to pair this with a slightly higher wattage LED bulb (60-75W equivalent) if you're using it as your primary task lighting. Over a dining table or kitchen island where ambiance matters more than high brightness, it's perfect. As the only light source in a small bathroom? You might need supplemental lighting.

Is the cord length adjustable, and can I customize the drop?

Most versions come with 3-6 feet of cord depending on the listing, and it's fully adjustable. An electrician can shorten or lengthen it, though going much longer than 8 feet requires additional wiring complexity. The standard length works well for 8-9 foot ceilings; if you have higher ceilings, confirm the cord length before purchasing since that's a common frustration point in the reviews.

Does this fixture come in other finishes besides brass?

Schoolhouse Electric offers variations—some listings show copper, nickel, or black powder-coat options alongside brass. The prismatic glass is typically consistent, but the metal hardware changes the entire vibe. Brass is the most versatile across decor styles, but definitely check your specific product listing since finishes vary by retailer and restock cycles.
