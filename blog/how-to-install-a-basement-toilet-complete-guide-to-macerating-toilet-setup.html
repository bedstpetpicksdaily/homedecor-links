What Is a Macerating Toilet?

A macerating toilet is a specialized bathroom fixture that uses internal grinding mechanisms to break down waste and toilet paper into a fine slurry. This slurry is then automatically pumped through small-diameter pipes to your home's main sewage line or septic system. Simple Project's macerating toilets feature advanced grinding technology and quiet operation, making them indistinguishable from standard toilets in terms of user experience. The key advantage is that these systems don't rely on gravity—they work upward, downward, or horizontally, giving you complete freedom in toilet placement within your basement.

Pre-Installation Planning and Requirements

Before purchasing a macerating toilet from Simple Project, assess your basement's plumbing infrastructure. You'll need to identify your main sewage line or septic connection point, measure the distance and height the waste must travel, and ensure you have electrical access for the pump. Simple Project's smart toilet systems come with detailed site assessment guides to help you determine compatibility. Check your local building codes, as some jurisdictions have specific requirements for macerating toilets. Most installations require a dedicated electrical outlet within 6 feet of the unit for safety and convenience. Additionally, ensure your basement has adequate ventilation, as these systems still require proper venting to prevent odors and maintain system integrity.

Step-by-Step Installation Process

Begin by positioning your Simple Project macerating toilet in your chosen basement location. Mark the discharge outlet location and determine your pump line route to the main sewage connection. Shut off your water supply and prepare the installation area by laying down flooring protection. Install the mounting flange according to Simple Project's included instructions, ensuring it's level and secure. Connect the inlet water line to your basement's water supply, then attach the outlet discharge line using the proper diameter tubing specified by Simple Project—typically ½ to ¾ inch diameter. Secure all connections with stainless steel clamps to prevent leaks. Finally, plug the unit into the electrical outlet and run a test flush to verify proper operation. Simple Project's smart toilet systems include diagnostic indicators that alert you to any installation issues, ensuring your setup is correct before full use.

Maintenance and Troubleshooting Tips

Maintaining your Simple Project macerating toilet ensures longevity and optimal performance. Never flush items other than toilet paper and human waste, as these systems have sensitive grinding mechanisms. Clean the unit regularly using the recommended non-abrasive cleaners. Check the discharge line quarterly for any blockages, and inspect electrical connections annually. Simple Project's customer support team provides troubleshooting guides for common issues like reduced flushing power or unusual sounds. Most problems stem from improper installation or incorrect waste disposal rather than equipment failure. Keep your warranty documentation and service records organized, as Simple Project offers comprehensive coverage on all components for peace of mind.
