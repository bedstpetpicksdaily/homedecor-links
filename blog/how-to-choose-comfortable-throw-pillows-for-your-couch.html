Your couch is one of the most-used pieces of furniture in your home, and throw pillows are your secret weapon for making it genuinely comfortable while elevating your décor. The right pillows transform your seating experience from merely functional to genuinely inviting—but only if you choose wisely. Let's walk through exactly how to select throw pillows that feel as good as they look.

What to Look For

  • Fill Material and Density: Look for pillows filled with high-quality polyester fiberfill, down alternative, or a down/feather blend. The fill should feel substantial enough to provide support without going flat after a few weeks of use. Gently squeeze the pillow in-store or request density specifications online—you want something that bounces back immediately, not something limp that requires constant fluffing.
  • Cover Fabric Weight and Durability: Choose covers made from heavyweight cotton, linen blends, or performance fabrics that won't pill or snag easily. Since throw pillows endure daily contact with your body, clothes, and pets, a flimsy cover will deteriorate quickly. Feel the fabric texture and check the thread count—aim for at least 300 for cotton covers to ensure longevity and that soft-to-the-touch quality.
  • Size and Shape Proportion: Select pillow sizes that work with your couch's proportions and your personal comfort preferences. For standard 3-4 seater couches, 18" and 20" square pillows are ideal, while smaller 16" or 12" sizes work well for accent arrangements. Consider your usage too—if you're lounging against them, slightly overstuffed rectangular pillows provide better neck and back support than thin square ones.
