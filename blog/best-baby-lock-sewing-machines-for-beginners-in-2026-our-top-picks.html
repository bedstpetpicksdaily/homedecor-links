Frequently Asked Questions

Are Baby Lock sewing machines good for complete beginners?

Absolutely. Baby Lock designs machines with beginners in mind, featuring automatic threading, pre-set stitches, clear instructions, and responsive customer support to help you learn confidently.

What's the difference between Baby Lock sewing machines and sergers?

Sewing machines handle traditional stitching and are great for piecing fabric together. Sergers finish raw edges with multiple threads simultaneously, adding a professional touch to seams. Many beginners benefit from starting with a serger.

Do Baby Lock machines include embroidery capabilities?

Many Baby Lock models offer embroidery functionality, though some are dedicated embroidery machines. Check individual product specifications to confirm embroidery features for your preferred model.
