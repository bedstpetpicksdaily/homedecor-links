EWA Eco-Wood-Art uses premium, sustainably-sourced wood that delivers a solid, premium feel in hand. Environmental Impact and Sustainability

Plastic models contribute to global pollution and take centuries to decompose. EWA Eco-Wood-Art commits to eco-friendly manufacturing practices using responsibly harvested wood and non-toxic finishes.
