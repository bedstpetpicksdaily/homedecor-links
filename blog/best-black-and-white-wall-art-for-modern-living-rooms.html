What to Look For

  • Scale and Proportion: Modern living rooms benefit from statement pieces that command attention without overwhelming the space. Look for artwork that covers at least 60% of the wall space above your furniture, or consider gallery walls with consistent 2-3 inch spacing between frames. The right size creates visual impact and prevents the room from feeling sparse or scattered.
  • Contrast and Clarity: Black and white art works best in modern interiors when the contrast is clean and purposeful. Seek out pieces with strong lines, geometric patterns, or high-definition photography that maintains visual interest from across the room. Avoid overly gray or muddy pieces that lack definition—modern spaces thrive on crisp, intentional contrast.
  • Frame Style That Complements: The frame matters as much as the art itself in modern living rooms. Opt for sleek metal frames (black, white, or natural wood), thin floating frames, or no frame at all for canvas prints. Your frame should echo other hardware in the room—think your lamp bases, curtain rods, or decorative accents—to create cohesive design flow.
