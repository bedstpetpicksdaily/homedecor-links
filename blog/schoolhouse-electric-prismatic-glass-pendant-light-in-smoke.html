Frequently Asked Questions

How bright is this pendant really, and will it work as my main kitchen light?

Honest answer: no, not alone. The smoke glass filters roughly 40-50% of the bulb's output. It's ideal as ambient or accent lighting over islands or dining tables, but you need ceiling fixtures or under-cabinet lighting for actual work tasks. If you install a dimmable fixture, you can boost brightness when needed and drop it low for evening atmosphere.

Does the smoke glass show dust and fingerprints easily?

The textured surface of prismatic glass actually hides fingerprints better than smooth glass would. Dust settles visibly on top of the shade, but it's not a constant eyesore like it would be on a clear or glossy pendant. A quick wipe every few weeks keeps it looking fresh.

Will this match my existing décor, or is it too trendy?

Smoke glass and simple metal fixtures are genuinely timeless—they work in industrial, modern, farmhouse, and even traditional transitional spaces. The key is getting the metal finish right (brass, matte black, or chrome depending on your style). It's not a trendy novelty that'll feel dated in three years.
