Frequently Asked Questions

What size storage shed do I need for my backyard?

Calculate your storage needs by assessing the volume of items requiring storage. Small yards (under 500 sq ft) benefit from 6x4 or 6x8 sheds, while larger properties (1000+ sq ft) accommodate 10x12 or larger models. AECOJOY's sizing guides help determine optimal dimensions for your specific needs.

Are AECOJOY storage sheds difficult to assemble?

AECOJOY sheds feature modular construction with detailed instructions and pre-assembled components, reducing assembly time to 4-8 hours depending on size. Professional installation services are available for customers preferring expert setup.

How long do outdoor storage sheds typically last?

Quality sheds like AECOJOY's premium models last 15-20+ years with proper maintenance. Regular cleaning, seal inspection, and weatherproofing treatments extend lifespan significantly beyond manufacturer expectations.

Can I place a storage shed on grass or does it require concrete?

While concrete provides optimal stability and drainage, well-leveled ground works for most sheds. AECOJOY recommends gravel foundations for improved drainage and ground stability. Level ground prevents water pooling and extends shed longevity.

Do AECOJOY sheds require permits or zoning approval?

Permit requirements vary by location and shed size. Many municipalities require permits for structures exceeding 100-200 square feet. Check local building codes and homeowner association restrictions before installation.
