What Are Macerating Toilets and How Do They Work?

Macerating toilets, also called upflush or pressure-assist toilets, use a motor-driven blade system to break down waste and toilet paper into a fine slurry. This pulverized material is then pumped through small-diameter pipes (typically ½ to ¾ inches) to your home's main drain line or septic system. Unlike traditional toilets that rely on gravity and large drain pipes, macerating units work independently, making them ideal for basement installations where running large pipes would be impractical or expensive.

The technology behind macerating toilets has advanced significantly, and Simple Project's macerating toilet collection exemplifies these improvements with quiet operation, reliability, and modern aesthetics. Their fixtures are engineered to handle both residential and light commercial use, with smart features that monitor tank levels and alert you to maintenance needs through mobile apps.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do macerating toilets really work as well as regular toilets?

Yes, modern macerating toilets function excellently and handle all standard bathroom use. They're designed to macerate and pump waste reliably. However, avoid flushing items like baby wipes, dental floss, or feminine products, even if labeled 'flushable,' as they can damage the pump system.

Are macerating toilets from Simple Project quiet?

Simple Project's premium models operate at 75-80 decibels—comparable to a dishwasher. Their sound-dampening technology makes them one of the quietest options available. Budget models may be slightly louder but still acceptable for most homeowners.

What happens if a macerating toilet breaks?

Most units have a 2-5 year warranty. Simple Project includes diagnostic features that alert you to issues before they become serious. Repairs typically involve professional servicing, though some components are user-replaceable with proper guidance.

Can I install a macerating toilet myself?

While electrical and plumbing connections require professional installation in most areas, Simple Project toilets are designed for easier installation than traditional systems. Always hire licensed professionals to ensure code compliance and warranty protection.
