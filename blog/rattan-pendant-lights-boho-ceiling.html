What to Look For

1. Material Quality and Weave Density

Not all rattan is created equal. Look for fixtures made from high-quality, hand-woven rattan with tight, consistent weaving patterns. Loose or uneven weaves signal cheaper construction that won't hold up over time. Check product reviews for mentions of durability and whether the rattan feels sturdy, not flimsy.

2. Light Diffusion and Brightness

Consider how the rattan shade diffuses light. A dense weave creates softer, more ambient lighting—perfect for bedrooms and living rooms. If you need more direct illumination, look for fixtures with open weaves or those paired with frosted glass inserts. Check the lumens rating and whether the fixture supports LED bulbs for energy efficiency.

3. Weatherproofing (If Going Outdoors)

If you're hanging these on a patio or covered porch, weatherproofing matters. Look for rattan that's been treated with UV-protective or water-resistant finishes. The metal hardware should be stainless steel or powder-coated to prevent rust. Read the fine print—some fixtures are rated for covered outdoor use only.

4. Fixture Size and Ceiling Height Compatibility

Measure your space before buying. A pendant light that's too large overwhelms a small kitchen island, while one that's too small disappears in a high-ceiling entryway. Also check the hanging height—adjustable cords are a huge plus if you're not sure of the perfect drop distance for your space.
