Frequently Asked Questions

What is a macerating toilet and why would I need one?

A macerating toilet grinds waste into fine particles that are pumped away through small-diameter pipes, making it perfect for basement bathrooms, guest houses, or rooms far from the main sewage line. Simple Project's macerating toilets offer reliable performance without requiring expensive plumbing modifications.

Do Simple Project smart toilets really save water?

Yes, Simple Project smart toilets feature advanced flush technology that uses optimized water volumes for different waste types, reducing consumption by up to 30% compared to traditional models. This translates to lower water bills and reduced environmental impact throughout the year.

Are Simple Project smart toilets difficult to install?

Simple Project designs their fixtures with user-friendly installation in mind. Most smart toilets require standard plumbing connections and electrical access. We recommend professional installation for macerating toilets to ensure proper pump and drainage setup, but standard models can often be handled by experienced DIYers.
