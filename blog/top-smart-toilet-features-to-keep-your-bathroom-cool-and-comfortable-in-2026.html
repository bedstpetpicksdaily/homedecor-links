Frequently Asked Questions

What makes smart toilets better than traditional toilets?

Smart toilets offer personalized comfort features like heated seats, bidet functions, and air drying that traditional toilets cannot provide. They also improve hygiene, reduce water waste, and integrate with home automation systems for enhanced convenience.

Are smart toilets suitable for basement bathrooms?

Yes, absolutely. Simple Project offers macerating toilets specifically designed for basement installations. These models handle uphill drainage challenges while providing all the comfort features of standard smart toilets, making them ideal for basement bathroom renovations.

How much electricity do smart toilets consume?

Modern smart toilets, including those from Simple Project, are highly energy-efficient. They typically use minimal electricity—comparable to leaving a small lamp on continuously. Most models include energy-saving modes that reduce consumption during periods of non-use.

Can I install a smart toilet myself?

While some users can install standard smart toilets, professional installation is recommended for optimal performance and warranty coverage. For macerating toilets or complex bathroom renovations, hiring a professional plumber ensures proper setup and functionality.
