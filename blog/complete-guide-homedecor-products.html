Frequently Asked Questions

What home decor products should I buy first when decorating a room?

Start with the largest pieces like furniture and window treatments, then layer in lighting fixtures and wall decor. This approach creates the foundation for your design before adding smaller accessories like throw pillows, plants, and artwork that tie everything together.

How do I choose home decor colors that won't look dated?

Use neutral colors (white, gray, beige) as your base and add color through easily replaceable items like pillows, rugs, and artwork. This allows you to update trends without investing in expensive furniture or wall paint every few years.

What home decor products are best for small spaces?

Focus on multi-functional furniture, vertical storage solutions, mirrors, and light-colored decor to make spaces feel larger. Avoid oversized pieces and cluttered accessories—instead, choose a few high-impact statement items that won't overwhelm your room.

Are expensive home decor brands worth the cost compared to budget options?

It depends on the product—invest in high-quality pieces you use daily (sofas, beds, lighting) while saving on trendy accessories you can replace affordably. Read reviews and check material quality rather than brand names to find the best value for your specific needs.
