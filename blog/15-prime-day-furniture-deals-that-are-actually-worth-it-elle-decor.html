Why This Matters

Furniture is one of the largest purchases most people make for their homes, and it's easy to get caught up in percentage discounts without considering quality, durability, or whether a piece truly fits your aesthetic. Prime Day's limited-time offerings create urgency, which can lead to impulse buys that don't align with your decorating goals.
