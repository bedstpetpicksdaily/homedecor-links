Frequently Asked Questions

How does wool hold up to pet hair and stains?

Wool naturally resists liquid absorption better than most synthetics, giving you a window to blot spills before they set. Pet hair sits on top of the pile rather than embedding into it, making vacuuming effective. The charcoal color also masks light shedding that's normal in the first few months. However, wool isn't stain-proof—professional cleaning every 18-24 months keeps it looking intentional and prevents odor buildup from pet accidents.

Will this rug slip on hardwood or tile?

Hand-knotted construction and wool weight provide natural grip on most floor types, but you'll want a non-slip rug pad underneath on slick surfaces like polished concrete or tile. This adds $20-40 to your investment but prevents sliding and protects your flooring from friction damage. It's not included with the rug, so budget for it separately.
