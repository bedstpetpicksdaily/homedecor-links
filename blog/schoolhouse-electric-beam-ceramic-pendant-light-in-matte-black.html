Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Beam's ceramic diffusion compare to frosted glass pendants?

Ceramic creates a warmer, more diffused light because the material has slight translucency rather than the sharper opacity of frosted glass. The light spreads in a gentler cone, which I noticed most clearly in task lighting situations like over kitchen islands—no harsh shadows, but also no diffusion-related dimness. Glass pendants in the same category tend toward either clinical brightness or frustrating dimness; ceramic splits the difference.

Is this pendant dimmable, and does the ceramic affect dimming performance?

The fixture itself is compatible with dimmers (check your specific model's specifications), and the ceramic actually behaves beautifully with dimmed light—it doesn't create weird flickering or color shifts like some materials do. However, dimming does require a compatible bulb and dimmer switch; standard setups work fine, but if you have older dimming infrastructure, verify compatibility before purchasing.

How does the matte black finish hold up to fingerprints and dust in kitchens?

Matte finishes are inherently more forgiving than glossy ones for showing fingerprints, which is the primary advantage here. Dust accumulation is visible but manageable with regular cleaning—I'd recommend a soft microfiber cloth monthly in kitchen environments where moisture and cooking residue are factors. The finish itself doesn't chip or wear noticeably; it's genuinely durable lacquer, not a quick spray coat.
