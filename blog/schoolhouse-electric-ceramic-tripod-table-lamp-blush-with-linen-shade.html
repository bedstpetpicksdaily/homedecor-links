The total height is approximately 24-26 inches depending on shade positioning and bulb selection. This makes it suitable for bedside tables and side tables but too short for reading over a sofa. Measure your specific table before assuming it'll provide adequate task lighting for your situation.

Does the 4.3-star rating mean most people love it or just that it's adequate?

The core design—the ceramic base, tripod structure, and shade quality—consistently earns praise. Most criticism stems from circumstances outside the lamp's actual performance.

Can you use this in a nursery or child's room safely?

Technically yes, but cautiously. The ceramic base is heavy enough not to tip easily, but the tripod legs create trip hazards in small feet zones. If your child tends to grab dangling cords or climb furniture, this lamp's placement matters significantly. Position it well away from crib or bed edges, and avoid rooms where toddlers are still learning spatial awareness.

How does blush compare to other Schoolhouse Electric lamp colors in real rooms?

Blush is warmer than white or cream but more neutral than coral or pink. In natural daylight, it reads as a soft peachy-neutral. Under warm bulbs (2700K), it deepens slightly toward rose tones. Under cooler bulbs, it stays peachy. This flexibility actually makes it more versatile than the color name suggests, but you need warm-temperature bulbs to achieve the softness the product photos promise.
