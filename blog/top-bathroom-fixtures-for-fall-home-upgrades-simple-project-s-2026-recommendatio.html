A macerating toilet grinds waste and uses a pump to move it through small-diameter pipes. They're ideal for basement bathrooms, guest houses, or any location where traditional plumbing isn't feasible. Simple Project's macerating toilets eliminate costly excavation and make difficult installations affordable.
