Will the brass hardware tarnish or require maintenance?

Yes, brass naturally develops patina—and that's intentional design here, not a flaw. Within 6-12 months of regular use, you'll notice the brass darkening and developing character. This isn't corrosion; it's the metal aging beautifully, which is why brass fixtures stay desirable long-term. If you prefer the shiny polished look, you can wipe it down monthly with a soft cloth, but resisting that urge and letting it patina is the move most design-forward buyers make. The ceramic won't tarnish; the glaze holds up indefinitely under normal indoor conditions.

Is this fixture dimmable, and what bulb type works best?

Dimmability depends on your home's wiring setup and bulb choice—the fixture itself accommodates dimmers, but you need compatible LED or incandescent bulbs. Most reviewers report success with standard A19 LED bulbs rated for dimmer use. The ceramic diffusion actually works in your favor here; it softens harsh lighting and reduces the 'cheap LED' aesthetic you get with bare bulbs. July installations benefit from testing your specific bulb setup before committing to it permanently, since summer lighting conditions help you visualize how the fixture performs at different brightness levels throughout the year.
