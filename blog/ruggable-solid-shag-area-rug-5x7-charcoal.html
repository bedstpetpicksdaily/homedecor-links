Frequently Asked Questions

Can you really throw this in a regular washing machine?

Yes—use cold water, gentle cycle, and mild detergent. Skip bleach and fabric softener. The backing is specifically designed to withstand machine washing without degrading. Multiple reviewers report washing theirs 4+ times per year without issues. A standard front-loader works fine; top-loaders with agitators may cause excess wear.

Does the charcoal color fade after washing?

Minimal fading reported across reviews. The dye appears set well, though you may notice a very slight lightening after 3-4 washes. For a charcoal color, this is actually ideal—it means initial dye runoff won't stain your machine on the first wash. Wash in cold water to preserve color intensity.
