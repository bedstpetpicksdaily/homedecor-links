Understanding Macerating Toilet Technology

Macerating toilets are engineered with a hidden grinding chamber beneath the bowl that pulverizes waste into fine particles. Once liquefied, a powerful pump forces the material through a ¾-inch discharge line connected to your main sewer or septic system. This innovation eliminates the need for costly below-grade plumbing modifications, making basement bathrooms financially feasible for most homeowners. Simple Project specializes in macerating solutions that combine durability with ease of installation, featuring units with ceramic bowls and whisper-quiet operation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do macerating toilets work with septic systems?

Yes, most macerating toilets, including Simple Project's models, work effectively with septic systems. The ground waste is safer and sometimes preferable for septic tanks since it's already broken down. Always verify compatibility with your specific system before installation.

How loud are Simple Project's macerating toilets?

Simple Project's modern macerating toilets operate at approximately 80-85 decibels during the macerating cycle—comparable to a garbage disposal. Premium models feature enhanced insulation reducing noise to 75-80 decibels, making them suitable for basements adjacent to living spaces.

Can I install a macerating toilet myself?

Many homeowners successfully install Simple Project's macerating toilets using their detailed guides, though basic plumbing and electrical knowledge helps. For complex installations or if you're uncomfortable with electrical work, hiring a professional plumber ensures warranty coverage and proper system operation.

What's the lifespan of a macerating toilet?

With proper maintenance, Simple Project's macerating toilets last 10-15 years. Regular cleaning, annual inspections, and prompt repairs extend longevity. The macerating pump typically lasts 7-10 years before requiring replacement—a $300-500 service depending on the model.

Are smart toilet features worth the extra cost?

Simple Project's smart toilet models cost $400-800 more than standard units but offer daily comfort and water-saving benefits. Features like heated seats, bidet functions, and automatic flushing add convenience and can reduce water usage by 30%, making them worthwhile for primary bathrooms or luxury basements.
