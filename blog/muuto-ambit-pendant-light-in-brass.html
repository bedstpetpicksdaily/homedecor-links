Frequently Asked Questions

Does the brass really develop a patina, and is that a problem?

Yes, and absolutely not. The patina development is one of the Ambit's best features. Within 3-4 months of regular use, the initial sheen mellows into a warmer, more sophisticated finish. Muuto uses a specific brass alloy that patinas beautifully without tarnishing unevenly. If you prefer the bright finish indefinitely, a clear lacquer application during installation preserves it, but I'd argue that defeats the purpose of choosing brass in the first place.

Will it work over my kitchen island if I have 9-foot ceilings?

Technically yes, but with caveats. The standard cord length tops out around 59 inches, which positions the fixture roughly 14-18 inches below a standard 36-inch-height fixture mount. At 9 feet, you're looking at extension cord purchases or hiring an electrician to extend the wiring. Plan for an extra $50-150 in supplementary hardware. Measure your specific height and suspension distance before ordering.

Is this dimmable, and does it work with smart home systems?

The fixture itself is dimmable, but only with compatible dimmer switches—not all standard dimmers work reliably due to the wiring configuration. For smart home integration, you'll want a dimmer-compatible smart bulb rather than trying to control it through wall switches. Several reviewers reported flickering with incompatible dimmers, so verify compatibility with your specific system before installation. Muuto's official documentation should clarify this; if it doesn't, contact them directly.
