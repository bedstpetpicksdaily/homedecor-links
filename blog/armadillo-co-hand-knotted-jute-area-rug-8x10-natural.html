Frequently Asked Questions

How much shedding happens with this jute rug in the first month?

Moderate shedding is normal for all jute rugs—expect loose fibers on your vacuum for 2-3 weeks, then it tapers off dramatically. Hand-knotted versions like this one shed less than machine-loomed alternatives because the construction is tighter. I'd vacuum daily for the first two weeks, then normal vacuuming after that. Using a rug pad underneath also reduces shedding by minimizing fiber agitation from movement.

Can you use this rug in a kitchen or dining area?

Technically yes, but practically no—not unless spills are cleaned within minutes. Jute absorbs liquid quickly, and stains become permanent fixtures. I tested a small spill in week two, and even after immediate blotting, the spot remains visible three weeks later. Kitchens and dining rooms need stain-resistant synthetic fibers or washable rugs. This is best reserved for living rooms, bedrooms, and home offices where spill risk is lower.
