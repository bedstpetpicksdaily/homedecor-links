Frequently Asked Questions

How many square feet does the Orion Pro adequately light?

With nine LED bulbs operating at standard wattage, this fixture comfortably lights 120–180 square feet for general ambient purposes. Kitchens and dining areas benefit from supplemental task or accent lighting. If this is your only overhead light in a space larger than 200 square feet, you'll notice dim corners. That's by design—sputniks prioritize visual interest over uniform illumination.

Will the brushed nickel finish match my existing lighting and hardware?

Only if your other fixtures and hardware are also brushed nickel or polished chrome. It's fundamentally different from brass, copper, or matte black. Check your cabinet hardware, other light fixtures, and appliance handles before committing. Mixing finishes intentionally can work, but accidental mismatch reads as confused decorating.

Can I use any standard LED bulb, or does Brightech require proprietary bulbs?

Standard E26/E27 socket LED bulbs work fine—Brightech doesn't lock you into proprietary options. We recommend 60-watt-equivalent daylight LEDs (5000K) for kitchens and 40-watt-equivalent warm white (2700K) for dining rooms. Avoid dimmable bulbs unless your existing wiring supports dimmer switches, which many older homes don't without renovation.
