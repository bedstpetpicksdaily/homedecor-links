Frequently Asked Questions

Will this rug work on top of hardwood floors without sliding?

Hand-knotted wool naturally grips hardwood better than synthetic rugs because of the fiber texture, but you should still use a non-slip rug pad underneath—especially in high-traffic zones like entryways. A quality pad costs $30-50 and prevents accidents while protecting your floors. Worth it.

How often do I actually need to clean a wool rug this size?

Light vacuuming every 1-2 weeks with a brush attachment is ideal; skip the beater bar for the first year while shedding is active. Deep cleaning every 18-24 months through a professional or quality steam cleaner keeps it looking fresh. The indigo dye is stable with both methods, so you're not locked into dry-clean-only paranoia.

Is the indigo color going to match my current décor, or is it too bold?

Indigo reads as a sophisticated navy rather than a statement color—it pairs with grays, whites, warm wood tones, and even terra cotta accents. The diamond pattern adds visual interest without being chaotic. If your existing color palette is neutral or cool-toned, this rug bridges the gap between boring and overwhelming. See actual reviews on Amazon showing room photos to verify fit with your aesthetic.

What makes hand-knotted better than power-loomed for this price range?

Hand-knotted means individual fibers are literally tied, not woven by machine. This creates density and durability that catches every spill rather than letting it seep through, lasts 2-3x longer under heavy foot traffic, and actually becomes softer and more character-filled with age. Power-loomed alternatives shed faster, wear thin quicker, and feel plasticky by comparison.
