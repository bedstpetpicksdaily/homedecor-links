Frequently Asked Questions

How does this compare to handmade macramé from independent sellers?

Handmade pieces offer customization and often tighter knots, but you're paying 1.5-2x the price and waiting weeks. This mass-produced option is machine-consistent—every piece has identical knot structure and fringe density. For 90% of buyers, the consistency and price advantage outweighs the 'handmade' appeal. Handmade wins if you want custom sizing or colors; this wins if you want reliable quality at a fair price.

Will the cream color stay bright or yellow over time?

Cream is naturally more stable than pure white because yellowing is less visible. Owners report minimal color shift after 12+ months even with direct window exposure. If your room gets intense afternoon sun (south or west-facing), consider the fringe will age slightly faster than the main body—that's normal for any natural fiber textile. Rotating the piece 90 degrees every 3-4 months evens out any exposure.

What wall anchors do I actually need for this?

The piece typically weighs 2-3 pounds depending on fringe density. Standard drywall anchors handle this easily, but the issue is the hanging hardware attachment point itself. Use heavy-duty anchors rated for at least 5 pounds or go into wall studs if possible. Many buyers make the mistake of using standard picture hangers—they fail. Invest in proper heavy-duty anchors ($8-12) and save yourself the frustration.
