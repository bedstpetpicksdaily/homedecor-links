Frequently Asked Questions

Is this rug too delicate for kids and pets?

No. Wool is naturally durable and stain-resistant, making it surprisingly forgiving. The cream and black colorway hides spills exceptionally well. That said, you'll want a quality rug pad underneath to prevent it from sliding if you have runners (kids and pets on hardwood floors). Professional cleaning every 18-24 months will keep it looking intentional even with heavy use.

How much will this rug shed, and for how long?

The first four to eight weeks involve noticeable shedding—this is normal for hand-knotted wool and not a defect. Vacuum frequently during this period and don't stress. After that initial phase, shedding drops dramatically. The fibers that come loose early are simply loose fibers from the knotting process being released, not a sign the rug is falling apart.

Does the price ever drop, or is September a good time to buy?

Nanimarquina pricing stays relatively consistent year-round because it's a premium product with genuine hand-knotted construction. September is actually strategic because it precedes holiday décor season and fall refresh projects. Buying now means your rug is settled and looking its best by November and December when you're entertaining.

How do I clean spills on a wool rug like this?

Blot immediately with a clean, dry cloth—don't rub, which works the stain deeper. For liquid spills, blot until no more moisture comes up. For solid messes, let them dry completely, then vacuum. The cream and black pattern means most everyday spills (coffee, juice, dirt) blend in visually even before you clean them. Professional cleaning is recommended annually for high-traffic rugs, which is far less expensive than replacing it.
