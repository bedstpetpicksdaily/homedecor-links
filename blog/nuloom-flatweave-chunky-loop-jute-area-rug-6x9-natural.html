Frequently Asked Questions

Will this rug work on hardwood floors or tile?

Absolutely, and it's particularly effective on hard surfaces where you want a visual anchor. The flatweave design means it won't shift as easily as high-pile rugs. I'd recommend a thin non-slip rug pad underneath—it protects both the rug and your flooring from friction. On hardwood specifically, a pad also prevents moisture from settling underneath.

How do I clean spills without damaging jute?

Speed matters. Blot immediately with a clean cloth—don't rub, which can push liquid deeper into fibers. For water-based spills like coffee or tea, plain water and blotting typically works. For greasy spills, a tiny bit of mild dish soap mixed with water, applied sparingly, followed by water-only blotting. Let it air dry completely. The flatweave actually makes cleanup easier than high-pile because liquids don't get trapped in dense pile.

Is this rug suitable for high-traffic areas like hallways or kitchens?

It's tough, but not ideal for the heaviest traffic zones. The 6x9 works well in living rooms or bedrooms with moderate foot traffic. For kitchen entries or high-traffic hallways, jute will show wear faster and can shed more noticeably. If your home is open-concept with kitchen traffic flowing across living areas, place it strategically away from the heaviest pathways, or consider it secondary to a more durable synthetic in primary traffic zones.
