Frequently Asked Questions

Will the embroidery hold up after multiple washes across all these options?

Yes, all four options feature hand-finished embroidery (not printing), which should hold up to regular use and actually look richer over time. However, delicate embroidery requires careful washing—hand washing or gentle machine cycles are recommended to prevent thread unraveling. The Suzani at the budget tier has the most risk of fraying given the price point, but 57 reviewers report minimal thread issues at the 4.4-star rating.

Which size should I choose if I already have standard pillow inserts?

Both the Indigo Throw (18x18) and Suzani (18x18) fit standard throw pillows without requiring custom inserts. The Lumbar (12x) is designed for lumbar support pillows specifically. The Colca (20x20) is slightly oversized and may require 20x20 inserts, though it can work with slightly undersized fills for a casual drape.

How do these Anthropologie options compare to West Elm or Etsy boho pillows at similar price points?

Anthropologie's hand-finished embroidery is the differentiator—most $15-20 boho alternatives use flat-printed designs that fade within a season. Even the $10 Suzani outperforms cheaper mass-market options in thread durability. West Elm competitors at the $40-60 range may offer similar quality, but Anthropologie's mid-tier options (Indigo, Lumbar at ~$35-45) deliver comparable craftsmanship at slightly lower price points. Etsy artisanal pillows vary wildly in quality; Anthropologie's consistent 4.3-4.4 ratings suggest more reliable quality control.

Are these pillow covers colorfast, or will they fade after a few washes?

All four options emphasize durable color vibrancy through quality fabric construction. The indigo and sage colors are specifically selected for their ability to hide dust and wear, suggesting they're dyed to last rather than surface-printed. The Suzani's 4.4-star rating with no color-fading complaints from 57 reviewers is a positive indicator even at the budget tier. Careful washing (avoid high heat, harsh detergents) will maximize longevity.

Which option is best for pet owners or households with heavy use?

The Indigo 18x18 Throw is the most practical for high-traffic homes—deep indigo hides pet hair and staining better than sage or lighter patterns, and the 18x18 size is replaceable without major investment. The Suzani ($10 replacement cost) is also practical for trial-and-error decorating in active households. Avoid the Colca and premium Lumbar in heavily-used pet environments; their delicate embroidery and higher replacement cost make them better suited to curated spaces with lower daily wear.
