How much does the Loloi AmAda 9x12 actually cost?

Check affiliate listings for current pricing and compare across retailers—price drops do happen, especially on neutral colors like sage.

Will this rug shed everywhere during the first year?

No—and this is where hand-knotted wool wins against machine-made synthetics. Some initial shedding happens with any new wool rug, typically within the first few weeks, but it's minimal and manageable with regular vacuuming. By month two, shedding drops dramatically. Synthetic rugs, by comparison, shed consistently for months or shed permanently throughout their life.

Can I use this rug with pets, or will it get destroyed?

Wool actually handles pet claws and accidents better than most people expect. The fiber is naturally resilient and resistant to staining when treated quickly. Customers with dogs and cats consistently report that spot-cleaning works well on wool. The real issue isn't destruction—it's commitment to vacuuming regularly (2-3 times weekly in high-traffic areas) and addressing accidents immediately rather than letting them set in.
