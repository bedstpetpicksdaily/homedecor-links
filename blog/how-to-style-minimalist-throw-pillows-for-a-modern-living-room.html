    Frequently Asked Questions

    What size throw pillows should I buy for a modern minimalist living room?

    For a minimalist aesthetic, choose pillows in 16x16" or 18x18" sizes, as these proportions look clean and intentional without overwhelming your furniture. Stick to one or two sizes per sofa to maintain visual simplicity and avoid a cluttered appearance.

    How many throw pillows should I put on a minimalist sofa?

    Two to four pillows is ideal for a minimalist living room—typically one on each end of the sofa, or two symmetrically placed in the center. Avoid mixing more than two different pillow designs to maintain the clean, uncluttered look that defines minimalism.

    What colors and fabrics work best for minimalist throw pillows?

    Opt for neutral tones like white, cream, gray, black, or soft beige in natural fabrics such as linen, cotton, or wool for an authentic minimalist feel. You can add one accent color in a complementary shade, but keep patterns minimal—solid colors or subtle geometric designs work best.

    Should minimalist throw pillows match my curtains or other decor?

    Minimalist pillows don't need to match other elements but should complement your overall color palette and coordinate with 1-2 dominant colors in the room. Aim for cohesion rather than matching—pillows in the same color family as your walls, rug, or furniture create a harmonious, intentional look.
