What to Look For

  • Solution-Dyed Outdoor-Grade Fabric: Look for pillows made with solution-dyed acrylic or polyester fabrics specifically designed to resist water and stains. These materials have color woven throughout the fiber rather than applied to the surface, meaning water beads right off and stains don't penetrate—they're the same fabrics used in high-end outdoor furniture.
  • Sealed Seams and Quality Construction: Waterproof pillows need reinforced, tightly sealed seams to prevent water from seeping into the filling. Check that pillows have double-stitched seams and quality zippers with protective flaps, ensuring water can't find its way inside even during direct contact with liquids.
  • Removable, Washable Covers: The best waterproof throw pillows feature removable covers that can be machine washed or hosed down for easy cleaning. This allows you to refresh your pillows quickly while keeping the inner core protected, and it makes seasonal style updates simple without replacing the entire pillow.

What to Avoid

  • Avoid "Water-Resistant" Claims Without Fabric Specifications: Not all water-resistant pillows are created equal. Some use cheap synthetic coatings that wear off after a few washes or break down in sunlight. Skip pillows that don't specify their fabric type or construction—you need to know exactly what you're buying, whether it's solution-dyed acrylic, performance polyester, or another proven material.
  • Avoid Non-Removable Covers: If a pillow's cover cannot be removed and washed, you're limited to surface cleaning only. Dirt and bacteria can accumulate in the seams and filling over time, and you won't be able to refresh the pillow or change its style seasonally without buying entirely new pillows.
