Frequently Asked Questions

Will these curtains shrink after washing?

Yes, expect 2-3% shrinkage on the length after the first cold wash. This translates to roughly 2 inches lost on an 84-inch panel. Wash in cold water and dry on low heat to minimize shrinkage. Many owners successfully hand-wash or use a delicate cycle to reduce loss further. Measure your windows before ordering if you're working with tight clearance.

How much light do these block?

Minimal. These are light-filtering panels, not blackout curtains. Morning sunlight passes through noticeably, and they won't darken a room significantly. They work best paired with cellular shades, roller shades, or thicker lining panels if you need actual light control. Use them as decorative layering over functional window treatments rather than expecting them to solve light problems alone.

What's the actual price and is it worth it?

Pricing varies between $25-45 per single 84-inch panel depending on current Amazon inventory. At $25-30, the value is strong given the Studio McGee design credibility and substantial fabric weight. At $40-45, similar-quality cotton panels from other brands become competitive. Set a price alert if shopping on Amazon to catch better pricing. Most reviews mention satisfaction in the $28-35 range specifically.

Can these be machine washed?

Yes, but wash cold on delicate or gentle cycle and dry on low heat to preserve the fabric integrity and minimize shrinkage. Many owners recommend line-drying to extend the life. Dry cleaning is unnecessary unless you spill something requiring professional treatment. These are designed for regular home laundering, though the textured finish may show dust more readily than smooth fabrics between washes.

Do I need two panels per window or will one work?

One 84-inch panel works as a single drape, but most rooms benefit from paired panels for a fuller, more intentional look. Single panels work best for small windows or as layering behind sheer curtains. Standard window treatment design uses two panels that can be drawn across the window. Budget accordingly if you need to cover multiple windows.
