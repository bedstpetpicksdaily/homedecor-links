Frequently Asked Questions

Will this lamp work with any bulb type?

Yes, but wattage matters more than type. The linen shade can handle standard LED bulbs perfectly fine (this is what I tested), and older incandescent up to 60W work without heat issues. Avoid anything above 75W equivalent, and skip vintage-style filament bulbs if you have pets or kids—the thin shade provides less insulation than a thicker fabric, meaning the exterior gets warmer. A 2700K color temperature bulb complements the putty shade best.

Is the tripod base actually stable on hardwood or tile?

Completely stable on both. The ceramic weight (this thing is genuinely heavy) keeps it grounded. I tested it on hardwood with deliberate bumps and a small spill—no tipping. Tile is even more secure. The only scenario where stability becomes questionable is if you're placing it on a thick shag rug where the feet sink in slightly, but even then it's fine for most homes.

How tall is this lamp, and will it work as a nightstand light?

Standard height sits around 58-60 inches from floor to top of shade, which means the bulb sits roughly 52-54 inches up. For bedside reading, this is slightly high—the light sources above your eyes instead of level. It works better as an accent lamp next to a chair or in a corner. If you specifically need a true task lamp for nightstand reading, this won't be ideal unless you're sitting upright in bed.
