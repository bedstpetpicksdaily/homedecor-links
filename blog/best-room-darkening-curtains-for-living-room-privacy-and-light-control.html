What to Look For

  • Blackout or Room Darkening Fabric Weight: Look for curtains with a minimum of 200 GSM (grams per square meter) fabric density. True blackout curtains block 95-99% of light, while room darkening options block 80-95%, which is often the sweet spot for living rooms where you still want some control over natural light without making the space feel like a cave during daytime hours.
  • Thermal Insulation Properties: The best room darkening curtains have multiple layers that not only block light but also provide insulation to regulate room temperature. This means lower energy bills and a more comfortable living space year-round, plus the added benefit of sound dampening from busy neighborhoods.
  • Lining Quality and Durability: Check that the curtains have a quality backing or lining, preferably acrylic or polyester-based, that prevents light from seeping through the weave. Reinforced seams and weighted hems ensure the curtains hang properly and maintain their privacy-blocking capability for years without sagging or deteriorating.
