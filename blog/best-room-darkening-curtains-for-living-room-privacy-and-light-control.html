What to Look For

  • Blackout or Room Darkening Fabric Weight: Look for curtains with a minimum of 200 GSM (grams per square meter) fabric density. True blackout curtains block 95-99% of light, while room darkening options block 80-95%, which is often the sweet spot for living rooms where you still want some control over natural light without making the space feel like a cave during daytime hours.
  • Thermal Insulation Properties: The best room darkening curtains have multiple layers that not only block light but also provide insulation to regulate room temperature. This means lower energy bills and a more comfortable living space year-round, plus the added benefit of sound dampening from busy neighborhoods.
  • Lining Quality and Durability: Check that the curtains have a quality backing or lining, preferably acrylic or polyester-based, that prevents light from seeping through the weave. Reinforced seams and weighted hems ensure the curtains hang properly and maintain their privacy-blocking capability for years without sagging or deteriorating.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What's the difference between blackout curtains and room darkening curtains?

    Blackout curtains block 99-100% of light and are typically thicker with multiple layers, while room darkening curtains block 80-99% of light and are lighter weight, making them more versatile for living rooms. Room darkening curtains are better if you want to maintain some ambient light during the day while still having privacy, whereas blackout curtains are ideal for complete darkness and maximum insulation.

    What fabric weight should I look for in room darkening curtains for a living room?

    Look for curtains with a fabric weight of at least 6-8 ounces per linear yard for effective light blocking and privacy without looking too heavy or institutional. Heavier fabrics (8+ oz) provide better light control and sound dampening, but make sure your curtain rod can support the weight and that the fabric drapes naturally in your space.

    Do room darkening curtains help with energy efficiency?

    Yes, room darkening curtains can reduce heat loss in winter and block solar heat gain in summer, potentially lowering your heating and cooling costs by 10-25%. The thicker fabric acts as an insulating barrier, so closing them during extreme temperatures can make a noticeable difference in your energy bills.

    What should I measure before buying room darkening curtains for my living room?

    Measure the width of your window or wall (multiply by 1.5-2 for proper fullness) and measure from the top of your curtain rod to where you want the curtains to end—typically just touching the floor or hovering 1/2 inch above. For best results, mount your curtain rod 4-6 inches above the window frame and extend it 8-10 inches beyond each side to maximize light blocking.
