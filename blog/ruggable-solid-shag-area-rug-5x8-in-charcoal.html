Frequently Asked Questions

How often does this rug actually need washing?

Real answer: depends on your household. In a home with pets or high foot traffic, you're looking at deep washing every 6-8 weeks, with spot cleaning in between. In quieter homes, every 3-4 months works fine. The beauty is that mild staining doesn't force a full wash—you can spot treat and move on. The machine washable feature means you're not locked into professional cleaning schedules.

Will the charcoal color fade or look dull after washing?

Charcoal holds up remarkably well through multiple washes if you follow care instructions—cold water, gentle cycle, air drying. Some owners report slight initial color release in the first wash (normal for dyed fibers), but it stabilizes after that. The color may lighten barely over years of regular washing, but it's not like going from deep charcoal to gray. Most reviews don't cite fading as a major complaint.

Is this suitable for high-traffic areas like entryways?

Partially. Shag in general isn't ideal for high-traffic zones because foot traffic compresses the pile and makes vacuuming harder. However, the Ruggable's washability actually makes it a better choice than traditional shag in foyers. You can refresh it more easily. Just manage expectations—after a year in a heavily trafficked entryway, you'll notice pile wear. It's better suited to living rooms, bedrooms, or media spaces where traffic is moderate and intentional.

How does this compare to non-washable shag rugs at similar price points?

The washable technology is the premium feature here. You're paying extra for convenience, and the math works in your favor if you own pets, have young kids, or live in an active household. Non-washable shags at the same price are often lower quality fiber with no offsetting benefit—you get neither durability nor ease of care. Ruggable's trade-off is worth making.
