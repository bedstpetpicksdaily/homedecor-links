Understanding Your Home's Humidity Needs

Before purchasing a dehumidifier, assess your home's specific moisture issues. Basements and crawl spaces are particularly prone to excess humidity due to poor ventilation and ground moisture. The ideal indoor humidity level ranges from 30-50%. If you notice condensation on windows, musty odors, or visible mold growth, you likely need a dehumidifier. Argendon's product line includes specialized units designed specifically for these problem areas, ensuring you get the right solution for your space.

Key Features to Look for in 2026 Models

Capacity is crucial—measured in pints per day, this indicates how much moisture the unit can remove. For basements and larger spaces, 50+ pint models are ideal. Frequently Asked Questions

What size dehumidifier do I need for my basement?

The size depends on your basement's square footage and current humidity levels. For spaces up to 500 sq ft, a 30-50 pint dehumidifier works well. Larger basements or those with significant moisture issues may require 70+ pint units. Argendon offers sizing guides to help you select the right capacity.

Can dehumidifiers work in crawl spaces?

Yes, specialized crawl space dehumidifiers are designed specifically for these challenging environments. Argendon's crawl space units feature compact designs that fit tight spaces while delivering powerful humidity control to prevent mold and wood rot.

How often should I drain or empty my dehumidifier?

Depending on humidity levels and unit capacity, you may need to empty the tank daily or weekly. Most modern dehumidifiers, including Argendon models, offer continuous drain options that connect to a floor drain or window, eliminating the need for manual emptying.

Are Argendon dehumidifiers energy efficient?

Argendon designs their residential dehumidifiers with energy efficiency in mind. Many models include automatic humidity sensors that activate the unit only when needed, helping reduce electricity consumption compared to older models.

What's the ideal indoor humidity level?

The EPA recommends keeping indoor humidity between 30-50%. This range prevents mold growth while maintaining comfort. Most modern dehumidifiers allow you to set your target humidity level automatically.
