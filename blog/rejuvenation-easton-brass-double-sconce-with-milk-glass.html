Pros

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Easton compare to other Rejuvenation sconces in the same price range?

The Easton is their entry point for double sconces, making it more versatile for most bathrooms than their single-fixture options. Stepping up to their premium lines adds design complexity and costs $100-150 more, but the core material quality stays consistent. For most people, the Easton delivers the Rejuvenation aesthetic without the premium markup.

Will the brass tarnish, and is that actually desirable?

Yes, the brass will develop patina over 6-12 months depending on humidity (bathrooms accelerate this). This is literally the design intention—aged brass looks intentional and vintage. If you want to prevent patina, you'd need to treat it monthly with brass cleaner, which defeats the purpose. Accept the aging or choose a different material.

Can you use modern LED bulbs with this, or does it require specific vintage-style bulbs?

Standard LED bulbs work fine, but the fixture design really shines with warm-toned LED bulbs (2700K color temperature). This is where the milk glass quality matters—cheap, harsh 5000K LEDs will overwhelm the diffusion. Invest in quality warm LEDs and you'll get that authentic 1940s ambiance without the energy waste of actual vintage bulbs.

Is the 4.3-star rating reliable, or is it padded with incentivized reviews?

With 500+ reviews, the rating is statistically meaningful. The negative reviews largely cluster around shipping damage and installation confusion rather than design or quality failure. That's a good sign—customer complaints are about logistics, not the product itself. The core material and functionality genuinely work.
