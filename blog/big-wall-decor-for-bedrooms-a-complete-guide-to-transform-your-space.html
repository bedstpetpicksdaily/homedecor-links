Sizing and Placement Tips for Maximum Impact

One of the most important considerations when choosing big wall decor is getting the size right. A common mistake homeowners make is selecting artwork that's too small for the wall space, which can make a room feel disconnected and cluttered. For bedrooms, aim for oversized canvas prints that cover at least 50-75% of your wall space above the bed or on a prominent wall. Big Wall Decor offers various sizes of their statement wall pieces, making it easy to find dimensions that perfectly suit your bedroom layout. Consider the wall dimensions, furniture placement, and room proportions when making your selection. Mounting your artwork at eye level (typically 57-60 inches from the floor to the center of the piece) ensures optimal visual impact and creates a balanced, professional appearance.

Frequently Asked Questions

What size should my big wall decor be for a bedroom?

For most bedrooms, oversized wall art should cover 50-75% of the wall space above your bed or on your focal wall. Measure your wall carefully and choose canvas prints or statement pieces that are proportional to your room size. Big Wall Decor offers various dimensions to help you find the perfect fit.

How do I hang large wall decor safely?

Use appropriate wall anchors or studs depending on your wall type and artwork weight. For canvas prints, locate studs in your wall and use heavy-duty hardware. Big Wall Decor pieces come with hanging instructions, and professional installation is recommended for larger, heavier framed art pieces.

Can abstract art work in any bedroom style?

Yes! Abstract art is incredibly versatile and can complement modern, contemporary, minimalist, and even traditional bedrooms. The key is choosing colors and compositions that coordinate with your existing palette and décor. Big Wall Decor's abstract collection offers options for every aesthetic preference.

What's the difference between canvas prints and framed art from Big Wall Decor?

Canvas prints offer a modern, gallery-like appearance and are typically more affordable, while framed art provides a classic, finished look with added protection. Both are excellent choices for bedroom wall decor—select based on your style preference and existing bedroom design.
