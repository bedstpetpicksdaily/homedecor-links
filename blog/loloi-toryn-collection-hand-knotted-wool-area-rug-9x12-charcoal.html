Frequently Asked Questions

How much does this rug actually shed, and for how long?

Expect noticeable shedding for the first 3-6 months—this is normal for hand-knotted wool and doesn't indicate defects. You'll notice lint on dark furniture and need to vacuum 2-3 times weekly during break-in. After six months, shedding drops dramatically. Use a high-quality vacuum with a HEPA filter and avoid dark upholstered pieces directly on the rug initially if shedding bothers you visually.

Will the charcoal color fade in natural sunlight?

All dark wool fades eventually under direct sun exposure—it's physics, not poor manufacturing. The Toryn's charcoal holds up better than lighter neutrals because the fading is less visible. If your space has all-day south-facing windows, expect noticeable color shift after 2-3 years. Using sheer curtains or UV-protective window film extends color life significantly.

Is this rug worth the price compared to lower-cost wool alternatives?

Hand-knotted construction means each knot is individually tied, creating superior density and longevity compared to hand-tufted or machine-made wool rugs at lower price points. You're paying roughly $15-25 per square foot depending on current pricing—reasonable for genuine hand-knotted wool. Budget synthetic alternatives ($8-12/sq ft) look fine initially but flatten noticeably after 2-3 years in high-traffic areas. The Toryn is a 7-10 year investment; budget rugs are 3-4 year replacements.

How should I clean and maintain this rug long-term?

Vacuum weekly with a beater-bar vacuum on medium setting—don't use suction-only vacuums on wool, as they don't agitate debris from the pile effectively. Spot-clean spills immediately with cool water and mild wool-safe detergent (test on a hidden corner first). Professional cleaning every 18-24 months extends the rug's life significantly. Avoid direct sunlight with window treatments and rotate the rug 180 degrees annually to even out wear patterns.
