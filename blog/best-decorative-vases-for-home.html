Frequently Asked Questions

What size decorative vase should I use for a living room console table?

For a console table, choose a vase between 12-18 inches tall so it doesn't obstruct sightlines or overwhelm the space. A medium-sized vase works best with other décor items, leaving room for candles or books on either side.

Which decorative vase materials are best for high-traffic areas?

Ceramic and glass vases are most durable for high-traffic spaces since they're easy to clean and resist chipping better than delicate options. Avoid thin porcelain or vases with intricate handles in homes with kids or pets.

How do I choose a decorative vase color that matches my home décor?

Pull your vase color from existing elements like throw pillows, artwork, or wall paint—this creates cohesion without matching exactly. Neutral tones like white, gray, and terracotta work with any style, while bold colors should complement your room's primary accent color.

Should decorative vases be filled with flowers or left empty?

Empty vases showcase the shape and texture of the piece itself, making them better for modern or minimalist décor, while filled vases add color and softness to traditional spaces. Your choice depends on your design style and whether you want low-maintenance décor.
