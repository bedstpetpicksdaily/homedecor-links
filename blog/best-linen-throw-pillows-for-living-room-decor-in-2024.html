What to Look For

  • Linen Weight and Weave Quality: Look for medium to heavyweight linen (at least 150 GSM) that feels substantial without being stiff. A tighter weave ensures better durability and resistance to pilling, which is crucial since throw pillows experience frequent use and handling in living rooms.
  • Color and Pattern Versatility: Choose neutral base colors like cream, gray, or soft taupe that complement existing wall art, rugs, and curtains. Consider subtle textures or tonal patterns that add visual interest without clashing with other décor elements in your space.
  • Zipper Quality and Care Instructions: Ensure pillows feature hidden zippers for a clean aesthetic, and verify that the linen is pre-shrunk and care-friendly. Machine-washable linen pillows save time and maintain their appearance through regular living room use.
