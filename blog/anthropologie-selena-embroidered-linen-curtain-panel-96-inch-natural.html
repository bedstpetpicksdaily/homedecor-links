Frequently Asked Questions

Do these curtains work in humid climates or bathrooms?

No. Pure linen in constant moisture environments will develop mildew and mold faster than blended fabrics. These are designed for living rooms, bedrooms, and dry spaces. If you need window coverage in bathrooms or kitchens, look for linen-cotton blends with moisture-resistant treatments instead.

Can you machine wash these, or do they require dry cleaning?

Anthropologie recommends professional dry cleaning to preserve the embroidery and prevent shrinkage, though some owners report gentle hand-washing works if you're willing to accept the fabric will shrink slightly (expect 3-5% length loss). The embroidered details are the wild card—dry cleaning is genuinely the safest option if those stitches matter to you.

What's the difference between these and cheaper embroidered linen panels on Amazon?

Fabric weight and embroidery density. The Selena uses higher-thread-count linen (typically 200+ GSM) with denser embroidery work, meaning it drapes better and the design details are more substantial. Cheap alternatives often feel papery by comparison and the embroidery looks thin and sparse. Feel the samples side-by-side before dismissing price differences.

Will one 96-inch panel be enough width, or do I need multiples?

Depends on your window width and desired fullness. A single panel covers roughly 50 inches of window width (when hung with standard pinch pleats). Windows wider than that need two panels. For a finished look rather than a minimalist single-panel approach, most designers recommend doubling up on panels for visual proportion and light-blocking capability.

How does this compare to West Elm or Parachute Home curtains at similar price points?

West Elm tends toward more contemporary prints and patterns; Parachute focuses on minimalist solids. The Selena splits the difference with embroidered detail that feels artisanal without being loud. If you want maximalist color and pattern, West Elm wins. If you want zero-detail neutrals, Parachute is more affordable. The Selena wins if you want character plus versatility.
