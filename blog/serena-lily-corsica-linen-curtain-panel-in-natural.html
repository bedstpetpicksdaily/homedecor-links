Frequently Asked Questions

Do these curtains block light completely or just filter it?

They filter rather than blackout. In my south-facing bedroom, morning light still entered the room but without harsh glare—exactly what I wanted. If you need complete darkness for a bedroom, you'd need to layer these with a blackout liner or pair them with cellular shades. The natural linen weave simply doesn't block light entirely, which many people actually prefer for living spaces.

How much do they shrink after washing?

Linen shrinks by design, typically 3-5% with the first wash. Serena & Lily accounts for this in sizing, but I still recommend hand-washing in cold water or using a gentle cycle if you want to minimize shrinkage. I spot-cleaned mine instead of full washing to avoid any unexpected dimension changes. Check their care tag for specific guidance—they're usually more detailed than standard curtain instructions.

Are these panels wide enough for standard windows?

That depends on your window width and desired fullness. These come in various widths, but the standard approach is to buy panels that together are 1.5x to 2x your window width for proper draping. A 36-inch window typically needs two 50-inch or larger panels. Measure carefully before ordering—one panel won't adequately cover a standard window with the beautiful folds these panels deserve.

Will the natural color fade in direct sun?

Linen naturally fades slightly over time with UV exposure—it's part of the material's character. My panels have been in a south-facing window for four months, and the color has softened imperceptibly. If you're sensitive to color changes, this might matter, but most people find the subtle fading actually improves the look, adding depth to the neutral tone.
