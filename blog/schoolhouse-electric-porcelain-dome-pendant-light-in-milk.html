What size space does this pendant work best in, and do I need multiple fixtures?

A single pendant suits tight spaces like entryways, bathrooms, or over small side tables. Over a kitchen island (36–48 inches wide), plan for two to three pendants spaced 24–30 inches apart for balanced lighting and visual impact. The dome shape concentrates light downward effectively, so you won't need as many fixtures as you might with open-frame designs—a real budget win if you're lighting multiple areas.

Is the milk finish durable, or will it stain and show dust easily?

Porcelain is naturally stain-resistant and wipes clean with a dry cloth; dust clings more noticeably to matte finishes than glossy ones, so expect monthly light dusting. The milk white specifically won't yellow over time like cream finishes sometimes do. If you live in a high-dust environment (near a fireplace, woodworking shop, or heavy traffic area), consider the finish commitment before buying—it's maintainable but not maintenance-free.
