Frequently Asked Questions

Can you really just throw the whole rug in a standard washing machine?

No—only the cover goes in the washer. The base stays put and doesn't get wet. It takes about 30 seconds to unsnap the cover, toss it in cold water on a gentle cycle, and reattach it when dry. This is the actual convenience factor that makes it practical, not the oversimplification the marketing sometimes suggests.

How often should you wash it if you have pets or kids?

Most owners wash every 4-6 weeks with regular use and occasional spills. The beauty is you can wash it whenever you want without guilt or scheduling around professionals. If there's a major accident, you can wash it immediately without damage—try that with a traditional rug.

Does the shag texture stay fluffy after multiple washes, or does it go flat?

It holds up better than standard shag rugs after washing. The fibers don't fully flatten, though like any plush rug, you'll get some compression in high-traffic areas over time. Air-drying (not machine drying) and occasionally brushing the pile with a soft-bristled brush helps maintain the texture longer.
