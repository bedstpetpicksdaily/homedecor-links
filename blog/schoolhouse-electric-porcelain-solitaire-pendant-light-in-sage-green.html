Frequently Asked Questions

Does this pendant work in small kitchens or just over large islands?

I've tested it over both a 36-inch kitchen island and above a 24-inch bar area in a narrow apartment layout. The 7-inch diameter bowl doesn't overwhelm smaller spaces, and it actually looks proportionate rather than cramped. The real consideration is ceiling height—it needs at least 7.5 feet clear from the bottom of the fixture to work comfortably in rooms where people spend time. If your ceiling is lower, this light will feel heavy overhead.

Is sage green going to look dated by 2027?

Sage green occupies an interesting middle ground—it's been consistent in design for years without being trendy in the way millennial pink or navy blue fixtures were. That said, it's definitely a color rather than neutral. This matters less if you're the type to refresh fixtures intentionally every five years, and more if you want something permanently timeless. For me, the aesthetic longevity feels safer than investing in true trendiness, but it's still a considered choice rather than a safe one.

How bright is this really, and does it actually work as task lighting?

I've used it as the sole light source while cooking, working at my laptop, and reading recipes. It's bright enough for real tasks when paired with a decent-wattage bulb (I used an 800-lumen LED equivalent), but it's not going to replace undercabinet lighting in a professional kitchen. The opal diffuser does prevent harsh shadows, so you're not squinting or creating glare. It's practical task lighting for residential kitchens and dining areas, not industrial-grade work lighting.
