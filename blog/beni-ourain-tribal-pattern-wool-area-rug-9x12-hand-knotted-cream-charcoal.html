Frequently Asked Questions

How does this compare to Ruggable or other washable 9x12 rugs?

Washable rugs like Ruggable typically start at $150-$300 for comparable sizes, but they sacrifice that authentic hand-knotted feel and weight. Washable rugs are thinner and more plastic-like underfoot. This Beni Ourain has real texture and density—the trade-off is you can't machine wash it. For most living spaces, spot-cleaning and occasional professional cleaning (annually) is perfectly adequate.

Will it shed excessively, and for how long?

Yes, it will shed—expect visible fibers for 2-3 months, especially with foot traffic. This is completely normal for hand-knotted wool and doesn't indicate defects. Vacuum 2-3 times weekly during the break-in period, then weekly maintenance handles it fine. The shedding actually decreases as the fibers settle, and by month four, it stabilizes significantly.

What's the actual thickness and weight compared to thin accent rugs?

Hand-knotted construction means this rug has real heft—you're looking at 1/4 to 3/8 inch pile depth, which feels substantial underfoot compared to flat-weave rugs. A 9x12 Beni Ourain typically weighs 40-60 pounds, so it stays in place without sliding, especially with a quality rug pad underneath (which costs $20-$40 separately).

Does the cream actually stay cream, or does it yellow/gray quickly?

Natural wool cream fibers do develop a subtle patina over time—this is desirable in design circles because it looks lived-in and authentic rather than factory-sterile. The charcoal pattern helps camouflage minor discoloration. If pristine cream is non-negotiable, this isn't your rug. But if you appreciate the character that comes with natural fibers, this aging is actually a feature.

How does the price at $10 factor into long-term value?

At $10, you're essentially risk-free. Even if this rug lasts only 3-5 years of moderate use before requiring replacement, you've paid roughly $2-$3 per year of ownership. Authentic Moroccan Beni Ourain rugs at specialist retailers can last 15-20 years but demand $1,000-plus investment. This price point is ideal for renters, people experimenting with room layouts, or anyone who wants authentic aesthetic without financial commitment.
