Frequently Asked Questions

How much space does the Blackspot Arc Pendant actually light up?

The arc extends approximately 12-14 inches from the wall mounting point, with the shade positioned at roughly 18-24 inches from the wall at its furthest reach. This creates a focused light pool ideal for countertop work or accent illumination over a side table or bed. It's not designed to light an entire room—it's a statement piece that also happens to be functional. In my kitchen, one Blackspot above the counter paired with overhead general lighting creates perfect task-and-ambient balance.

Will polished brass tarnish, and how do I maintain it?

Polished brass naturally develops a patina over 6-12 months, and frankly, this is part of its charm. The finish doesn't tarnish like uncoated brass; instead, it develops character. I dust mine monthly with a soft cloth, and I've intentionally avoided polishing compounds because the emerging patina makes it look more curated than brand-new hardware. If you want to restore the mirror shine, brass polish works beautifully—takes about five minutes.

Can the Blackspot Arc work in modern versus traditional spaces?

This is where Schoolhouse Electric's design genius shows. The Blackspot works equally well in mid-century modern interiors, industrial lofts, and even contemporary farmhouse spaces. The geometric simplicity of the arch transcends stylistic boundaries. I've tested it in three different room aesthetics during my review period, and it adapted seamlessly to each. The polished brass reads as both vintage-inspired and contemporary depending on surrounding decor—it's genuinely versatile despite being deeply distinctive.
