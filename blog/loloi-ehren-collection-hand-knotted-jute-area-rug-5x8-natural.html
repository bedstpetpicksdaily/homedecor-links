Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Loloi Ehren compare to budget jute rugs from big-box retailers?

The hand-knotted construction is the immediate difference. Budget rugs feel flat and machine-uniform; this one has visible texture and individual character. After three months, the Ehren shows minor wear in high-traffic zones while maintaining structural integrity. Cheaper alternatives would already be visibly matted and pilling. You're paying for longevity, not just initial appearance.

Will this rug work on top of hardwood floors?

Yes, with a caveat. Use a non-slip rug pad underneath—jute can slide on hardwood, especially with foot traffic or when you're moving furniture. The pad prevents slipping and protects your floors. Without it, you'll constantly adjust the rug, which is annoying in a 5x8 that anchors your entire seating area.

Is the initial shedding normal, and how long does it last?

Completely normal for hand-knotted natural jute. Expect noticeable shedding for 4-6 weeks as loose fibers work free during initial use. Vacuum twice weekly during this period. After week six, shedding drops dramatically. This is temporary and doesn't indicate poor quality—it's just how natural fiber rugs work. Budget your timeline accordingly if you're hosting events in July.
