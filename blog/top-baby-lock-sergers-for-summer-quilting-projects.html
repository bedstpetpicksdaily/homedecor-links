Frequently Asked Questions

What is a serger and why do quilters need one?

A serger is a specialized sewing machine that trims, wraps, and stitches fabric edges in one motion. Quilters use sergers to finish quilt edges professionally, prevent fraying, and add decorative finishes that enhance the overall appearance of their projects.

Can Baby Lock sergers handle thick quilting fabrics?

Yes, Baby Lock sergers are specifically designed to handle multiple fabric layers and batting used in quilting. Their powerful motors and adjustable tension settings accommodate various fabric weights and thicknesses with ease.

How do I maintain my Baby Lock serger during summer use?

Keep your Baby Lock serger clean by regularly removing lint and thread scraps, change needles frequently for optimal performance, and ensure the machine is stored in a cool, dry place. Follow Baby Lock's maintenance guidelines in your manual for best results.

Is it difficult to thread a Baby Lock serger?

Baby Lock sergers feature innovative threading systems that simplify the process compared to other brands. Many models include color-coded guides and easy-access thread paths, making threading straightforward even for beginners.

What's the difference between 3-thread and 4-thread serging?

3-thread serging creates a rolled hem perfect for decorative finishes, while 4-thread serging provides a stronger, more durable seam suitable for structural quilting applications. Baby Lock sergers offer flexibility to switch between these options.
