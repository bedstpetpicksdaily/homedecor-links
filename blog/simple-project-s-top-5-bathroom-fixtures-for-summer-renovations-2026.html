Frequently Asked Questions

What is a macerating toilet and why would I need one?

A macerating toilet, like those offered by Simple Project, grinds waste into fine particles for easier drainage. They're ideal for basement bathrooms, additions, and spaces where traditional plumbing installation is impractical or expensive. They allow flexible bathroom placement without major structural modifications.

Are Simple Project smart toilets water-efficient?

Yes, Simple Project's smart toilets feature advanced flushing technology that significantly reduces water consumption compared to standard toilets. Many models include dual-flush options and adjustable water pressure settings, helping homeowners save money while promoting environmental sustainability.

Can I install Simple Project bathroom fixtures myself or do I need a plumber?

While some fixtures like modern faucets may be DIY-friendly, most bathroom installations—especially smart toilets and macerating systems—require professional plumbing expertise. Simple Project recommends consulting licensed plumbers to ensure proper installation and warranty compliance.

What bathroom style do Simple Project fixtures work best with?

Simple Project offers fixtures for all design styles including modern, contemporary, transitional, luxury, and traditional bathrooms. Their diverse collection ensures you'll find options that complement your existing décor and personal aesthetic preferences.

How do I maintain Simple Project macerating toilet systems?

Macerating toilets require regular maintenance including periodic cleaning of the grinding mechanism and monitoring for clogs. Simple Project provides detailed maintenance guidelines with each unit, and professional servicing is recommended annually for optimal performance.
