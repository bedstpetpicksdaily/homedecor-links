Assess Your Wall Space and Room Dimensions

Before selecting your big wall decor, measure your wall space carefully and consider the room's proportions. A general rule is that your statement wall piece should take up about 50-75% of the wall space above your furniture. For living room wall art, consider the size of your sofa and seating arrangement. Big Wall Decor offers extensive sizing options in their oversized canvas prints collection, allowing you to find dimensions that perfectly fit your specific space. Don't underestimate the impact of scale—a larger piece often has more dramatic effect than multiple smaller artworks.

Choose Your Style: Abstract Art and Beyond

Summer is an excellent time to experiment with bold color palettes and contemporary designs. Abstract art from Big Wall Decor's collection offers versatility, working with both modern and transitional décor styles. Consider the existing color scheme of your room and whether you want your wall decor to complement or contrast with it. Abstract pieces in cool blues, vibrant greens, and warm sunset tones are particularly popular for summer refreshes. Their framed art options also provide a more traditional look if you prefer statement pieces with added dimension and sophistication.

Consider Lighting and Wall Placement

Natural and artificial lighting dramatically affects how your big wall decor appears in your space. Position large wall art where it receives adequate light to showcase the colors and details. Avoid placing statement wall pieces directly opposite windows where glare might diminish their visual impact. Big Wall Decor's bedroom wall decor collection and living room options are designed to look stunning in various lighting conditions, from morning sunlight to evening ambient lighting. Consider the wall's orientation and test how different times of day affect your chosen piece before making a final decision.

Match Your Personal Style and Home Aesthetic

Your big wall decor should reflect your personality while complementing your home's overall design. Are you drawn to bold, vibrant abstract art or prefer subtle, neutral tones? Do you favor modern minimalism or eclectic maximalism? Big Wall Decor's diverse collection ensures you'll find oversized canvas prints and statement wall pieces that align with your vision. Don't be afraid to choose pieces that speak to you emotionally—the best wall decor is what makes you happy when you look at it daily.

Frequently Asked Questions

What size big wall decor should I choose for my living room?

For most living rooms, choose oversized canvas prints that occupy 50-75% of wall space above your sofa. Measure your wall width and aim for art that's about three-quarters that measurement. Big Wall Decor offers multiple sizing options to match your specific dimensions.

Does abstract art work in all room styles?

Yes, abstract art is incredibly versatile and can complement modern, contemporary, transitional, and eclectic décor styles. The key is selecting colors that coordinate with your existing palette. Big Wall Decor's abstract collection includes pieces in various color ranges for any aesthetic.

How do I hang large wall art properly?

Use appropriate wall anchors for your wall type, find studs when possible, and use a level to ensure straight placement. For heavier oversized canvas prints, consider professional installation. Big Wall Decor's framed art and canvas options come with hanging hardware and installation guidance.

Can I use big wall decor in bedrooms?

Absolutely! Bedroom wall decor benefits greatly from oversized pieces. Choose calming colors and styles that promote relaxation. Big Wall Decor's bedroom collection features serene abstract art and soothing designs perfect for creating a peaceful retreat.

What's the difference between canvas prints and framed art?

Canvas prints offer a contemporary, gallery-like appearance without frames, while framed art adds dimensional sophistication and complements traditional décor. Both from Big Wall Decor serve as excellent statement wall pieces depending on your style preference.
