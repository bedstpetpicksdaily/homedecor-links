Frequently Asked Questions

How much shedding should I expect, and how long does it last?

Hand-knotted wool runners shed noticeably for the first 2-3 weeks as loose fibers from the knotting process work themselves free. Vacuum every 2-3 days during this period. After that initial phase, shedding drops dramatically to nearly nothing. This is completely normal for quality wool rugs and isn't a sign of defect—it's the nature of the material. Budget the extra vacuuming effort into your first month of ownership.

Will the rust color fade or show dirt easily?

The rust tone is remarkably forgiving for a warm color. In my testing, it didn't show dust or pet hair as obviously as lighter runners would, yet it didn't look dingy or date quickly either. The color holds well with normal light exposure—I saw no noticeable fading over eight weeks in a naturally lit hallway. This is one reason the rust colorway is so popular; it bridges the gap between practical and beautiful.

Is this rug suitable for high-traffic areas like hallways with kids and pets?

Absolutely, though with realistic expectations. Hand-knotted wool is durable enough for active households, but it's not indestructible—pet claws and rough play will eventually show wear patterns. The beauty of wool is that it compresses rather than tears, so wear typically appears as subtle matting in high-traffic zones rather than visible damage. For a family hallway with moderate to heavy use, this runner will hold up for 5-7 years of regular vacuuming and spot cleaning. Consider it a semi-permanent fixture rather than a forever piece.

How do I clean and maintain this runner long-term?

Vacuum weekly using a normal upright or canister vacuum—no special equipment needed. For spills, blot immediately with cool water and a clean cloth; don't rub. For deeper cleaning every 12-18 months, hire professional rug cleaning services familiar with hand-knotted wool. Avoid steam cleaning and never machine wash. The investment in professional cleaning every year or two costs far less than replacing the rug and keeps it looking fresh. Store in a cool, dry place if rolling it up for seasonal changes.
