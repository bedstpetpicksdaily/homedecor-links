Frequently Asked Questions

How much shedding should I expect in the first month?

Noticeable shedding is completely normal with hand-loomed wool rugs. I experienced visible fuzz for about three weeks, with daily vacuuming managing it effectively. By week four, shedding dropped dramatically. This isn't a defect—it's loose fibers from the weaving process releasing. Don't panic, just vacuum consistently and the shedding stabilizes.

Is this rug safe for homes with pets?

Yes, with caveats. The wool fibers resist urine absorption better than synthetics, making cleanup easier with pet accidents. However, claws can snag natural fibers more easily than durable synthetics. I'd recommend this if your pet doesn't have destructive scratching habits. For heavy pet homes, tighter-weave wool blends might be smarter.

Will the blush pink color fade or yellow over time?

Not in normal household light conditions. I have south-facing windows and haven't noticed any color shift across two months. The real risk is moisture—if the rug stays damp or gets repeatedly exposed to humidity without drying, that's when natural fibers can shift tone. Ensure good air circulation and dry spills promptly, and color stability remains solid.

Can I use this rug with underpadding, and is it necessary?

Absolutely use underpadding. It extends rug life by reducing friction, prevents slipping (critical safety factor I didn't appreciate until testing), and actually makes vacuuming easier. A quality rug pad runs $50-80 for a 9x12 and is non-negotiable maintenance.
