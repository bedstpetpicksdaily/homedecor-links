Frequently Asked Questions

How much water do smart toilets actually save?

Smart toilets typically use 1.28 gallons per flush or less, compared to 3.5-5 gallons in older models. This represents a 67% reduction in water usage, translating to approximately 13,000 gallons saved annually for an average household.

Are Simple Project smart toilets difficult to install?

Most Simple Project smart toilet models are designed as direct replacements for standard fixtures, making installation straightforward for most homeowners. Their macerating toilet options provide additional flexibility for challenging spaces like basements.

What makes macerating toilets different from regular smart toilets?

Macerating toilets grind waste before pumping it upward, allowing installation in basements or areas without traditional gravity-fed drainage. Simple Project offers macerating models that combine this functionality with water-saving smart features.

Will a smart toilet increase my home's resale value?

Yes, bathroom upgrades with modern fixtures typically increase home value. Energy and water-efficient upgrades appeal to eco-conscious buyers and demonstrate responsible home maintenance, making them valuable selling points.

Do I need an electrician to install a smart toilet?

Smart toilets require access to an electrical outlet for bidet features and sensors. While basic installation is DIY-friendly, hiring a professional ensures proper electrical connections and warranty compliance.
