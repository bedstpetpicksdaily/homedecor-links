What Color Curtains Go with Grey Walls

White, cream, navy blue, and charcoal curtains are the best choices for grey walls, as they create balanced, sophisticated color schemes. The ideal curtain color depends on your grey's undertone and the mood you want to achieve—cool greys pair well with blues and whites, while warm greys work beautifully with creams and warm neutrals.

The Short Answer

Grey walls are incredibly versatile and work with nearly any curtain color, but the most popular and foolproof options are white, cream, navy, and charcoal. For a modern, clean aesthetic, choose white or light grey curtains that match your walls. If you want contrast and drama, navy blue or deep charcoal create striking focal points. Warm neutrals like taupe and beige also complement grey beautifully without requiring bold color choices.

"Grey walls provide a neutral canvas that pairs beautifully with jewel tones like emerald or sapphire curtains for drama, or soft whites and creams for a serene aesthetic, while warm neutrals like taupe and greige offer sophisticated transitional options that complement both modern and traditional interiors. The key is considering your room's lighting and existing décor elements—cooler grey walls benefit from curtains with blue undertones, while warmer greys work best with curtains featuring warm beige or soft gold accents."

The Full Explanation

Neutral Curtain Colors

White and cream curtains are timeless partners for grey walls. White curtains brighten the space and create a contemporary, airy feel—perfect for minimalist or Scandinavian-inspired interiors. Cream offers similar benefits but adds subtle warmth, making spaces feel more inviting. Both neutrals allow grey walls to remain the subtle backdrop while other décor elements, like throw pillows, wall art, and rugs, take center stage.

Cool-Toned Curtains

Navy blue curtains provide excellent contrast against grey walls while maintaining a sophisticated, cohesive look. The cool tones in navy complement both cool and warm greys, making it universally appealing. Charcoal grey curtains that are slightly darker than your walls create depth without overwhelming the space. Light blue or powder blue curtains work well if you want to introduce color while keeping the palette calm and serene.

Warm-Toned Curtains

If your grey walls have warm undertones (think greige or warm grey), taupe and warm beige curtains create beautiful harmony. Soft gold or champagne-colored curtains add elegance and warmth without clashing with grey. These options work particularly well in traditional, transitional, or eclectic design styles where layered, warm textures feel appropriate.

Bold Color Options

Grey walls are neutral enough to support bolder curtain colors if you want to make a statement. Charcoal black curtains create dramatic, modern spaces. Sage green, dusty rose, or muted purple curtains introduce personality while remaining sophisticated. Even burnt orange or terracotta works if the rest of your décor supports these warmer, earthier tones.

Considering Your Grey's Undertone

The undertone of your grey walls matters significantly. Cool greys with blue or purple undertones pair beautifully with whites, creams, navy, and cool-toned colors. Warm greys with brown or yellow undertones complement warm neutrals, soft golds, and warm-toned colors better. If you're unsure of your grey's undertone, compare it to pure white in natural light—if grey looks cool, it's a cool grey; if it looks slightly yellowish or brownish, it's a warm grey.
