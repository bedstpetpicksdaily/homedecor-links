Frequently Asked Questions

How much shedding should I actually expect with this rug?

Significant shedding for the first 6-8 weeks, then it drops off dramatically. I'm talking daily vacuuming for the first month, then weekly after that. This isn't poor quality—it's how hand-knotted wool works as individual fibers settle. Use a high-pile vacuum or carpet cleaner, not a broom. By week 8, shedding becomes minimal. If you have pets, the initial period will feel more noticeable because you'll see fibers on dark fur, but they separate easily.

Will this rug work on top of hardwood or concrete floors?

Yes, but you need a quality rug pad underneath. Wool naturally grips slightly, but on slick surfaces like polished hardwood or sealed concrete, you're creating a slip hazard without padding. A non-slip rug pad also prevents the rug from shifting under foot traffic, which protects both the rug and your flooring. The pad costs $40-80 for this size but is non-negotiable. I tested it both with and without padding—the difference in stability and safety is significant.

How does the sage color coordinate with existing decor?

This sage leans toward muted, slightly warm-toned green rather than cool or gray-green. It pairs naturally with warm wood tones, brass or bronze hardware, and both cool and warm neutrals like beige, gray, or white. I've seen it work in contemporary spaces with cool grays and metallics, and equally well in traditional rooms with burgundy and navy accents. The challenge is that sage reads differently under various lighting—test it in your actual room lighting before committing. Afternoon natural light showed it cooler than evening warm lighting did.
