Frequently Asked Questions

How does hand-knotted wool hold up to kids and pets?

Better than you'd expect. Wool is naturally resilient—it resists stains, doesn't show footprints easily, and the fibers bounce back from furniture impressions. Owners with dogs and multiple kids consistently report the rug looking decent after years. That said, you'll need to blot spills immediately and do annual professional cleaning, not just casual vacuuming. It's durable, but it still needs actual care.

Is the 9x12 size right for my living room?

A 9x12 works best if your living room is roughly 12x15 feet or larger. It should anchor your seating area with all furniture legs on the rug (or at least the front legs). If your room is smaller, you might feel like the rug dominates the space. If it's massive, go 10x14. Measure first. The wrong size will make you regret the purchase immediately.

Will terracotta clash with my existing décor?

Terracotta is warm and earthy—it works beautifully with neutrals, warm grays, rust accents, and natural wood tones. It's terrible if you lean cool-toned grays, navy, or contemporary minimalism. Before buying, grab paint samples or fabric swatches from your space and compare them side-by-side with photos of this exact rug. A mismatch here is a $1,000+ mistake.

What's the real maintenance cost over 10 years?

Budget $150-300 annually for professional cleaning, so roughly $1,500-3,000 over a decade. Regular vacuuming (weekly) is essential. Spot-clean spills immediately with plain water and white vinegar. Rotate the rug 180 degrees every year to even out wear. Skip the maintenance, and you're looking at permanent stains and matting. Factor this into your decision.
