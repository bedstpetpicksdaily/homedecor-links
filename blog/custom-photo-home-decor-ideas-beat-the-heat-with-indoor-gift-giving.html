Frequently Asked Questions

What types of photos work best for custom home decor?

High-resolution photos with good lighting and clear subjects work best. Family portraits, vacation memories, milestone moments, and pet photos are popular choices. GiftLab recommends images that are at least 300 DPI for optimal print quality.

How long does it take to receive custom photo home decor from GiftLab?

Production times vary by item type, typically ranging from 3-7 business days after order confirmation. GiftLab provides estimated delivery dates at checkout, and expedited options are often available for rush orders.

Can I order multiple personalized pieces to decorate an entire room?

Absolutely! GiftLab offers bulk ordering and coordinated sets, making it easy to create a cohesive photo-filled space. You can mix wall art, custom mugs, flipbooks, and other items to tell your complete story.

Are custom photo gifts from GiftLab suitable for all occasions?

Yes! Personalized home decor works for birthdays, anniversaries, weddings, housewarmings, baby showers, holidays, and more. GiftLab's versatile options adapt to any celebration or recipient.

What's the difference between GiftLab's custom photo flipbooks and other photo products?

Photo flipbooks offer interactive, tactile engagement that static wall art doesn't provide. They're perfect for creating a storytelling experience, combining multiple photos in a compact, displayable format that recipients actively enjoy.
