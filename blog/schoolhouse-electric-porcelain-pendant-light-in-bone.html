Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Bone color really white, or is it more off-white/cream?

It leans toward off-white with slight warmth—closer to cream than bright white. If your kitchen or dining area has warm lighting already, it blends seamlessly. If you have cool-toned white walls, test it first or look at customer photos to see how it reads in similar lighting. Most reviewers say it's exactly what they expected from the product images.

How often do you need to clean porcelain pendant lights?

Dust accumulates on any exposed light fixture, porcelain included. Weekly quick dusting keeps it looking fresh, but you won't need special cleaning solutions or careful handling like you would with delicate glass. A soft cloth and occasional damp wipe handles it. Real parents report this is genuinely low-maintenance compared to glass alternatives that show fingerprints constantly.

Does this pendant light get hot during use?

Modern pendant fixtures produce minimal heat compared to older incandescent setups. The ceramic itself stays cool even with standard bulbs. This makes it safe around curious hands and eliminates concerns about heat damage to nearby fabrics or surfaces. Your main consideration is choosing bulb brightness and color temperature, not heat management.

Can you use this with a dimmer switch?

Yes, but it depends on your bulb choice. LED bulbs work with dimmers, but not all LEDs are dimmable—check the package before buying. Incandescent bulbs are natively dimmable. This flexibility is actually a major advantage, since you can adjust mood lighting without buying special fixtures.
