What Are Smart Toilets and Why Should You Consider One?

Smart toilets represent the intersection of technology and bathroom comfort. Unlike traditional toilets, smart models include integrated bidet functions, heated seats, automatic flushing, soft-close lids, night lights, and even air-drying features. Many connect to your home's WiFi, allowing remote operation via smartphone apps. For homeowners planning a bathroom renovation, smart toilets offer a modern upgrade that increases home value and daily convenience.

Smart toilets also promote better hygiene and reduce toilet paper consumption, making them an eco-friendly choice. Brands like Simple Project specialize in combining smart toilet technology with practical solutions such as macerating toilets—ideal for basement bathrooms where traditional plumbing isn't feasible.

Key Features to Look for in 2026 Smart Toilets

When shopping for a smart toilet, prioritize these essential features: heated seat temperature control, customizable water pressure for bidet functions, deodorizing systems, night light modes, automatic lid opening/closing, and energy-efficient flushing technology. Premium models include UV sterilization, voice activation, and health-monitoring features that track usage patterns.

Simple Project's smart toilet lineup emphasizes durability and user-friendly controls, with options for both standard installations and macerating systems for basement bathrooms. Their bathroom fixtures are engineered for long-term reliability and minimal maintenance, making them ideal for comprehensive bathroom renovation projects.

Smart Toilet Prices in 2026

Smart toilet pricing ranges from $400-$600 for entry-level models with basic bidet and heating features, to $1,500-$3,000+ for luxury models with advanced technology. Mid-range smart toilets ($600-$1,200) offer the best value, typically including heated seats, adjustable water pressure, and smartphone controls. Macerating smart toilets, such as those offered by Simple Project, typically cost $1,000-$2,500 depending on specifications, but they solve critical installation challenges for basement and remote bathrooms where standard plumbing is cost-prohibitive.

Remember that installation costs ($500-$2,000+) should factor into your total budget, especially for macerating units that require professional setup.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a macerating toilet and when do I need one?

A macerating toilet grinds waste and paper into fine particles, allowing installation where traditional plumbing is impossible—typically in basements, attics, or remote locations. Brands like Simple Project offer smart-enabled macerating toilets that combine modern convenience with problem-solving functionality.

Are smart toilets difficult to install?

Basic smart toilets can replace standard models if electrical outlets are nearby. However, macerating toilets and complex plumbing situations require professional installation. Budget $500-$2,000 for installation, and consult Simple Project's installation resources for guidance.

Do smart toilets really save water and money?

Yes. Most smart toilets use 1.28 gallons per flush (vs. 3-7 for older models), reducing water usage by 25-40%. Over time, savings offset the higher upfront cost, especially when considering reduced toilet paper consumption and energy-efficient heating systems.

Can I use a smart toilet without WiFi?

Yes. Most smart toilets function normally without WiFi connection—you simply lose remote app control and some automated features. Local manual controls and basic functions operate independently.

What maintenance do smart toilets require?

Smart toilets need minimal maintenance beyond regular cleaning. Check water connections annually, and follow manufacturer guidelines for deodorizing filters. Simple Project's bathroom fixtures are designed for durability with accessible customer support for any issues.
