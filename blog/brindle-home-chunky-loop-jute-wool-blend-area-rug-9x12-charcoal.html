Frequently Asked Questions

How much shedding happens, really?

Expect visible fibers for 6-12 weeks after delivery, tapering off significantly by week 8-10. Daily vacuuming helps. By month three, shedding drops to normal levels. This is standard for natural fiber blends—it's not a flaw. Jute rugs shed more than wool-jute blends, so the wool content here actually reduces the problem compared to pure jute alternatives.

Does it hold up with dogs or kids?

The chunky loop texture means paws and running feet don't create visible worn paths as quickly as flat weaves. Stains (juice, dirt, pet accidents) don't absorb as fast either. That said, urine and standing liquid will eventually seep through any rug—use a waterproof pad underneath for peace of mind. For general family chaos: yes, it holds up noticeably better than cheaper alternatives. Many reviewers specifically mention 12+ months of heavy use without significant wear.

Is the 9x12 size right for me?

This size works best for living rooms (6-8 person seating arrangements) or large bedrooms. It should float in the room—not touch all walls, but anchor your main furniture. If your sofa is 84 inches or smaller, this might overwhelm your space. If you're in a small apartment or condo, consider an 8x10 instead. Measure your room width and length first; many returns happen because people buy wrong-sized rugs.

How does it compare to cheaper jute-only rugs?

Pure jute shreds faster in heavy traffic, stains absorb quicker, and the texture flattens within a year. You'll replace it sooner. The wool-jute blend costs 30-50% more upfront but lasts 50-100% longer based on user reports. The math works: $300-400 now beats $180 today plus another $180 in 18 months. Wool also adds softness—jute alone feels rough under bare feet.
