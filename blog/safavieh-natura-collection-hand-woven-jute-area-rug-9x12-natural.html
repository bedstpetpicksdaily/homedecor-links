Frequently Asked Questions

How much shedding should I actually expect with the Safavieh Natura jute rug?

Heavy shedding for 6-12 weeks is completely normal and not a quality issue. Plan to vacuum 2-3 times weekly during this period. After that, shedding drops significantly but doesn't completely stop. The rug stabilizes as it settles and gets more foot traffic. If you have light-colored furniture or dark hardwood, position it away from these surfaces or use a rug pad with a non-slip barrier to contain the fibers.

Is a 9x12 jute rug too large or small for typical living rooms?

The 9x12 size works for most living rooms where you want the rug to define a seating area without overwhelming the space. If your sofa is 8 feet wide, the rug should extend about 2-3 feet on either side for visual balance. For smaller living rooms under 200 square feet, this size might feel slightly large—consider measuring your space first or testing with a temporary marker outline on your floor to see how it proportions.

Can I use this rug in a high-traffic kitchen or entryway?

Not recommended. While the Safavieh Natura is durable, jute's weakness is moisture and the inevitable spills of kitchen and entryway environments. Jute absorbs liquids quickly and can develop mold or permanent water stains. Save this rug for living rooms, bedrooms, or formal dining areas away from water sources. For kitchens and entryways, choose synthetic fiber or wool blends specifically rated for moisture-heavy spaces.
