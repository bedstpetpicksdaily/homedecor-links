Frequently Asked Questions

What size canvas print should I choose for my living room?

The ideal size depends on your wall space and room proportions. A good rule of thumb is to select an oversized canvas print that covers 50-75% of your wall. Big Wall Decor offers various sizes to fit different spaces, and their team can help you determine the perfect dimensions for your living room.

What's the difference between canvas prints and framed art from Big Wall Decor?

Canvas prints offer a modern, gallery-like aesthetic and come stretched on wooden frames, while framed art pieces include protective glass or acrylic and come in finished frames. Both are high-quality options; your choice depends on your style preference and whether you want a contemporary or traditional look.

Does abstract art work well in living rooms?

Absolutely! Abstract art is a popular choice for living rooms because it complements various design styles and sparks conversation. Big Wall Decor's collection of abstract statement wall pieces offers bold colors and intriguing compositions that can serve as stunning focal points in any living room.

How do I care for and maintain large wall art?

Most Big Wall Decor pieces require minimal maintenance—simply dust occasionally with a soft cloth. Canvas prints should be kept away from direct sunlight to prevent fading, while framed art benefits from being hung away from moisture and heat sources.
