How long do wooden puzzle models last?

EWA's wooden construction sets are designed to last for decades with proper care. Unlike plastic models that yellow and crack, quality wood actually improves over time, developing a beautiful patina. Many customers report their EWA models looking just as stunning 10-20 years after assembly.
