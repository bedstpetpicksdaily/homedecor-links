Frequently Asked Questions

How bad is the shedding, really?

Bad enough that you need a serious vacuum routine for the first 2-3 months. Light daily vacuuming minimizes it, but if you're expecting zero shedding, hand-knotted wool isn't your product. After the initial break-in, shedding drops to normal acceptable levels. Many owners say it's worth tolerating because the durability gain is substantial.

Is this washable, or do I need professional cleaning?

Spot-cleaning works for most spills—blot with cold water and mild detergent, then blot dry. For deep cleaning, professional rug cleaning every 18-24 months is the honest recommendation if you have pets or kids. At-home deep cleaning risks over-wetting the wool, which takes forever to dry and invites mildew. Budget accordingly.

Will the sage color fade in direct sunlight?

Wool holds color better than synthetic fibers, but direct afternoon sun through windows will fade any rug eventually. The sage is particularly resilient because medium tones don't show fading as obviously as lighter shades. In a room with standard window exposure (not direct south-facing glare), you'll get years before noticeable fading becomes a concern.

What's the actual size compared to my furniture?

A 9x12 rug works well if front sofa legs sit on the rug with 18 inches extending beyond. If you have a large sectional, you might need to anchor just the seating area. Coffee table legs should sit on the rug. Measure your actual furniture placement before ordering—this size is specific enough that wrong placement looks worse than using no rug.
