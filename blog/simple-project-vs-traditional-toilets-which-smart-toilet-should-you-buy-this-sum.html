What Makes Simple Project Smart Toilets Different?

Simple Project has established itself as a leader in bathroom innovation, specializing in macerating toilets and advanced smart bathroom fixtures. Their macerating toilet technology allows for installation virtually anywhere in your home—even basement bathrooms—without requiring extensive plumbing modifications. Unlike traditional toilets that demand specific drainage configurations, Simple Project's systems feature powerful grinding mechanisms that break down waste and paper into fine particles, enabling installation in locations previously considered impossible for bathroom fixtures.

The smart toilet features offered by Simple Project include heated seats, customizable water pressure settings, integrated bidets, and energy-efficient flushing systems. These aren't just luxury add-ons; they're practical upgrades that enhance daily comfort and reduce water consumption. Traditional toilets offer none of these capabilities, making them feel outdated by comparison.

Installation Flexibility and Basement Bathroom Solutions

One of the most significant advantages of Simple Project macerating toilets is their installation flexibility. Traditional toilets require gravity-fed drainage and proper slope alignment, which limits where you can place them. Simple Project's innovative approach eliminates these constraints. For homeowners planning bathroom renovations in basements, attics, or remote areas of their property, Simple Project provides a game-changing solution that traditional fixtures simply cannot match.

The installation process for Simple Project toilets is streamlined and often requires less invasive plumbing work, potentially saving thousands in renovation costs. This flexibility makes them ideal for modern bathroom design where you're not restricted by existing plumbing infrastructure.

Water Efficiency and Long-Term Savings

Traditional toilets typically use 3.5-7 gallons per flush, while Simple Project smart toilets are engineered for efficiency without compromising performance. Advanced flushing technology means fewer water-wasting refills and reduced environmental impact. Over time, this efficiency translates to significant savings on your water bill—a benefit that continues delivering value year after year, unlike the upfront cost of upgrading.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Simple Project macerating toilets really be installed anywhere?

Yes, within reason. Simple Project's macerating technology allows installation in basements, upstairs bathrooms, and remote locations where traditional gravity-fed toilets won't work. However, they do require an electrical outlet nearby and proper ventilation venting.

How much water do Simple Project smart toilets save compared to traditional models?

Simple Project smart toilets typically use 1.28 gallons per flush compared to 3.5-7 gallons for older traditional models. This efficiency can save families thousands of gallons annually and reduce water bills significantly.

Are Smart Project smart toilets difficult to maintain?

No. Simple Project toilets are designed with user-friendly maintenance in mind. Regular cleaning is similar to traditional toilets, and the electronic components are durable and reliable. Simple Project provides comprehensive support and warranty coverage.

What's the cost difference between Simple Project and traditional toilets?

Simple Project smart and macerating toilets cost more upfront—typically $800-$2,500+ compared to $200-$500 for basic traditional models. However, installation savings and reduced water bills often offset this difference within 3-5 years.
