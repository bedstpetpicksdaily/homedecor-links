Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Loloi Torrance compare to Safavieh's hand-knotted wool options?

Safavieh typically offers lower knot density and thinner wool pile for 25-35% less money. The Torrance feels noticeably denser underfoot. If you walk on both in a showroom, you'll feel the difference immediately. Safavieh wins if you need budget-friendly; Loloi wins if you want longevity as the priority.

Will an 8x10 rug fit my small apartment living room?

Measure your seating area first. The rule: at least two legs of your sofa should sit on the rug. In apartments under 600 sq ft, 8x10 often overwhelms. Consider 6x9 instead. The Torrance Collection comes in multiple sizes if dimensions are the only hesitation.

How often do you need to vacuum this rug, and will it shed forever?

Vacuum 2-3 times weekly during the first 4-6 weeks—that's when loose fibers from the knitting process shed out. After month two, drop to weekly. Shedding doesn't stop entirely with wool, but it becomes minimal. This is completely normal for hand-knotted wool and not a defect. Synthetic rugs shed less initially but also degrade faster overall.
