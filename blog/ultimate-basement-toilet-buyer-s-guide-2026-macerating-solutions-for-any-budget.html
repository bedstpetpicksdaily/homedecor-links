What Are Macerating Toilets and Why You Need One for Your Basement

Macerating toilets are innovative bathroom fixtures that grind and pump waste upward, allowing installation anywhere in your home regardless of plumbing location or elevation. Unlike traditional gravity-fed toilets, these systems use a powerful grinding mechanism to break down waste into a slurry that travels through small-diameter pipes to your main drain line. This revolutionary approach eliminates costly excavation, rerouting, and structural modifications—saving thousands on installation costs.

Simple Project specializes in macerating toilet technology that combines reliability with modern design.
