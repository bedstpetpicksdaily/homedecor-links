Frequently Asked Questions

What size large canvas print should I choose for my living room?

The ideal size depends on your wall space and room dimensions. A good rule of thumb is that your canvas print should take up 50-75% of the wall space above furniture. For most living rooms, a 40x60 inch or larger canvas creates the perfect statement wall without overwhelming the space.

Are Big Wall Decor's large canvas prints ready to hang?

Yes, Big Wall Decor offers both framed and gallery-wrapped canvas prints that come ready to hang. Their oversized canvas prints are professionally stretched and come with hanging hardware included.

What abstract art designs work best for summer 2026 interior trends?

Summer 2026 trends favor bold, colorful abstract art with organic shapes and bright color palettes. Watercolor-inspired designs, geometric patterns, and nature-influenced abstracts are particularly popular. Big Wall Decor's collection includes all these trending styles in various sizes.

Can large canvas prints work in smaller bedrooms?

Absolutely! Even smaller bedrooms can accommodate large canvas prints when positioned strategically, such as above the bed. A 30x40 or 36x48 inch canvas creates impact without making the room feel cramped.

How do I choose between different canvas print options?

Consider your room's color scheme, existing furniture style, and the mood you want to create. Big Wall Decor offers abstract art, landscape, and modern designs—browse their collection and visualize pieces in your space before deciding.
