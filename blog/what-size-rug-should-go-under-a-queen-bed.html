What Size Rug Should Go Under a Queen Bed?

An 8x10 area rug is the ideal size for a queen bed, giving you roughly 18 to 24 inches of exposed rug on both sides and at the foot of the bed. If your bedroom is smaller or you're working with a tighter budget, a 6x9 rug positioned two-thirds of the way down the bed is the next best option.

The Short Answer

For a queen bed (60" x 80"), an 8x10 rug is the gold standard. Place the rug so the top edge starts just below the nightstands, leaving the upper third of the bed off the rug entirely. This gives you soft flooring exactly where your feet land in the morning while framing the bed as the visual anchor of the room. If your room is under 11 feet wide, drop down to a 6x9 and center it under the lower two-thirds of the bed.

The Full Explanation

A queen mattress measures 60 inches wide by 80 inches long, and once you add a bed frame, you're typically looking at 64 to 68 inches wide and 84 to 88 inches long. That footprint is what every rug decision has to work around.

An 8x10 rug measures 96 inches by 120 inches. Slide a queen bed onto it and you get about 16 inches of rug exposed on each side and a generous stretch at the foot. That exposed border is what makes a room feel finished rather than accidental. Rugs that are too small — the classic 5x7 shoved under a bed — read as an afterthought and visually shrink the entire space.

There are three standard placement approaches for a queen bed:

  • Full placement: The entire bed, including nightstands, sits on the rug. This requires a 9x12 and a bedroom of at least 13 by 15 feet. It's the most luxurious look but overkill for most rooms.
  • Two-thirds placement: The most popular and practical option. The rug starts just below the nightstands and extends past the foot of the bed. An 8x10 nails this proportion for a queen.
  • Runner placement: Two 2x6 or 2x8 runners flank the bed. This works in narrow rooms or when you have beautiful hardwood you don't want to cover.

Room size matters as much as bed size. In a 10x12 bedroom, an 8x10 rug will leave only about a foot of floor showing around the perimeter, which can feel cramped. In a 12x14 or larger room, the 8x10 sits comfortably with breathing room on all sides. As a general rule, leave 12 to 24 inches of bare floor between the rug edge and the walls.

One detail people miss: rug thickness. A low-pile or flatweave rug slides under a bed frame easily and won't interfere with door clearance. A thick shag or high-pile rug can lift the bed slightly and make nightstands sit unevenly if only the front legs are on the rug. If you love plush texture, make sure all four bed legs are either on the rug or off it — never split.

What the Experts Say

Interior designers almost universally recommend sizing up when you're between two rug options. The most common mistake in bedroom design isn't choosing the wrong color or pattern — it's choosing a rug that's too small. A slightly oversized rug makes a room feel intentional and expansive; an undersized one makes even a large bedroom feel disjointed.

Designers also point to the "step test" as the practical benchmark: when you swing your legs out of bed in the morning, both feet should land on rug, not hardwood or tile. With an 8x10 under a queen, that happens on both sides of the bed with room to spare.

Another expert consensus: the rug should extend at least 18 inches beyond the sides of the bed frame. Anything less and the rug looks like a bath mat. Anything more than about 30 inches and you're paying for coverage you'll mostly hide under furniture.

The Product Solution

An 8x10 area rug is the single most versatile bedroom purchase you can make for a queen setup. It hits the proportions designers recommend, works with two-thirds placement in most standard bedrooms, and gives you enough exposed border to actually see the pattern or texture you paid for.

When shopping, prioritize a pile height under half an inch for easy bed placement, and look for materials that hold up to daily foot traffic — wool blends, polypropylene, and low-pile synthetics all work well in bedrooms. Neutral or tonal patterns tend to age better than bold statement rugs, since the bed and bedding already carry a lot of visual weight. Don't skip a rug pad; it prevents shifting under the bed frame and adds cushioning underfoot.

Quick Tips

  • Tape it out first. Use painter's tape on the floor to mark an 8x10 footprint before you buy. It takes two minutes and prevents an expensive return.
  • Start the rug below the nightstands. Leave the top 24 to 30 inches of the bed off the rug — this is the two-thirds rule and it works every time.
  • When in doubt, size up. An 8x10 that's slightly large beats a 6x9 that's slightly small in almost every bedroom.
  • Always use a rug pad. It stops slipping, protects your floors, and adds noticeable cushion — especially important under a bed you walk around daily.

Conclusion

An 8x10 area rug is the right answer for the vast majority of queen bed setups, delivering the 18-plus inches of side coverage that makes a bedroom look professionally designed. Measure your room, tape out the dimensions, and size up if you're torn — you'll almost never regret a rug that's a little too generous.

Frequently Asked Questions

What size rug should go under a queen bed?

A 9x12 foot rug is the ideal size for a queen bed, as it extends 3 feet on both sides and the foot of the bed. If you prefer a smaller footprint, an 8x10 rug will work but will only reach about 2.5 feet on the sides. For a more luxurious look that anchors the entire bedroom, consider a 10x14 rug.

Should a rug go completely under a queen bed or just partially?

The best practice is to have the rug extend at least 2-3 feet beyond the sides of the bed so your feet land on the rug when you get out of bed. You can choose to have the rug go partially or completely under the bed depending on your room size and design preference, but at minimum it should frame the bed on all sides.

Can I use an 8x10 rug under a queen bed?

Yes, an 8x10 rug works well under a queen bed and is a popular choice for medium-sized bedrooms. It will provide coverage under the bed with some overhang on the sides, though slightly less than a 9x12 rug. Make sure the rug extends at least 18-24 inches on each side of the bed for proper visual balance.

What if my bedroom is small—what's the minimum rug size for a queen bed?

For small bedrooms, a 5x8 rug is the minimum size and should be positioned so the front two legs of the bed rest on it. While this size doesn't provide as much coverage as larger rugs, it still defines the space and adds a finished look without overwhelming a tight room.
