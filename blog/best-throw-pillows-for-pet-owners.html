Best Throw Pillows for Pet Owners

If you're a pet owner who loves stylish home decor, finding throw pillows that can withstand paws, claws, and the occasional accident is essential. Your furniture deserves to be both beautiful and functional, which is why choosing the right throw pillows makes all the difference in creating a home that's pet-friendly without sacrificing your design aesthetic. The good news is that durability and style aren't mutually exclusive—you just need to know what to prioritize.

What to Look For

  • Performance Fabrics with Pet-Proof Construction: Look for throw pillows made from solution-dyed polyester, microfiber, or specially treated cotton blends that resist staining and shedding. These fabrics are specifically engineered to repel liquids and prevent pet hair from embedding into the fibers, making cleanup quick and easy without requiring professional cleaning.
  • Removable, Washable Covers: Choose pillows with zippered, removable covers that can be thrown in the washing machine. This feature is non-negotiable for pet owners—it allows you to tackle accidents, odors, and general grime without worrying about damaging the pillow itself or needing to replace the entire piece.
  • Darker Colors and Patterns That Hide Imperfections: Select throw pillows in charcoal, navy, gray, or patterned designs rather than light neutrals. These colors naturally camouflage pet hair, dirt, and minor stains, keeping your pillows looking fresher between washes and reducing the frequency of cleaning needed.
