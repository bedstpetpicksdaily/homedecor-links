How does the Dorset pendant compare to West Elm or CB2 brass dome options?

Schoolhouse Electric prioritizes material authenticity over price competition. What's the actual light output, and does one pendant illuminate a full dining table?

A single Dorset pendant produces roughly 400-500 lumens depending on bulb selection, which illuminates an intimate 3-4 person dining scenario effectively. For standard 8-foot ceilings and 6-8 person tables, most designers recommend either clustering two pendants (about 36 inches apart) or pairing it with ambient wall or ceiling fixtures. The ceramic dome focuses light downward, so height matters—mount it 30-36 inches above table surface for optimal coverage without glare.

Is the blackened brass finish durable, or will it wear through to brass underneath?

The blackened finish is a genuine surface treatment, not paint. It develops patina naturally and individual marks add character rather than reveal manufacturing flaws. With normal dusting and occasional brass-specific cleaning (avoid vinegar and acidic products), the finish remains stable for years. Some owners intentionally enhance patina by allowing humidity exposure in kitchens and bathrooms—it creates a lived-in aesthetic that actually improves the fixture's visual story.
