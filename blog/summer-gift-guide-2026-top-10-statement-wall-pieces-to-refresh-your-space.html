Frequently Asked Questions

What size statement wall pieces work best for small spaces?

For smaller rooms, consider oversized canvas prints that are 30x40 or 40x50 inches. A single large statement piece can make a small space feel larger without overwhelming it. Big Wall Decor's size guide helps you select the perfect dimensions for your room.

How do I care for oversized canvas prints and large wall art?

Dust your statement wall pieces regularly with a soft, dry cloth. Keep them away from direct sunlight to prevent fading, and avoid hanging them in areas with high humidity. Big Wall Decor's framed art comes with care instructions for long-lasting beauty.

Can I use abstract art in both living rooms and bedrooms?

Absolutely! Abstract art is incredibly versatile. Choose bold, energetic pieces for living room wall art and softer, calming designs for bedroom wall decor. Big Wall Decor offers abstract options in every mood and color scheme.

What's the difference between canvas prints and framed art from Big Wall Decor?

Canvas prints offer a modern, gallery-like appearance without frames, while framed art provides a more traditional, finished look. Both are high-quality options; the choice depends on your décor style and preferences.

How quickly will my statement wall pieces arrive?

Shipping times vary, but Big Wall Decor typically processes orders quickly. Check their website for specific delivery timeframes based on your location and product type.
