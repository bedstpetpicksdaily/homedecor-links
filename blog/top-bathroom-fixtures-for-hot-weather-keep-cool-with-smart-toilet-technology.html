Frequently Asked Questions

How do smart toilets help in hot weather?

Smart toilets reduce humidity buildup through advanced ventilation systems and moisture-wicking technology. Many models feature cooled seat options and automated deodorizing that rely on cooling rather than chemicals, keeping bathrooms fresher and cooler during hot months.

What are macerating toilets and why are they good for hot climates?

Macerating toilets grind waste into fine particles before pumping them to main plumbing lines. They offer flexible installation options that reduce heat distribution from plumbing systems, making them ideal for basement bathrooms and renovations in warm climates.

How much can smart bathroom fixtures reduce energy costs in hot weather?

Water-efficient smart fixtures from Simple Project reduce hot water demand and lower bathroom humidity levels. This means your air conditioning system works more efficiently, potentially reducing energy costs by 15-25% depending on usage patterns and climate conditions.

Are Simple Project smart toilets difficult to install?

Simple Project smart toilets are designed for professional installation but offer clear setup guidelines. Macerating toilet systems provide flexible installation options suitable for various bathroom layouts, including challenging basement spaces.
