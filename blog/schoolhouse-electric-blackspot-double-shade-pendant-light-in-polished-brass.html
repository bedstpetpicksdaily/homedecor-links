How does the polished brass hold up to daily use?

Polished brass darkens and develops character over time—that's intentional with Schoolhouse's design. It won't look pristine forever, and that's the point. If you prefer brass that stays shiny, you'll need to polish it monthly. Most buyers embrace the aging; some find it annoying. Know which camp you're in before buying.

Is the double-shade design worth the extra cost over single-shade pendants?

If you care about even illumination and visual complexity, yes. The upper shade directs light upward (reducing ceiling shadows) while the lower shade lights your task or dining area. Single shades create that 'spotlight' effect that works in some spaces but feels harsh in others. It's not a gimmick—it's functional design.
