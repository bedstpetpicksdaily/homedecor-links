Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Buying based on color alone without considering the scale of your space—a beautiful oversized rug or art piece can overwhelm a small room, so measure twice and visualize before purchasing.
  • Neglecting to check return policies and shipping times, especially during peak sale season when inventory moves quickly and backorders become common.
  • Mixing too many patterns and textures at once; stick to a cohesive color palette and layer in patterns gradually to avoid a cluttered, chaotic look.

Expert Tips for Best Results

  • Start with larger anchor pieces like rugs or curtains first, then build your accent pieces (pillows, lamps, wall art) around them—this ensures better color harmony and visual balance throughout your room.
  • Take advantage of September's natural light to assess how products will actually look in your space; photograph potential pieces against your existing decor using your phone's camera to see them in context.
  • Layer lighting with multiple sources (overhead, lamps, natural light) rather than relying on one statement piece; this creates depth and makes rooms feel larger and more inviting.
