Frequently Asked Questions

Can you really wash this in a regular washing machine?

Yes, but there's technique involved. Use cold water, gentle cycle, and make sure your machine is large enough that the rug isn't crammed. The manufacturer specifically designed this for machine washing, so it won't unravel or shrink significantly like traditional rugs would. Many owners report washing theirs monthly without issues.

Will this rug stay in place on hardwood or tile?

Not without a pad underneath. The backing is designed for grip, but on slippery surfaces like hardwood or tile, it'll shift with foot traffic. A separate non-slip rug pad ($15-20) is essential if you have these flooring types. It's an extra cost, but prevents the safety hazard of a sliding rug.

How long does an 8x10 actually last with daily use?

Most owners report 2-3 years of solid daily use before noticeable thinning occurs. That's reasonable given the price point. Pet owners with high-traffic households might see wear faster. The washable feature helps extend life because you're not dealing with set-in stains that force premature replacement.

Is the indigo color true to photos?

The indigo is fairly accurate in online images—it reads as a true medium blue rather than navy or purple. It pairs well with both warm and cool-toned decor. It will lighten slightly over time with repeated washing, so expect some color shift after the first few machine cycles.
