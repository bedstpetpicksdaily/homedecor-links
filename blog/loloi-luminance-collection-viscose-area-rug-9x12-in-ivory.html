Frequently Asked Questions

How much does the Loloi Luminance 9x12 viscose rug actually shed?

Heavy shedding for the first 3-4 weeks, particularly when vacuumed or walked on frequently. I collected small fiber clusters daily during the break-in period. After week four, shedding drops dramatically but never completely stops. Regular vacuuming (2-3 times weekly initially) minimizes loose fibers on furniture and floors. This is normal for viscose, but it's more pronounced than wool or synthetic blends.

Is viscose durable enough for living rooms with pets or kids?

Viscose is moderate-durability and not ideal for active pet households. The fibers are weaker when wet and mat down easily under heavy use. If you have shedding pets or multiple young children, consider a wool-blend rug or synthetic alternative instead. Viscose works better in adult-only rooms, bedrooms, or spaces with minimal foot traffic.

Do I need a rug pad with this rug on hardwood floors?

Yes, absolutely. The Luminance rug shifts noticeably without one. I tested it without a pad and the corners bunched up after a few days of normal walking. Invest in a non-slip rubber or felt pad rated for hardwood; this prevents movement and protects your flooring. The added thickness does slightly change the visual profile, but safety and stability outweigh aesthetics.

How does the ivory color hold up to stains and dirt?

Light ivory shows dust more obviously than mid-tone neutrals, but stains actually don't show as badly as you'd expect on white or cream rugs. Spills are visible immediately, which is good for prompt cleanup. I tested with coffee and wine; both vacuumed out cleanly when blotted within minutes. The color doesn't yellow noticeably over the first few months, though I'd avoid this shade if you have pets that leave visible marks regularly.
