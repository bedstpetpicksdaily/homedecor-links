Frequently Asked Questions

What size should statement wall pieces be for different rooms?

For living rooms, look for oversized canvas prints between 40-60 inches wide to serve as a true focal point. Bedrooms typically work best with pieces in the 30-50 inch range, while larger wall spaces can accommodate Big Wall Decor's biggest statement pieces. Always measure your wall and account for furniture placement before selecting your size.

How do I choose between abstract art and other styles for wall decor?

Abstract art offers maximum versatility and works with any decor style, making it an excellent choice if you're unsure. Consider your recipient's existing aesthetic—modern minimalists may love bold abstract pieces, while eclectic decorators might prefer figurative or landscape art. Big Wall Decor offers diverse styles within their abstract collection.

Are Big Wall Decor's oversized canvas prints easy to install?

Yes, Big Wall Decor's large wall art comes ready to hang with hardware included. Most oversized canvas prints and framed pieces can be installed in under an hour with basic tools. For extra-large statement pieces, having a second person assist makes installation easier and safer.

What's the best way to style wall art in a room with existing furniture?

Hang statement wall pieces at eye level (57-60 inches from floor to center). Large wall art works best as a focal point above a sofa, bed, or console table. Big Wall Decor's oversized designs create balance in rooms with substantial furniture while adding visual interest and depth.
