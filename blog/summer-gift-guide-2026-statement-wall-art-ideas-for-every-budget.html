How to Choose the Right Statement Wall Art

When selecting a statement piece from Big Wall Decor's collection, consider the recipient's existing decor style, room dimensions, and color palette. Large wall art should complement rather than clash with furniture and accessories. Abstract art offers versatility and works with nearly any interior style, while specific designs allow for more personalized gifting. Consider room function too—bedroom wall decor might feature calming, softer tones, while living room wall art can be bolder and more dramatic. Remember that oversized canvas prints create maximum impact in larger rooms, while smaller spaces benefit from medium-sized statement pieces that add interest without overwhelming the space.

Frequently Asked Questions

What size canvas print should I choose for my living room?

Room size matters significantly when selecting oversized canvas prints. For large living rooms (20x20+ feet), consider 48x60" or larger statement pieces. Medium rooms (15x18 feet) work best with 40x50" prints, while smaller spaces benefit from 30x40" or 24x36" options. Big Wall Decor provides dimension guides to help you choose the perfect size for your space.

Are Big Wall Decor's canvas prints suitable for bedrooms?

Absolutely! Big Wall Decor offers beautiful bedroom wall decor options in their collection. For bedrooms, consider slightly smaller pieces than living room art, and choose designs with calming color palettes like soft blues, grays, or warm neutrals. Abstract art in these tones creates serene, sophisticated bedroom wall art without being overstimulating.

How do I hang oversized wall art safely?

Large wall decor requires proper installation for safety. Use wall studs whenever possible, or invest in heavy-duty drywall anchors rated for the weight of your framed art or canvas print. Big Wall Decor's pieces come with hanging hardware, and it's recommended to have two people assist with installation of very large statement pieces. When in doubt, consult a professional installer.

What abstract art styles work best with modern decor?

Geometric abstract designs, minimalist compositions, and bold color-block artwork pair beautifully with modern and contemporary interiors. Big Wall Decor's abstract art collection includes pieces featuring clean lines, bold colors, and sophisticated compositions that complement minimalist to industrial design styles perfectly.

Can I return wall art if it doesn't match my space?

Return policies vary, so check Big Wall Decor's specific policy before purchasing. Many retailers offer 30-60 day return windows for unopened items. Always order samples or check dimension guides carefully before ordering large statement pieces to ensure they fit your space and match your aesthetic.
