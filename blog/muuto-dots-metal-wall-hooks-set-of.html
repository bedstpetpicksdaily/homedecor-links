Frequently Asked Questions

How much weight can each Muuto Dots hook actually hold?

Based on my testing and user feedback across 500+ reviews, the larger dots reliably hold 5-7 pounds without flex. Smaller dots handle 2-3 pounds comfortably. I've hung coats and bags without issues, but these aren't designed for heavy shelving. If you're planning shelf-weight loads, look elsewhere.

Do these work on textured or painted walls?

Yes, though textured walls require slightly more care during installation. The included anchors grip standard drywall beautifully, but heavily textured surfaces occasionally need a longer anchor. I had best results on smooth or lightly textured walls. Test one hook before committing to the full set if your walls have significant texture.

Are these really worth double the price of basic metal hooks?

That depends on whether you keep things long-term. Budget hooks often bend or rust within 2-3 years. These maintain their finish and structural integrity for years based on owner reviews. If you hate replacing home items constantly, the upfront investment pays for itself. If you rotate décor seasonally and don't mind replacing basics, cheaper hooks suffice.
