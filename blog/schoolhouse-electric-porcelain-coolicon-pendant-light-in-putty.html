The matte Putty glaze actually hides dust remarkably well—better than glossy finishes would. Fingerprints are invisible on the exterior. The interior shows dust if you're looking directly up at it, but from normal viewing angles, it's not noticeable. I wipe the inside occasionally with a dry microfiber cloth, which takes 30 seconds.

What kind of bulb does it take, and what's the light quality?

Standard E26 base, so you have complete flexibility. I tested it with both 60W equivalent LED (2700K warm white) and 75W equivalent. The warm white is essential—anything cooler looks jarring against the warm porcelain tone. Is the Putty color warmer or cooler than it appears in online photos?

Slightly warmer. Online photos sometimes push it toward gray, but in person it reads as warm greige with beige undertones. This is actually ideal—it avoids cold industrial aesthetics and instead creates something approachable and lived-in. Under warm white bulbs, it glows.
