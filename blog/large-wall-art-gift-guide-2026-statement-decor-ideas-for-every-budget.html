Frequently Asked Questions

What size large wall art should I choose as a gift?

Consider the recipient's wall space and room size. For most living rooms, 40x60 inches or larger makes an impact without overwhelming the space. For smaller rooms or apartments, 24x36 to 30x40 inches works well. Big Wall Decor provides sizing guides to help you choose the perfect dimensions.

How do I know if someone will like abstract art vs. other styles?

Consider their existing home decor and personal taste. If they prefer clean, modern spaces, abstract or geometric art works well. For those who love nature, look for landscape or nature-inspired pieces. Big Wall Decor's website lets you browse by style, making it easy to find pieces that match their aesthetic.

Is large wall art suitable for renters or those who move frequently?

Yes! Canvas prints are easy to remove and transport, making them ideal for renters and people who move. Framed pieces require more careful handling but are equally portable. Both options at Big Wall Decor work perfectly for temporary or permanent spaces.

What's the difference between canvas and framed wall art?

Canvas prints offer a modern, gallery-like aesthetic without frame glass and are typically more affordable. Framed art provides a polished, traditional look with added protection. Big Wall Decor offers both options in most designs, so you can choose based on the recipient's preference and budget.
