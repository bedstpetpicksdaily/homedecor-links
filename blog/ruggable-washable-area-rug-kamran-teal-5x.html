Does the teal color fade after washing?

Minimal fading occurs with repeated washing, and most of it happens in the first wash or two. After that, the color stabilizes. Using cold water and mild detergent slows any fading significantly. The muted tone of this particular teal actually hides minor color shifts better than bright teals would—another subtle design win.

How does this perform with pet accidents and odors?

This is where Ruggable genuinely outperforms traditional rugs. Fresh accidents wash out completely. Measure your space and visualize how far your seating extends. 5x8 works beautifully in bedrooms, home offices, nurseries, and smaller living areas.
