Frequently Asked Questions

How much shedding should I actually expect with a new Beni Ourain rug?

Significant shedding for the first 3-4 months is completely normal. I vacuumed mine twice weekly during the initial period and filled a standard vacuum bin each time. By month five, shedding dropped to negligible levels. This isn't a manufacturing defect—it's loose fibers from the hand-knotting process working their way out. If you can't tolerate this during the initial phase, consider a rug pad underneath to contain the shed fibers and make cleanup easier.

Is a 9x12 rug actually large enough for a standard living room?

Yes, it's the Goldilocks size for most homes. In a typical living room, 9x12 allows you to anchor furniture groupings while leaving perimeter floor space visible, which keeps the room from feeling cramped. I've placed this size with a sectional, coffee table, and accent chairs—all four legs of the sofa typically land on the rug, which is the interior design standard. If your living room is exceptionally large (over 20x20 feet), you might want to consider larger dimensions, but for standard rooms, this works beautifully.

How do I clean and maintain this rug to keep it looking like new?

Regular vacuuming with a high-quality upright vacuum (not a robot vacuum, which won't penetrate the natural wool fibers deeply enough) is your first line of defense. Rotate the rug 180 degrees every 6-8 months to ensure even wear. For spills, blot immediately with white cloth and cool water—don't rub. For stains, resist DIY solutions and invest in professional wool rug cleaning every 18-24 months depending on foot traffic. This maintenance routine is non-negotiable if you want the rug to age gracefully rather than looking tired in five years.
