Frequently Asked Questions

What makes personalized gifts better than generic presents?

Personalized gifts demonstrate thoughtfulness by incorporating individual memories, preferences, and inside jokes. They show the recipient that you've invested time and effort into creating something unique, making them far more meaningful than mass-produced items. Custom gifts also tend to have longer shelf lives in memory and are less likely to be regifted.

How far in advance should I order personalized gifts from GiftLab?

We recommend ordering personalized items 2-3 weeks in advance to allow for design approval, production time, and shipping. However, GiftLab offers expedited options for last-minute gift needs. Check their website for specific turnaround times based on your chosen product.

Can I order personalized gifts for group activities?

Absolutely! GiftLab specializes in custom gifts perfect for group memories. You can create matching mugs, coordinated home decor, or personalized items for entire friend groups or sports teams. Consider bulk orders for summer camp groups, sports teams, or friend circles celebrating shared adventures.

What photo resolution do I need for high-quality custom gifts?

For best results, use photos with at least 300 DPI (dots per inch) resolution. Smartphone photos typically work well, but ensure adequate lighting to avoid grainy or blurry results. GiftLab's platform provides guidance on acceptable image dimensions for each product type.

Are GiftLab's personalized products suitable for outdoor use?

Many GiftLab products are outdoor-friendly, including custom mugs, water bottles, and personalized beach bags. However, delicate items like photo flipbooks or canvas prints should be protected from extreme weather. Always check product specifications before gifting for outdoor-exclusive activities.
