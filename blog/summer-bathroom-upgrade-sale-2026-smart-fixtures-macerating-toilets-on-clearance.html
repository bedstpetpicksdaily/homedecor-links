Why Macerating Toilets Matter for Modern Bathrooms

Macerating toilets have revolutionized bathroom placement possibilities, especially for basement bathrooms where traditional plumbing isn't feasible. Simple Project's macerating toilet options feature powerful grinding mechanisms that reduce waste to a fine slurry, which can be pumped through small-diameter pipes to your main sewage line. This means you can place a fully functional toilet almost anywhere without expensive excavation or plumbing overhauls.

The technology is remarkably reliable and whisper-quiet in newer models. Are macerating toilets reliable for everyday use?

Yes, modern macerating toilets from Simple Project are highly reliable with minimal maintenance. They feature durable grinding mechanisms designed for daily use and typically operate quietly. However, they do require electrical power and should have access to a licensed plumber for any service needs.

Do smart toilets work during power outages?

Most smart toilets, including Simple Project models, have manual override functions that allow basic flushing during power outages. The smart features—bidet, heated seat, air dry—won't operate without power, but the toilet remains fully functional as a standard fixture.

Can I install a macerating toilet in my basement bathroom myself?

While some handy homeowners can install macerating toilets, professional installation is strongly recommended. These systems require proper electrical setup, secure plumbing connections, and correct pump configuration to function optimally and safely.
