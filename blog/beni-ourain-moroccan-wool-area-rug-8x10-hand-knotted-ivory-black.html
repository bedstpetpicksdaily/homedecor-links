Frequently Asked Questions

How much does shedding actually happen?

Expect moderate shedding for 2-4 weeks, then minimal ongoing loss. Vacuum twice weekly during break-in, and you'll notice a dramatic drop-off. By month two, you're looking at normal lint levels. This is standard for hand-knotted wool—it's not a defect, it's a characteristic. If immediate perfection matters more than quality materials, synthetic rugs shed less upfront but deteriorate faster overall.

Is this rug actually hand-knotted or am I getting scammed?

Real hand-knotted Moroccan rugs have irregular knots visible on the back—machine-made pieces show perfect uniformity. Check close-up photos before ordering. The 500+ reviews mentioning authenticity suggest Amazon's version is legitimate, but inspect it immediately upon arrival. If it looks machine-perfect, return it. Authentic hand-knotted construction justifies the price; fake versions don't.

Will this work over hardwood, or do I need a rug pad?

Get a rug pad. Period. Wool on hardwood without padding will slip dangerously and potentially stain the wood underneath as the rug absorbs moisture. A quality non-slip pad costs $40-70 for 8x10—think of it as mandatory, not optional. This adds to your total investment but protects both your floors and the rug itself.

How does this compare to cheaper Wayfair or Rugs USA alternatives?

Budget rugs at those retailers often use wool-synthetic blends (usually 60/40 or 50/50), which means less durability and faster matting. This 100% wool Beni Ourain will outlast them by 5+ years in normal use. You're paying more upfront but spending less per year of ownership. Run the math: if this lasts 10 years and a $600 rug lasts 4 years, you're actually ahead financially.
