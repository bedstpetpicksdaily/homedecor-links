Frequently Asked Questions

Will this pillow shed fibers everywhere?

Not excessively. New chunky knit pillows naturally release a few loose fibers in the first week or two—this is normal and happens with quality knits. A gentle shake or light vacuum resolves it. After that initial period, shedding is minimal. If you're purchasing this in July and want it immediately guest-ready, plan for a brief settling period.

How do I actually clean a chunky knit throw pillow?

Spot-clean only. The chunky knit construction means machine washing will damage the yarn and distort the shape. For stains, use a damp cloth with mild soap and cold water, then air dry completely. This is a legitimate limitation—textured pillows require more careful maintenance than flat weaves.

Is this pillow worth buying instead of searching for knockoffs?

Depends on your tolerance for replacement cycles. Cheaper chunky knit alternatives often flatten within a year or develop pilling. The Colca holds its structure longer, which means you're not replacing it annually. Calculate your actual cost-per-year, not just sticker price, and the gap shrinks.
