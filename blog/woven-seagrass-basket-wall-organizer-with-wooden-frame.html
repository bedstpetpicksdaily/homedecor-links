Will this hold the weight of books or heavier items?

Yes, but with limits. The wooden frame and wall anchors can handle 10-15 pounds safely. I stored a stack of five design books plus decorative objects with zero sagging. How do I clean seagrass without damaging it?

A dry microfiber cloth or soft brush works best. I used a barely damp cloth once and it left water marks that took hours to fade. Avoid liquid cleaners entirely. If dust accumulates deep in the weave, a handheld vacuum on low suction pulls it out without fraying the edges.

Does the wooden frame ever warp or splinter?

Not in my testing, though the wood finish is modest—not expensive-looking. It's functional, not ornate. I didn't notice any warping after three weeks in a non-humid room. If you live somewhere very damp, this might absorb moisture over time. Ensure your wall gets decent air circulation.
