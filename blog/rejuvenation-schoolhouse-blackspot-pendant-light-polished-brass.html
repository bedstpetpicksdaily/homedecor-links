Frequently Asked Questions

How does the polished brass hold up in a kitchen with cooking steam?

According to real owner reviews, the polished brass develops a natural patina over 1-2 years but doesn't tarnish heavily like untreated brass would. Most owners say occasional gentle wiping keeps it looking intentional and vintage rather than grimy. In steamy kitchens, expect a subtle darkening rather than complete discoloration. If you want pristine brass forever, this isn't the fixture—but if you like that aged-metal aesthetic, it's actually a feature.

Is this pendant bright enough for task lighting over a kitchen island?

It depends on the bulb you pair with it. The fixture itself is designed for form and ambient light, not blinding task lighting. Reviewers note that pairing it with a 60-75W equivalent LED bulb gives decent functional light over a counter, but if you need bright task lighting for food prep, you might want to add an additional under-cabinet light source. Don't expect this to be your only lighting solution in a work-heavy kitchen.

How does the price compare to similar Schoolhouse-style pendants on Amazon?

Rejuvenation's version runs premium, but you're primarily paying for their sourcing and design curation. Generic schoolhouse brass pendants on Amazon start around $40-70, while mid-range alternatives run $80-120. The Rejuvenation sits above that, justifying the difference only if the finish quality and construction actually last longer—which the reviews suggest they do. If budget is your primary constraint, test a $60-80 alternative first; if you love the style and want it forever, the Rejuvenation investment makes sense.
