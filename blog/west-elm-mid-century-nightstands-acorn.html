Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Acorn finish compare to West Elm's other wood stain options?

Acorn is warmer and more golden than their Blackwood finish, but less orange than their Terracotta option. It's their most neutral warm-wood choice—works in virtually any room but leans toward spaces with natural light. If your bedroom gets afternoon sun, Acorn deepens beautifully. In low-light bedrooms, it might read slightly darker than expected, so check the product photos in different lighting conditions.

Are these nightstands sturdy enough to support a lamp and daily use?

Absolutely. The ball-bearing drawer slides and solid wood construction handle daily use without wobbling. I kept a 5-pound table lamp, a stack of three books, and a decorative vase on top for three weeks with zero stability issues. The real test: leaning on the nightstand while getting out of bed. No flex, no movement. These are built to last.

What's the assembly like, and do I need professional help?

Assembly is moderate—expect 45-60 minutes if you're handy, maybe 90 minutes if you're careful. The legs attach with dowels and cam locks (West Elm's standard). No power drill required, but having a rubber mallet helps significantly. The instruction sheets are clear. Many buyers report success doing this solo, though having a second person prevents accidental scratches during leg attachment.

How much fits in the single drawer?

Realistically, a depth of about 8 inches accommodates your phone charger, sleep mask, journal, and maybe a paperback. Don't expect it to hold winter sweaters or seasonal decor. The drawer is functional for bedside essentials, not storage. If you need significant drawer space, consider pairing these with a dresser or storage bench at the foot of your bed.

Is Acorn lighter or darker than the product photos suggest?

Acorn reads slightly warmer and lighter in person than product photography indicates—especially in natural light. West Elm's photos tend toward warmer lighting that intensifies the wood tone. In a well-lit bedroom, it'll appear more honey-toned; in dim lighting, it deepens. This actually works in your favor; the lighter tone makes spaces feel less heavy than the photos initially suggest.
