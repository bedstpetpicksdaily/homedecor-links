Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I order personalized gifts from GiftLab?

For summer events, order 2-3 weeks in advance to allow time for personalization and production. Peak season (May-June) may require longer lead times, so earlier ordering is recommended for June and July events.

What are photo flipbooks, and why are they good summer gifts?

Photo flipbooks are compact books containing 10-20 photos that create animation when you flip through pages. They're perfect summer gifts because they're lightweight, travel-friendly, interactive, and create a unique way to share vacation memories or special moments.

Can I customize GiftLab's products for group gifts?

Yes! GiftLab offers customization options for group gifts. You can add multiple photos, names, or messages to items like mugs, photo blankets, or home decor pieces, making them ideal for family reunions, friend group gifts, or team celebrations.

Many GiftLab products are outdoor-friendly, including personalized mugs, beach towels, and insulated drinkware. However, delicate items like photo canvas art are best suited for covered outdoor spaces or indoor display.
