Assess Your Space and Wall Size

Before browsing through Big Wall Decor's impressive collection of large wall art, take time to evaluate the specific wall you want to decorate. Measure your wall dimensions and consider the furniture placement in the room. For larger walls, oversized canvas prints from Big Wall Decor create a dramatic focal point that commands attention without overwhelming the space. Consider the distance from which people will view the art—larger pieces work best in rooms where viewers can step back and appreciate them fully, such as living rooms or master bedrooms.

Consider Your Color Palette and Existing Décor

The most stunning statement wall pieces are those that complement your existing color scheme and furniture. Look at your room's dominant colors, accent colors, and any artwork or decorative elements already present. Big Wall Decor's wide selection of abstract art and living room wall art ranges from vibrant, bold designs to subtle, neutral tones. If your room features warm earth tones, consider their warm abstract collections. For modern, minimalist spaces, their contemporary pieces in blacks, whites, and grays create a sophisticated ambiance. The right framed art or canvas print should tie your room together rather than compete with existing elements.

Choose Your Art Style and Theme

Your personal style should guide your selection of bedroom wall decor or living room wall art. Big Wall Decor specializes in various styles including modern abstract art, geometric patterns, nature-inspired designs, and more. Summer refresh projects often benefit from lighter, airier aesthetic choices. Abstract art pieces can add visual interest and movement to a room, while landscape or botanical designs bring a fresh, outdoorsy feel perfect for the season. Browse Big Wall Decor's curated collections to find pieces that resonate with your aesthetic vision and complement the mood you want to create in your space.

Evaluate Quality and Framing Options

Quality matters when investing in statement wall pieces. Big Wall Decor's oversized canvas prints and framed art are crafted with attention to detail and durability in mind. Consider whether you prefer traditional framed art for a classic look or modern canvas prints for a contemporary feel. Canvas pieces offer versatility and typically work well in casual settings, while framed options add elegance and formality. Think about the finish—matte, glossy, or textured—and how it will interact with your room's lighting and décor style.

Frequently Asked Questions

What size canvas should I choose for my wall?

Measure your wall space and leave adequate margin around the art. A general rule is that wall art should cover about 50-75% of the wall's visual space. For large walls, Big Wall Decor's oversized canvas prints create an impactful statement piece.

How do I know if abstract art will work in my home?

Abstract art is versatile and works well in modern, contemporary, and eclectic spaces. Consider your existing décor colors and style. Big Wall Decor's diverse abstract collection offers options that complement various design aesthetics.

Should I choose framed art or canvas prints?

Both options from Big Wall Decor have merit. Framed art offers a polished, traditional look ideal for formal spaces, while canvas prints provide a modern, casual feel. Choose based on your room's style and personal preference.

How can I ensure my large wall art doesn't look out of place?

Match the art's color palette to your room's existing décor, consider the style compatibility, and ensure proper sizing relative to your wall and furniture. Big Wall Decor's curated collections help simplify this selection process.

Is oversized wall art suitable for small rooms?

Yes! One well-chosen large piece can actually make a small room feel bigger and more sophisticated. The key is proper proportioning—ensure the art isn't so large it overwhelms the space.
