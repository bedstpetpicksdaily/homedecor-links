Frequently Asked Questions

How long do macerating toilets last?

High-quality macerating toilets, particularly Simple Project models, typically last 15-20 years with proper maintenance. The key is avoiding flushing non-biodegradable items and performing routine maintenance as recommended by the manufacturer.

Are macerating toilets sanitary?

Yes, macerating toilets are completely sanitary. The sealed system prevents odors and bacteria from escaping into your bathroom. Simple Project's models include advanced sealing technology and self-cleaning features that maintain hygiene standards.

Can you flush regular toilet paper in a macerating toilet?

Absolutely. Macerating toilets are designed to handle standard toilet paper without issues. However, avoid flushing paper towels, wipes, or other non-standard items, as these can damage the macerating mechanism.

What happens if the power goes out?

Most macerating toilets, including Simple Project models, have a temporary storage capacity in the holding tank. You can still use the toilet for a limited time during power outages, though you'll want to restore power or use an alternative facility if the outage is extended.

How much does a macerating toilet installation cost?

Installation costs vary but typically range from $800-$2,000 for a complete basement bathroom setup with a macerating toilet. This is significantly cheaper than traditional basement plumbing, which can exceed $5,000-$10,000.
