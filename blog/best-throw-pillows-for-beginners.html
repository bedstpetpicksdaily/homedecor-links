What to Look For

  • Neutral Base Colors with One Statement Pillow: As a beginner, stick with 2-3 pillows in soft neutrals (cream, gray, beige) that match your existing furniture, then add one pillow with color or pattern. This approach is forgiving and teaches you about balance without overwhelming your space.
  • Quality Fabric That's Easy to Care For: Look for durable, washable fabrics like cotton blends or performance fabrics that resist staining. Beginners benefit from pillows they can actually clean without worry, making them more practical for learning and experimentation.
  • Proper Sizing for Your Furniture: Measure your sofa or chair before buying. Standard sizes are 18x18" for most couches and 12x20" for accent chairs. Getting the size right prevents your space from looking cluttered or sparse, which is crucial when you're still developing your design eye.

Frequently Asked Questions

What size throw pillows should I buy for my couch?

For most standard couches, 18x18 inch and 20x20 inch pillows work best, with 16x16 inches being ideal for smaller sofas or accent chairs. A good rule of thumb is to choose pillows that are about one-third the width of your seating surface, and mixing two different sizes creates visual interest without overwhelming the space.

How many throw pillows should I put on my couch?

Two to four pillows is the sweet spot for most couches—typically an even number looks more balanced and intentional. Start with two pillows at opposite ends, then add a third in the center or a fourth if your couch is sectional-sized.

What fabric is best for throw pillows that are easy to maintain?

Microfiber, performance fabrics like Sunbrella, and cotton blends are the most durable and easiest to clean for beginners. Avoid delicate fabrics like silk or velvet unless you're willing to hand-wash or dry-clean them regularly, as these require special care.

Should throw pillow covers match my couch color?

No—in fact, complementary or contrasting colors create more visual appeal than matching your couch exactly. Choose pillow colors that coordinate with your existing décor and either echo accent colors in your room or provide subtle contrast while keeping the same undertone as your sofa.
