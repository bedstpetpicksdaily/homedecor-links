Are wooden model kits actually eco-friendly?

Authentic eco-friendly kits use sustainably sourced wood and non-toxic finishes. EWA Eco-Wood-Art specializes in this approach, ensuring their materials are responsibly harvested and their manufacturing processes minimize environmental impact. How long does it take to complete a wooden model kit?

Do wooden model kits require special tools?
