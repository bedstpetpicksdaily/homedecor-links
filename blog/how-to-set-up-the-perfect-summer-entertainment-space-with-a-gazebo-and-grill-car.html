Frequently Asked Questions

What size gazebo is best for a typical backyard?

The ideal gazebo size depends on your yard dimensions and entertaining needs. A 10x10 or 10x12 gazebo works well for smaller yards and intimate gatherings, while 12x16 or larger options suit bigger properties and frequent entertaining. AECOJOY offers various sizes to match any outdoor space.

Should the grill cart be inside or outside the gazebo?

Ideally, position your grill cart at the edge of the gazebo or slightly outside it to minimize heat and smoke exposure under the canopy. Ensure proper ventilation and maintain safe clearance from any overhead structures. AECOJOY's grill carts are designed with these considerations in mind.

What outdoor furniture materials work best for durability?

Look for weather-resistant materials like aluminum frames, high-density polyethylene, and solution-dyed fabrics. AECOJOY's outdoor furniture collections feature these premium materials that withstand UV exposure, moisture, and temperature fluctuations while maintaining their appearance.

How much storage do I need for outdoor entertaining supplies?

Plan for storage that accommodates grilling tools, entertaining accessories, seasonal cushions, and pet supplies. AECOJOY's outdoor storage sheds range from compact units to spacious solutions, allowing you to choose based on your specific needs and available space.
