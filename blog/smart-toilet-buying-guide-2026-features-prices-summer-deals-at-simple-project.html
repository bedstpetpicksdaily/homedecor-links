Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between a smart toilet and a bidet toilet?

Smart toilets incorporate electronic features like app connectivity, heated seats, air drying, and health monitoring. Bidet toilets focus primarily on water-based cleansing functions. Many smart toilets include bidet technology as one feature among many.

Are macerating toilets suitable for permanent installations?

Yes, macerating toilets are designed for permanent use and are ideal for basement bathrooms, additions, or any location where traditional plumbing is impractical. Simple Project's models are backed by manufacturer warranties and professional installation support.

How much can a smart toilet save on water bills?

Smart toilets with dual-flush technology use approximately 1.28 gallons per flush versus 3.5-7 gallons for older models, potentially saving families $100+ annually on water costs.

What should I look for in a bathroom renovation with smart toilet installation?

Consider electrical outlet placement, plumbing accessibility, ventilation for air-drying features, and coordination with other fixtures. Simple Project offers consultation services to optimize bathroom layout and functionality during renovations.
