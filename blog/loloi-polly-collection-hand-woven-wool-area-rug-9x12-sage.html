Frequently Asked Questions

Does this rug work with both modern and traditional decor?

Yes, the sage green and hand-woven texture adapt well to multiple styles. I've seen it work in Scandinavian-inspired rooms, farmhouse spaces, and contemporary interiors. The key is pairing it with complementary throw pillows and curtains—neutral linens or cream-toned accents let the rug anchor the space without competing for attention.

How much maintenance does a wool rug actually require?

Routine vacuuming twice weekly keeps it looking fresh, and I spot-clean spills immediately with a damp cloth and mild detergent. Professional cleaning every 12-18 months extends its life significantly. The wool fibers resist dirt better than synthetic options, so it requires less frequent deep cleaning than you might expect, which saves money over time.

Is this suitable for high-traffic areas like hallways or playrooms?

Absolutely. The moderate pile height and hand-woven construction handle foot traffic well without matting down. I'd hesitate putting it in a mudroom or entryway where wet shoes are constant, but for living rooms, bedrooms, and playrooms that get regular use, it performs reliably. Pair it with a quality rug pad to protect your flooring and prevent slipping.

What's the difference between this and cheaper synthetic alternatives?

Wool breathes better, ages more gracefully, and naturally resists staining because the fiber structure repels liquids. Synthetic rugs mat down faster in high-traffic areas and often develop a plasticky feel over time. This rug costs more upfront but maintains its appearance and feel for years longer, making it a better investment if you plan to keep furniture stable.

Will the sage color fade or change over time?

Wool does fade slightly with direct sunlight exposure, but the sage green in this collection is dyed to resist fading reasonably well. Rotating furniture placement every 6-12 months and using curtains or sheers during peak sun hours keeps color consistent. It hasn't noticeably shifted in my eight months of use, though I don't have western-facing direct sunlight in my placement area.
