What to Look For

1. Material Quality and Weave Pattern

  • Look for hand-woven rattan or water hyacinth (more durable than paper-thin imports)
  • Check if the weave is tight and even—loose weaving means dust accumulation and faster deterioration
  • Verify the rattan is treated for moisture resistance, especially in kitchens with steam from cooking
  • Natural rattan with visible variations is authentic; uniform perfection often indicates lower-quality synthetic materials

2. Size and Spacing for Your Island

  • Measure your kitchen island length first—typically you need 1 pendant per 2-3 feet of counter space
  • Pendant diameter should be 10-14 inches for standard islands; larger islands (8+ feet) need either bigger fixtures or more pendants
  • Check hanging height specifications—you want 30-36 inches from island countertop to fixture bottom for optimal lighting and sightlines
  • Consider if you need a coordinating rod or track system for multiple pendants

3. Light Bulb Compatibility and Brightness

  • Confirm whether fixtures come as plug-in cord sets or require hardwired installation
  • Check for dimmable capabilities—essential for creating that boho ambient feel without full brightness
  • Verify bulb type: LED (energy-efficient), E26/E27 socket compatibility, and wattage recommendations
  • Look for warm white (2700K) color temperature to match the natural boho aesthetic

4. Hardware Finish and Design Details

  • Match metal hardware (brass, black, or brushed gold) to existing kitchen fixtures and faucets
  • Examine the interior dome or fixture design—some allow light diffusion while others create dramatic shadows
  • Check if the fixture has an open top or closed design (open tops offer more light output)
  • Verify the mounting hardware is rated for your ceiling type (drywall anchors, joists, etc.)
