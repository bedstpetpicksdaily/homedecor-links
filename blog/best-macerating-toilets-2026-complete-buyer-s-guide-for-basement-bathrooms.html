What Are Macerating Toilets and Why Choose One?

Macerating toilets are self-contained units that use a grinding mechanism to break down waste before pumping it away. Unlike standard toilets that rely on gravity, these fixtures include an electric motor and pump system housed in the base or behind the unit. This makes them perfect for basement bathrooms where traditional plumbing isn't available or would be cost-prohibitive to install.

The primary advantage is flexibility in bathroom placement. You can install a macerating toilet virtually anywhere in your basement without relocating existing drain lines. Simple Project specializes in these systems with models that combine durability with modern aesthetics, making them an excellent choice for homeowners seeking both function and style in their bathroom renovation projects.

Frequently Asked Questions

How loud are macerating toilets during operation?

Quality macerating toilets like those from Simple Project operate at 70-75 decibels—comparable to a dishwasher. Budget models may be noisier. Sound-dampening technology significantly reduces operational noise, making them suitable for basement use.

Can macerating toilets handle flushable wipes and feminine hygiene products?

No. Macerating toilets are designed for human waste and toilet paper only. Flushable wipes, feminine products, and other items can damage the grinding mechanism and require expensive repairs. Always follow manufacturer guidelines.

What happens to a macerating toilet during a power outage?

Without electricity, the pump cannot operate. For this reason, many users install battery backup systems or generators. Simple Project offers compatible backup solutions to ensure continuous functionality during power interruptions.

How often do macerating toilets require maintenance?

Most macerating toilets need minimal maintenance—typically annual inspections and occasional filter cleaning. Simple Project's units are designed for low-maintenance operation with easily accessible components for routine care.

Are macerating toilets compatible with septic systems?

Yes, quality macerating toilets work with both municipal sewer lines and septic systems. Ensure your unit is specifically rated for septic use. Simple Project offers models suitable for either system, clearly marked in product specifications.
