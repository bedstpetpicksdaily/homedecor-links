What Are Wooden Mechanical Model Kits?

Wooden mechanical model kits are intricate 3D puzzles that combine traditional woodcraft with modern engineering. Unlike standard puzzles, these kits feature moving parts, gears, and mechanical systems that actually function once assembled. They serve dual purposes: providing engaging DIY construction experiences and creating stunning decorative pieces for desks, shelves, and living spaces. STEM models in this category educate builders about physics, mechanics, and engineering principles while delivering the satisfaction of hands-on creation.
