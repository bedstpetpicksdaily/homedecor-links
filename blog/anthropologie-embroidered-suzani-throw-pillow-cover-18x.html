Frequently Asked Questions

Is this cover machine washable, or does it really need hand washing?

Most Suzani embroidered covers can technically go through a delicate machine cycle in cold water, but hand washing extends the life of the embroidery significantly. If machine washing, use a mesh bag and skip the dryer—lay it flat. Given the $10 price, hand washing adds maybe 5 minutes per month of maintenance. Decide if that's worth it before buying.

How does this compare to similar Suzani covers on Amazon for $8-15?

The Anthropologie brand carries some premium pricing, but the 4.4-star rating with 57 reviews suggests better consistency than ultra-budget alternatives with fewer reviews. Check recent reviews on competing covers (especially those with 10+ reviews from the last 3 months) to see if reviewers report fading or poor stitching. Often you'll save $2-3 elsewhere without losing quality, but this one's defensible at $10.

Will the embroidery thread snag or unravel after washing?

Not typically, based on the review data. The solid 4.4 rating suggests the embroidery holds up reasonably well. However, Suzani textiles are inherently delicate—pilling and minor fraying around edges are normal after several washes. This isn't a forever pillow; expect 2-3 years of regular use before visible wear becomes noticeable.

Can I use this with a down insert, or does it need a specific pillow type?

The 18x18 cover fits standard 18-inch inserts (down, polyester, or memory foam). Some reviewers noted that very fluffy down inserts can make the cover feel slightly tight, but it works. If you're buying a new insert, opt for a standard rather than extra-loft version for the best fit.
