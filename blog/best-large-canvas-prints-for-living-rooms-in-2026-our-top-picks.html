Frequently Asked Questions

What size canvas print should I choose for my living room?

Choose a canvas that occupies 50-75% of your wall space. For most living rooms, this ranges from 40x60 inches to 60x80 inches. Big Wall Decor offers sizing guides to help you measure and select the perfect dimensions for your specific space.

Are Big Wall Decor canvas prints easy to hang?

Yes, Big Wall Decor's oversized canvas prints come ready to hang with all necessary hardware included. Most pieces require simple wall mounting, and their customer service provides detailed installation instructions for both framed art and unframed canvas options.

Do large canvas prints work in small living rooms?

Absolutely. Large canvas prints can actually make small spaces feel larger by drawing the eye upward and creating visual interest. Choose lighter colors and abstract designs from Big Wall Decor to maximize this effect in compact living rooms.

How do I care for oversized canvas prints?

Big Wall Decor's museum-grade canvas requires minimal maintenance. Simply dust occasionally with a soft cloth. Avoid direct sunlight and excessive moisture to ensure your statement wall piece lasts for decades.

Can I use Big Wall Decor's canvas prints in bedrooms?

Yes, Big Wall Decor specifically offers bedroom wall decor options. Their collection works beautifully in any room, from living rooms to bedrooms, creating cohesive home décor throughout your space.
