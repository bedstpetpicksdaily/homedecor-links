What Are Macerating Toilets and Why Choose One for Your Basement?

Macerating toilets, also called upflush or pump toilets, feature an integrated grinding mechanism that breaks down waste and toilet paper into fine particles. This innovative technology allows the unit to pump waste upward against gravity, making basement installations possible without extensive excavation or new sewage lines. Traditional gravity-fed toilets require the drain to be below the main sewage line, which is impossible in below-grade spaces. Macerating technology solves this problem elegantly and cost-effectively.

Simple Project's macerating toilet collection combines cutting-edge engineering with elegant design, making them ideal for basement renovations. Types of Basement Toilets: Macerating vs. Standard Solutions

Are macerating toilets from Simple Project quiet?

Yes, modern macerating toilets from Simple Project operate remarkably quietly—typically 75-80 decibels, comparable to a running shower. Older models were noisier, but technological improvements have made contemporary units nearly silent. The grinding cycle lasts only a few seconds per flush.

Do macerating toilets require special maintenance?

Macerating toilets require minimal maintenance beyond regular cleaning. Simple Project units feature durable components designed for long-term reliability. Avoid flushing non-flushable items, as they can damage the grinding mechanism. Annual inspections help ensure optimal performance.
