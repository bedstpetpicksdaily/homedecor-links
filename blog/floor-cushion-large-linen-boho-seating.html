What to Look For

1. Linen Quality and Weave

Not all linen is created equal. Look for 100% pure linen (avoid linen blends if possible) with a thread count of at least 200. The weave matters too—a slightly looser weave is more breathable and authentic boho, while a tighter weave offers more durability. Check product descriptions for weight (measured in GSM); 150-200 GSM is ideal for floor cushions that won't sag quickly.

2. Cushion Fill Quality and Type

The exterior is just half the story. High-quality fills include buckwheat hulls (firm, moldable), shredded memory foam (comfortable, supportive), or high-density foam (durable and long-lasting). Avoid cheap polyester fill that compresses within months. Look for removable, washable inner covers that allow you to replace the fill later—this extends the life of your investment significantly.

3. Size Specifications That Actually Matter

Large floor cushions typically range from 26" to 36" across. Measure your intended space before buying. A 30" cushion works for single lounging, while 36"+ accommodates two people or one very sprawled lounger. Check depth as well—deeper cushions (18"+) are more supportive for extended sitting. Verify the actual filled dimensions, as some cushions compress when sat on.

4. Color and Design Longevity

Neutral, natural linen tones (cream, sand, charcoal, natural) outlast trend-specific colors. These work with any décor update and hide dust/wear better than bright shades. If you want pattern, look for natural dyes or colorfast options. Consider whether the cover is removable and machine-washable—this is crucial for summer-used pieces that'll collect pollen, spills, and dust.
