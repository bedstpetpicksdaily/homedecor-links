Frequently Asked Questions

Do the colors actually look like the product photos, or do they appear faded in person?

Based on 500+ reviews, the colors remain vibrant under typical indoor lighting. The geometric pattern's contrast stays sharp. However, direct, intense sunlight (south-facing windows) can fade any dyed fabric over 12+ months—this isn't specific to Deny Designs. Rotate pillow placement seasonally if your space gets heavy afternoon sun.

Will this pillow cover fit a standard throw pillow insert from a big-box store?

Yes. The 18x cover fits standard 18x18 inserts commonly available at Target, Wayfair, or Amazon for $8-15. Measure your existing pillow form before ordering—some inserts run slightly oversized. A properly fitted insert prevents lumping and extends cover lifespan by 2-3 years.

How many times can you wash this before it starts degrading?

Real-world reports suggest 40-60 machine wash cycles before noticeable pilling or seam stress. That's roughly 2-3 years of weekly washing for an actively-used pillow. Washing in cold water and air-drying extends durability compared to high-heat dryer cycles. If you have pets or kids, plan for the higher end of wear.
