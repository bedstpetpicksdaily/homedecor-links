Frequently Asked Questions

What is a macerating toilet and who needs one?

A macerating toilet features a grinding mechanism that breaks down waste into fine particles, allowing installation without direct access to sewer lines. They're perfect for basement bathrooms, guest houses, and renovation projects where traditional plumbing is impractical. Simple Project specializes in reliable macerating toilet solutions for these challenging installations.

Are smart toilets worth the investment?

Smart toilets offer significant comfort and hygiene benefits through features like adjustable bidet functions, heated seats, and customizable water pressure. They also typically reduce water consumption, offsetting initial costs through long-term savings. Simple Project's smart toilet options provide excellent value for homeowners prioritizing bathroom experience and efficiency.

How do I choose between different smart toilet models?

Consider your budget, desired features (bidet, heating, air-drying), installation requirements, and bathroom layout. If you need a basement bathroom, macerating technology might be essential. Simple Project offers consultation resources and product comparisons to help identify the perfect fixture for your specific needs and renovation goals.

Can smart toilets be installed in any bathroom?

Most smart toilets require electricity and standard plumbing connections. For challenging locations like basements, Simple Project's macerating toilets provide flexible installation options. Consulting with a professional plumber ensures compatibility with your home's existing systems before purchase.

What maintenance do smart toilets require?

Smart toilets generally require minimal maintenance beyond regular cleaning. Simple Project's fixtures feature durable components and self-cleaning functions that reduce upkeep. Following manufacturer guidelines and avoiding harsh chemicals helps ensure long-term performance and reliability of your bathroom investment.
