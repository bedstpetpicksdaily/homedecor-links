Frequently Asked Questions

What are macerating toilets and why are they useful for basement bathrooms?

Macerating toilets use powerful grinding mechanisms to break down waste into a fine slurry before pumping it upward to your main sewer line or septic system. This eliminates the need for traditional gravity-based plumbing, making them perfect for basement bathrooms, powder rooms in unusual locations, or homes with challenging plumbing layouts. Simple Project's macerating toilet systems make adding a bathroom to your basement possible without extensive and expensive plumbing work.

How much can smart bathroom fixtures save on water bills?

Smart bathroom fixtures, particularly sensor-activated faucets and efficient toilets, can reduce water consumption by 20-30% compared to standard fixtures. Over a year, this translates to significant savings for most households. Additionally, smart toilets with dual-flush technology and optimized water pressure settings provide substantial long-term savings while maintaining excellent performance.

Is professional installation necessary for Simple Project's bathroom fixtures?

While many of Simple Project's smart bathroom fixtures are designed for easier installation, professional installation is recommended for optimal performance and warranty coverage. Their macerating toilet systems particularly benefit from expert installation to ensure proper water pressure, electrical connections, and drainage configuration. Many contractors are familiar with Simple Project products and can handle installation efficiently during your fall renovation project.
