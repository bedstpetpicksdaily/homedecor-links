Frequently Asked Questions

Can you really just throw it in a standard washer without damage?

Yes, but follow the rules: use cold water, gentle cycle, mild detergent. The 5x7 size fits in most standard machines, though larger 8x10 versions might require a commercial laundromat washer. Ruggable's backing is specifically engineered for this—it's not experimental. Thousands of the 500+ reviewers confirm this works repeatedly without backing separation or fiber loss.

How does the price compare to renting or buying cheaper washable rugs?

Ruggable costs $400-600 upfront. Budget washable rugs from big-box retailers run $100-250 but shed fibers faster and wear thin after 18-24 months. Over a five-year period, buying three cheap rugs ($750 total) versus one Ruggable ($500) actually makes the Ruggable the smarter math—plus you're not replacing it every couple years.

Does the latte color hide stains better than lighter or darker options?

Medium-tone latte hides general dirt and dust well, but dark stains (red wine, chocolate, pet accidents) will be visible until washed. It's a practical compromise that works across most décor without showing every footprint like white would. Since you can machine wash it, actual staining is less of a permanent problem than with traditional rugs.
