What's the actual brightness level, and is it bright enough for task lighting?

Brightness depends entirely on the bulb you select. The lamp works with standard E26 bulbs up to 60W equivalent (LED). I tested it with a 40W equivalent warm LED (2700K) for ambient reading light and a 60W equivalent for workspace illumination above a console table. It's not a flood of light—it's directional and focused, which is the entire point. For primary task lighting in an office, you'll likely want to pair it with desk lighting. For accent and bedside reading, it's perfectly sufficient.
