Frequently Asked Questions

How much shedding should I expect initially?

Hand-knotted rugs shed noticeably for 2-4 weeks when new—this is the pile settling, not a defect. Vacuum frequently without guilt. After the initial period, shedding should drop dramatically. If heavy shedding continues past month two, the rug has a construction issue and you should contact support. Most owners report this phase passes quickly and isn't the horror show some internet comments suggest.

Does the saffron color fade in direct sunlight?

Wool naturally fades with sun exposure over years, but the hand-dyed saffron has good color stability compared to synthetic dyes. If your 9x12 sits in a south-facing window getting 6+ hours of direct daily sun, expect noticeable fading within 3-4 years. Use UV-filtering curtains if this is your situation. Rotating the rug annually (if furniture arrangement allows) helps even out any gradual color shift.

Is professional cleaning really necessary, or can I just vacuum?

Professional cleaning every 12-18 months is necessary if you want the rug to maintain appearance and structural integrity. Vacuuming alone removes surface dust but doesn't address dirt embedded in hand-knotted wool's deeper fibers. Skipping professional cleaning means you're watching a $600-1200+ investment gradually look tired. For a family with kids or pets, budget for that cleaning cost when you calculate the total cost of ownership.
