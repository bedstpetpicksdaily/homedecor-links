Frequently Asked Questions

Will this work over existing wallpaper or only painted walls?

Stick to painted surfaces only. Applying peel-and-stick grasscloth over existing wallpaper creates two problems: the adhesive won't bond securely to that slick finish, and when you remove it, you risk peeling away the underlying wallpaper. Prime any patched drywall areas with quality primer first, since fresh joint compound is slightly absorbent and can affect adhesion consistency.

How does the Warm Beige look in rooms with different lighting directions?

North-facing rooms will see it as slightly cooler and more gray-leaning, while south and west exposures bring out warmer undertones. East-facing spaces typically show the most balanced version of the color. This isn't a flaw—it's actually why grasscloth textures are superior to flat wallpaper; the weave catches directional light and prevents that 'one color' flatness that makes cheaper options look cheap.

How long can you leave it up before removal becomes difficult?

Eighteen months is the practical limit before adhesive becomes sticky and removal starts requiring heat gun assistance. Most people report clean removal up to 12 months with no problems. If you're renting or testing a look, plan on refreshing it annually or using it seasonally—that's where removable wallpaper truly excels compared to permanent options.

Do I need primer or special wall prep beyond standard paint?

Flat latex paint is ideal; eggshell works fine but glossier finishes reduce adhesion slightly. Don't use primer unless your walls have stains or damage. If you've got wall imperfections, fill and sand them first—grasscloth texture won't hide flaws the way darker solids might. Clean walls with a magic eraser and let them dry completely before application.

What's the honest difference between this and cheaper peel-and-stick grasscloth options?

You're paying for actual woven fiber content versus printed texture on vinyl. Cheaper options photograph identically online but feel synthetic when you touch them—plastic weave instead of natural grasscloth construction. Sunworthy's heritage in traditional wallpaper manufacturing shows in the material quality. If budget is your ceiling, accept that you're getting the look without the tactile experience. At Sunworthy's price point, you're getting genuine construction worth keeping.
