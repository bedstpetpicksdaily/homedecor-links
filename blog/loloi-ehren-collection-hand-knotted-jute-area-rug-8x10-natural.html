Frequently Asked Questions

Is jute really that prone to shedding, and how long does it actually last?

Yes, initial shedding is normal with hand-knotted jute because natural fibers release during the finishing process. Expect heavy shedding for 2-4 weeks, then dramatic improvement. Lifespan depends entirely on traffic and care—in moderate use, 5-7 years is realistic before visible wear. High-traffic areas like entryways will show matting sooner. Daily vacuuming during the first month accelerates the shedding process and reduces the overall fiber loss you'll experience.

How should I clean this rug without damaging it?

Vacuum weekly using a suction-only setting (avoid beater bars, which can pull fibers). For spills, blot immediately with a white cloth and cool water—never rub. For deeper stains, a light spray of vinegar-water solution (1:3 ratio) works for organic spills. Avoid steam cleaning or harsh chemicals; jute doesn't respond well to moisture. Professional cleaning every 12-18 months is recommended if the rug sees heavy use. Rotation every 6-8 months extends life by evening out wear patterns.

Does the natural color work with modern décor, or does it feel too rustic?

The Ehren's warm, neutral tone works across multiple design styles—contemporary, farmhouse, mid-century modern, and minimalist spaces all benefit from it. The key is pairing it with your existing palette: it grounds cooler grays and whites beautifully while complementing warm earth tones and wood furniture. In July lighting, which tends to be bright and warm, the jute's natural undertones actually become more visible and sophisticated. If you're worried about it looking too casual, layer it with structured furniture and crisp textiles; the rug becomes a foundation rather than a focal point.
