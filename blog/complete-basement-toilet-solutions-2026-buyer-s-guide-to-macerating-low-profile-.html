What Are Macerating Toilets and How Do They Work?

Macerating toilets represent a breakthrough in bathroom flexibility, particularly for basement installations where traditional gravity-fed plumbing isn't practical. Unlike conventional toilets that rely on gravity to move waste to your main sewer line, macerating toilets use a motorized grinding mechanism to break down waste into a fine slurry. This processed material can then be pumped upward through small-diameter pipes to your septic system or municipal sewer line, making them perfect for below-grade bathrooms.

Simple Project's macerating toilet collection combines this innovative technology with sleek, modern designs that complement any bathroom aesthetic. Their models feature quiet motors, efficient flushing systems, and user-friendly controls that make them feel like regular toilets despite their advanced engineering. With water-saving features and smart flush technology, Simple Project macerating toilets reduce water consumption by up to 20% compared to standard models.

Low-Profile Toilet Options for Compact Basement Spaces

When ceiling height is limited or you prefer a more traditional approach, low-profile toilets offer an excellent alternative. These specially designed fixtures sit closer to the floor, maximizing headroom in cramped basements while maintaining full flushing power and comfort. Simple Project's low-profile collection includes compact models that don't sacrifice performance for space-saving design.

Low-profile toilets work with existing plumbing layouts more easily than macerating systems, making them ideal for renovations where you want to minimize structural changes. Many Simple Project models feature soft-close seats, water-efficient dual-flush mechanisms, and ceramic construction that ensures durability and easy maintenance. They're particularly popular among homeowners who want modern bathroom functionality without extensive basement modifications.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do macerating toilets require electricity?

Yes, macerating toilets need an electrical outlet to power the grinding mechanism. Most Simple Project models include GFI-protected outlets for safety. This is a minor consideration compared to the plumbing savings you achieve.

How often do macerating toilets need maintenance?

Simple Project macerating toilets typically require minimal maintenance—just avoid flushing non-flushable items and perform basic annual inspections. The motorized systems are designed for longevity and reliability with proper use.

Can low-profile toilets be installed in any basement?

Low-profile toilets work with existing plumbing connections, making them versatile for most basements. However, they still require proper drainage to your main sewer line. Simple Project specialists can assess your space to recommend the best solution.

Are basement toilets from Simple Project water-efficient?

Absolutely. All Simple Project basement toilet models meet or exceed EPA WaterSense standards, using 1.28 GPF or less while maintaining powerful flushing performance—helping you conserve water and reduce utility costs.

What's the typical cost difference between macerating and low-profile toilets?

Macerating toilets typically cost $800-$2,000 installed, while low-profile models range from $300-$800. However, factor in plumbing savings—macerating systems often cost less overall when you consider eliminated structural modifications.
