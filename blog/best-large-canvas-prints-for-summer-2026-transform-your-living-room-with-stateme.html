Frequently Asked Questions

What size large canvas print should I choose for my living room?

For most living rooms, canvas prints ranging from 40x60 inches to 60x90 inches work best as statement wall pieces. Big Wall Decor offers a range of sizes, so measure your wall space and consider viewing distance to select the perfect oversized canvas print. Larger walls can accommodate bigger pieces, while smaller spaces work well with medium-sized big wall decor.

Are Big Wall Decor canvas prints ready to hang?

Yes, Big Wall Decor offers both framed and unframed large canvas prints. Many of their statement wall pieces come pre-stretched and ready to hang, making installation simple and straightforward. Check product details for specific hanging options available for each oversized canvas print.

How do I care for and maintain large canvas prints?

Large canvas prints from Big Wall Decor are designed for durability and easy maintenance. Dust them gently with a soft, dry cloth regularly. Avoid direct sunlight to prevent fading of the vibrant colors in your abstract art or bedroom wall decor. Keep them away from moisture and extreme temperature changes for longevity.

Can I use abstract art canvas prints in a minimalist bedroom?

Absolutely! Big Wall Decor's collection includes abstract art that perfectly complements minimalist aesthetics. Choose pieces with neutral color palettes or simple geometric designs that maintain the clean, uncluttered look while adding visual interest to your bedroom wall art.

What is the best placement for oversized canvas prints?

Large canvas prints work best as focal points above sofas, beds, or on feature walls. Big Wall Decor's statement wall pieces should be positioned at eye level or slightly higher for optimal visual impact. Ensure adequate wall space and consider room traffic patterns when selecting placement for your big wall decor.
