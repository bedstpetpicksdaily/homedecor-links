Frequently Asked Questions

How much does the Loloi Gathered 9x12 actually shed?

Initial shedding is normal for hand-woven wool rugs and typically subsides significantly after 3-4 weeks of regular vacuuming. This isn't a defect—it's loose fibers from the weaving process working themselves out. After the initial period, shedding drops dramatically. A quality vacuum or broom handles it easily.

Will the rust color fade in sunlight or look dingy after a year?

Rust is actually one of the most color-stable choices in the wool world because it's an earthy tone that hides aging well. You won't see dramatic fading like you might with brighter reds or lighter neutrals. The hand-woven nature means variations in the weave create natural depth that masks minor color changes over time.

Is a 9x12 rug big enough for a standard living room?

For most living rooms, 9x12 is the sweet spot. If your seating arrangement is roughly 10x14 feet or smaller, the rug will anchor it properly with furniture legs either on or near the rug. In larger rooms, you might want to go bigger, but 9x12 works well for medium-sized spaces and creates better visual balance than undersizing.

Can you use this rug over radiant floor heating or in humid climates?

Wool handles moisture well and is suitable for most climates, but radiant heating can cause wool to dry out and potentially shift slightly. Use a quality rug pad with moisture barrier, and avoid placing it directly over heat vents. In humid climates, wool actually performs better than synthetic options because it naturally regulates moisture without trapping it.

What kind of pad do I need, and does it affect the price?

A non-slip, moisture-barrier rug pad is essential and typically runs $30-60 depending on size. It prevents shifting, protects your floor, and actually extends the rug's life by reducing wear on the base. Consider it part of the investment—a good pad prevents more damage than its cost.
