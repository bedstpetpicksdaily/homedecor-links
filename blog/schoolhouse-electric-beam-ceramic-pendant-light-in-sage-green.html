Budget ceramic pendants ($30-60) use thinner ceramic, less refined glazing, and standard production techniques. They're fine for rental spaces or experimental installations. What bulb type and wattage works best?

Ceramic handles heat well, so you have flexibility here. A warm white LED bulb (2700K) in 60-75 watt equivalent gives you soft ambient light without feeling clinical. Avoid anything above 100-watt equivalent unless you specifically want bright task lighting. The ceramic diffuses light nicely, so you don't need an opal or frosted bulb—clear or standard finishes work fine and show off the fixture's silhouette better.
