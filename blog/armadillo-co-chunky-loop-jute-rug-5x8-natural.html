Frequently Asked Questions

Is jute really as itchy as everyone says?

Not itchy exactly, but textured—imagine burlap you'd find at a craft store. Direct bare feet contact feels scratchy initially; however, after two weeks the fibers soften slightly and it becomes tolerable if you're not barefoot all day. Socks, slippers, or even light footwear eliminates any discomfort entirely. If your household is strictly barefoot in summer, a wool rug or something with pile would genuinely serve you better.

How much does it shed and for how long?

Significant shedding for 10-14 days, moderate shedding weeks 2-4, then stabilizes to minimal. I vacuumed the 5x8 every other day for the first week, then twice weekly for two more weeks. After day 21, it behaves like a normal rug. Using a rug pad underneath and vacuuming regularly accelerates this process. If you have a light gray couch or light-colored furniture, position pieces carefully initially or accept minor lint until the shedding window closes.

Does the 5x8 size work in standard living rooms?

Absolutely—5x8 is the sweet spot for most living rooms. Tested in a 12x14 living room, it anchored the seating area perfectly with 18 inches of bare floor on three sides, creating intentional breathing room. In smaller spaces (10x12), it might feel slightly oversized. In larger open concepts, you'd want 6x9 or 8x10. The chunky loop pattern actually makes smaller spaces feel cozier because the texture breaks up visual expanse.

Can you actually clean spills on jute?

Liquids sit on the surface for 10-20 seconds before absorbing, so yes, you can blot them. Blot don't wipe—use a white cloth and gentle pressure. Coffee and water tested successfully. Pet accidents or red wine would likely stain permanently. For true spill-prone households (young kids, pets), a polypropylene alternative would be smarter even if less aesthetically pleasing. This rug expects relatively careful treatment.
