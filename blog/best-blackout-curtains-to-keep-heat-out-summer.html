What to Look For

1. Triple-Layer Construction with Thermal Backing

Look for curtains specifically labeled "thermal insulated" with three distinct layers: a blackout fabric front, a middle insulating layer, and a protective backing. This construction is what actually stops heat from penetrating your windows. Single or double-layer blackout curtains won't deliver the same cooling performance.

2. Dense Fabric Weight (Minimum 10 oz per linear yard)

Heavier isn't always better, but in this case, fabric weight directly correlates with heat-blocking capability. Curtains weighing at least 10 ounces per linear yard have enough density to create an effective thermal barrier. Check product specifications—reputable brands always list this detail.

3. 100% Light Blockage Rating

Don't settle for "99%" or "room-darkening." True blackout curtains block 100% of outside light and heat. This matters because even minimal light penetration means some infrared heat is getting through. Only fully opaque curtains deliver complete thermal protection.

4. Grommet or Rod Pocket Installation with Proper Sealing

The best curtains are useless if heat sneaks around the edges. Look for options with deep rod pockets that fit snugly or reinforced grommets that hang close to the wall. Some premium options come with side rails or velcro strips to seal gaps—this feature is worth the extra cost.
