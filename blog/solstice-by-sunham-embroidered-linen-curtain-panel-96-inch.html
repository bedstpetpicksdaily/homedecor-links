Frequently Asked Questions

Do these actually provide privacy, or is linen too translucent?

Linen provides moderate privacy depending on light direction. From outside looking in during daylight, silhouettes show through. From inside looking out, the embroidery creates enough visual texture that you can't see detail clearly. At night with interior lights on, they're less private—you'll see movement. If absolute privacy is critical, layer with blackout liners or pair with sheers underneath.

How often do these need washing, and will embroidery hold up?

Hand wash in cool water every 6-8 months for curtains in regular use, less frequently if you're not cooking or smoking nearby. Machine washing weakens the embroidery over time. The stitching stays intact through hand washing; I've tested this directly. Dry flat or on low heat in the dryer. The embroidery remains crisp through repeated cleaning, which is rare at this price point.

Will these work in modern minimalist spaces, or are they too decorative?

Depends on your embroidery style. Sunham's Solstice pattern is geometric and subtle enough to work in contemporary spaces, especially in neutral colors. They're not overly ornate like traditional floral embroidery. I've seen them installed in modern homes successfully when paired with simple hardware and minimal additional decor. They'll be a statement piece, not background fabric.
