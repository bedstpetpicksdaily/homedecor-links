Smart Toilets and Macerating Solutions for Modern Bathrooms

Simple Project's smart toilet technology represents the future of bathroom functionality. These fixtures offer water-saving capabilities, touchless operation, and customizable comfort settings that elevate your daily bathroom experience. Frequently Asked Questions

What is a macerating toilet and when would I need one?

A macerating toilet uses a pump to grind waste into a fine slurry that travels through small-diameter pipes to your main sewage line. You'd need one for basement bathrooms, additions, or any location where traditional plumbing connections aren't practical. Simple Project specializes in macerating systems that don't sacrifice style or functionality.

Are smart toilets worth the investment?

Yes, smart toilets from Simple Project offer significant benefits including water conservation, enhanced hygiene with bidet features, heated seating, and long-term durability. They appeal to modern homeowners seeking convenience and sustainability, plus they can increase your home's resale value.

How do I choose the right bathroom fixtures for my renovation?

Start with your must-haves and your budget, then consider your space layout and style preferences. Simple Project's comprehensive range allows you to mix and match fixtures that coordinate beautifully while meeting your functional needs. Their expert team can help guide your selections for any project type.

Can I install macerating toilet systems in existing bathrooms?

Yes, macerating systems are excellent retrofit options for existing bathrooms or homes where adding traditional plumbing would be costly. Simple Project's systems are designed for flexible installation, making basement and unconventional bathroom projects much more feasible.
