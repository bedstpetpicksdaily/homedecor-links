How does this hand-knotted rug compare to machine-made wool rugs in the same size?

Hand-knotted rugs are built knot-by-knot, making them significantly more durable and resistant to pile flattening over years of use. Machine-made alternatives wear faster and show footprints more visibly, but cost 50-60% less upfront. For a rental or temporary space, machine-made wins. For keeping the rug 10+ years, hand-knotted wins on value-per-year calculations.
