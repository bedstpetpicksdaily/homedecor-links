Frequently Asked Questions

What size canvas print is considered 'oversized' for living rooms?

Oversized canvas prints typically measure 40 inches or larger in at least one dimension. For most living rooms, prints ranging from 40x60 inches to 60x90 inches work best, though size depends on your wall space and room dimensions. Big Wall Decor offers sizing guides to help you choose the perfect fit for your specific space.

How do I choose the right oversized canvas print for my living room décor?

Consider your existing color palette, furniture style, and the mood you want to create. Abstract art works well in contemporary spaces, while landscape prints suit traditional and modern homes alike. Visit Big Wall Decor to browse collections organized by style and color theme to find your perfect match.

Are oversized canvas prints from Big Wall Decor ready to hang?
