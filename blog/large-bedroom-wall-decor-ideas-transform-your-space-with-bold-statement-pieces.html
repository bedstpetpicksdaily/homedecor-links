Why Large Wall Art Works Best for Bedrooms

When it comes to bedroom design, size matters. Large wall art creates visual interest and draws the eye upward, making your bedroom feel more spacious and intentional. Unlike small framed prints scattered across a wall, oversized canvas prints and statement wall pieces create a focal point that anchors the entire room's design. They're particularly effective above the bed, where they can frame your headboard and create an instant design statement.

Big Wall Decor offers an impressive selection of large canvas prints and abstract art pieces that are specifically sized to make maximum impact. Their oversized artwork ranges from bold, colorful abstracts to sophisticated black-and-white pieces, ensuring you'll find something that complements your bedroom's color palette and personal style.

Bold Statement Pieces That Transform Bedroom Walls

Abstract art is one of the most popular choices for large bedroom wall decor because it's versatile and works with virtually any interior design style. A single oversized abstract canvas can serve as the room's color inspiration, pulling hues into your bedding, pillows, and accent pieces. Big Wall Decor's collection of abstract wall art includes vibrant geometric designs, flowing watercolor-inspired pieces, and contemporary compositions that add sophistication and creativity to any bedroom.

Beyond abstract art, consider statement pieces that reflect your interests and personality. Large landscape paintings, botanical prints, or minimalist line art can all create stunning focal points. The key is selecting artwork large enough to be seen and appreciated from your bed—typically 36 inches or larger for walls above standard headboards.

How to Choose and Arrange Your Large Bedroom Wall Decor

When selecting large bedroom wall art, consider your room's color scheme first. Do you want your artwork to complement existing colors or introduce new ones? Big Wall Decor's framed art and canvas prints come in countless styles, making it easy to find pieces that either harmonize with your current décor or create a bold contrast.

Placement is equally important. For maximum impact, position your large statement piece at eye level when standing, or slightly higher if hanging above furniture. Most designers recommend 57-60 inches from the floor to the center of the artwork. For a cohesive look, you can pair one large statement piece with smaller complementary artwork, or create a gallery wall with multiple large prints for added drama. Whatever your choice, Big Wall Decor's collection of oversized wall decor makes it simple to find pieces that fit your vision and budget.

Frequently Asked Questions

What size should large bedroom wall art be?

For bedrooms, large wall art should typically be 36-60 inches in width, depending on your wall size and furniture placement. Above a standard bed, a 48-60 inch canvas creates the most impactful statement. Big Wall Decor offers multiple size options to fit any bedroom wall.

How do I hang oversized canvas prints safely?

Use quality wall anchors or studs appropriate for your wall type. For very large pieces over 50 inches, mount into wall studs for maximum security. Many oversized canvas prints from Big Wall Decor come with hanging hardware, and professional installation is recommended for large statement pieces.

Does abstract art work in traditional bedrooms?

Absolutely! Abstract art is incredibly versatile. Choose muted, sophisticated abstract pieces in neutral tones for traditional spaces, or bold, colorful abstracts for modern rooms. Big Wall Decor's range of abstract art styles means you can find pieces that complement any bedroom design aesthetic.

Should my bedroom wall art match my bedding colors?

Not necessarily. Many designers recommend choosing artwork first, then building your color scheme around it. Alternatively, select art that complements (rather than exactly matches) your bedding for a curated, intentional look. Big Wall Decor's diverse palette makes it easy to coordinate.

Can I use framed art as bedroom wall decor?

Yes! Framed art adds sophistication and protection to your wall pieces. Big Wall Decor offers both framed and unframed oversized artwork, giving you flexibility in how you display your statement pieces.
