Smart toilets represent one of the most impactful upgrades you can make to your bathroom. Simple Project's smart toilet collection brings premium features to an accessible price point, allowing homeowners to experience luxury bathroom upgrades without luxury-level costs. Many models include water-saving technology, reducing your household's environmental footprint while lowering utility bills over time.

Macerating Toilets: Perfect for Basement Bathrooms

If you're planning to add a bathroom to your basement or other challenging locations, macerating toilets from Simple Project are game-changers. Unlike traditional toilets that require extensive plumbing modifications, macerating toilets grind waste and can pump it to main drain lines through small-diameter pipes. This makes them ideal for basement bathroom renovations where traditional toilet installation would be costly and disruptive. They're also excellent for powder rooms, guest bathrooms, and creative remodeling projects where standard plumbing access is limited.
