What to Look For

1. Material Quality and Weave Density

Not all seagrass is created equal. Look for baskets with tight, consistent weaving that feels sturdy when you press on the sides. Higher-quality seagrass has a finer weave, which means better durability and a cleaner appearance. Check product photos closely for loose threads or gaps in the weave pattern—these are signs of lower quality.

2. Size Appropriateness for Your Space

Measure your storage area before purchasing. Seagrass baskets come in sizes ranging from small desktop organizers (8 inches) to large floor-standing options (18+ inches). Consider what you're storing—blankets require deeper baskets, while shelf organization benefits from wider, shallow options. Most quality sets include multiple sizes, which is more cost-effective than buying individually.

3. Handle Construction and Reinforcement

If you need to move your baskets frequently, handle quality matters significantly. Reinforced handles with extra weaving at stress points will last years longer than flimsy alternatives. Test the handles in product reviews—look for comments mentioning durability and whether they hold up to regular use with heavy items.

4. Linings and Interior Finishes

Premium baskets often feature cloth linings that prevent items from catching on rough seagrass fibers. Check whether the basket comes pre-lined or if you'd need to add your own. Waterproof or water-resistant linings are crucial for bathrooms and entryways where moisture exposure is higher.
