Skip the Outdated: What Interior Designers Say You Should Avoid in 2026

As we move into 2026, interior design trends are shifting away from the maximalist excess and trendy-for-trend's-sake decor that dominated recent years. If you're planning to refresh your space this year, it helps to know which popular styles designers are officially calling dated—and what timeless alternatives will serve your home better.

Why This Matters

Chasing every new trend is expensive, time-consuming, and leaves your home feeling dated within months. Understanding which styles are falling out of favor helps you make smarter purchasing decisions and invest in pieces that will last. When you know what to avoid, you can confidently choose decor that feels current without being trendy, and that actually reflects your personal style rather than what Instagram told you to buy.

What Home Decorating Enthusiasts Should Know

Designers are moving away from:

  • Overly bright, saturated accent walls — Instead, opt for subtle color blocking or soft, muted tones that create depth without overwhelming a room
  • Novelty or gimmick furniture — Skip items designed purely for social media appeal and choose pieces with function and longevity in mind
  • Matchy-matchy decor sets — Mix textures, eras, and finishes intentionally for a curated, collected-over-time look
  • Fast-fashion home decor — Invest in quality basics that won't fall apart or look cheap within a season
  • Overly themed rooms — Balance thematic elements with neutral pieces so your space doesn't feel costume-like
  • Extreme minimalism without warmth — Add texture, greenery, and personal touches to prevent your space from feeling cold and sterile
  • Logo-heavy branding in decor — Choose subtle, timeless designs over pieces that scream a specific brand or trend

The common thread? Smart decorators are choosing quality, intentionality, and personal style over trend-chasing and novelty.
