Understanding the Right Size for Your Bedroom Wall Decor

One of the most critical factors when selecting big wall decor is getting the size right. The scale of your artwork should be proportional to both your wall space and bedroom furniture. A common rule of thumb is to choose wall art that takes up about 60-75% of your available wall space above furniture like a dresser or bed. Oversized canvas prints work particularly well in larger bedrooms, while medium-sized framed art suits more compact spaces. Big Wall Decor offers a wide range of sizes, making it easy to find statement wall pieces that fit your specific dimensions and create the perfect visual balance in your room.

Selecting the Perfect Design Style and Color Palette

Your bedroom wall decor should reflect your personal style and complement your existing décor. Consider whether you want bold, eye-catching abstract art or something more subtle and serene. Large wall art that features calming colors like soft blues, greens, and neutrals can promote relaxation—ideal for a bedroom setting. Alternatively, if you prefer a more vibrant space, statement wall pieces with rich jewel tones or warm hues can add energy and personality. Big Wall Decor's extensive collection includes abstract art designs, minimalist pieces, and contemporary styles, giving you plenty of options to match your aesthetic preferences and bedroom color scheme.

Material Quality and Framing Options

The quality of your wall art directly impacts its longevity and visual appeal. When shopping for bedroom wall decor, look for premium canvas prints made from high-quality materials that resist fading and maintain their vibrancy over time. Framed art options provide a polished, gallery-like appearance, while unframed canvas pieces offer a more modern, casual feel. Big Wall Decor specializes in durable oversized canvas prints and framed art that are crafted to last, ensuring your investment in wall decor will continue to beautify your bedroom for years to come.

Installation and Wall Placement Considerations

Before making your purchase, consider the wall you want to decorate and how your big wall decor will be installed. Measure your space carefully, check for optimal lighting, and think about the viewing angle from your bed. Large wall art should be positioned at eye level when sitting down for best visual impact. Most statement wall pieces from Big Wall Decor come with everything you need for easy installation, and their customer service team can provide guidance on proper placement to ensure your bedroom wall decor looks flawless.

Frequently Asked Questions

What size wall art should I choose for my bedroom?

The ideal size depends on your wall space and furniture. Generally, aim for wall art that covers 60-75% of the wall above your bed or dresser. Big Wall Decor offers various sizes, from medium to oversized canvas prints, to fit any bedroom dimension.

Should I choose framed art or canvas prints for my bedroom?

Both options work well for bedroom wall decor. Framed art offers a polished, gallery look, while canvas prints provide a modern, casual aesthetic. Big Wall Decor specializes in both, so you can choose based on your personal preference and existing décor style.

What colors work best for calming bedroom wall decor?

Soft, cool tones like blues, greens, purples, and grays are ideal for promoting relaxation in a bedroom. Big Wall Decor's collection includes abstract art and statement wall pieces featuring these calming colors, as well as warmer tones if you prefer a more energetic space.

How do I ensure my large wall art is properly installed?
