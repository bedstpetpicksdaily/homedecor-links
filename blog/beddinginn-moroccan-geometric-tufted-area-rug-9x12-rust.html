Frequently Asked Questions

How much shedding should I expect, and how long does it last?

Hand-tufted Moroccan rugs shed deliberately for the first 3-4 weeks as loose fibers release from the construction process. Expect to vacuum 2-3 times weekly during this period. By week 5-6, shedding drops to normal levels (basically what you'd expect from any quality rug). This is completely normal and not a sign of defect—it's actually proof the rug is hand-knotted rather than glued.

Will the Rust color fade in direct sunlight?

Rust-based dyes are generally colorfast, but all rugs fade with prolonged UV exposure—it's physics, not a product flaw. The 9x12 size means it likely covers most of your living room floor, so direct sunlight will hit different sections at different times of day. Rotating the rug quarterly (a good practice anyway) dramatically slows visible fading. Interior designers typically recommend sheer curtains in sunny rooms as additional UV protection for any rug investment.

Is this rug suitable for high-traffic areas, or does it work better in living rooms?

The hand-tufted construction makes it surprisingly durable for traffic. That said, 9x12 rugs in hallways or entryways see concentrated foot traffic in specific paths, which can accelerate wear. This size genuinely thrives as a living room foundation rug or bedroom statement piece where traffic is distributed. If you need something for a high-traffic entry, consider a smaller geometric runner (5x8 or smaller) instead—it's more practical for that application.
