What to Look For

Material honesty over styled photos

  • Look for stated materials: stoneware, glazed ceramic, solid mango wood, iron, 100% cotton or linen.
  • Be cautious with vague terms like "premium resin composite" or "wood-effect" — these often photograph far better than they arrive.
  • Check dimensions in inches. Trending vases and mirrors are frequently smaller in person than the styled shot suggests.

Shipping timelines and origin

  • Look for a clearly published processing time plus transit time. Anything over 14 days usually signals overseas fulfillment.
  • Fragile decor (glass, ceramic, mirrors) shipped long-distance has a higher breakage rate — check whether replacements are covered.

A real, findable return policy

  • Find the returns page before adding to cart. Look for a window (30 days is standard), who pays return shipping, and a physical return address.
  • "Store credit only" or "buyer pays international return shipping" effectively means final sale on decor.

Payment protection and contact info

  • Prefer credit card or a payment processor with dispute protection over direct bank transfers.
  • Look for a working email, responsive chat, and a business address. No contact details is the single biggest red flag in trending-decor shopping.
