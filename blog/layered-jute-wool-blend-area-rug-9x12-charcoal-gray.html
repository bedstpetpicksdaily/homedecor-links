Will this rug work in a high-traffic entryway or main living space?

Yes, but with caveats. The wool component makes it durable for general use, and jute is naturally stain-resistant. However, entryways with heavy moisture (wet shoes, tracked-in rain) are harder on natural fibers than dry living areas. If moisture is a concern, place a mat at entry points to protect the rug itself.

Is charcoal gray too dark for a smaller room, or does it work everywhere?

Charcoal gray actually opens up smaller spaces because it doesn't compete visually the way lighter neutrals sometimes do. It reads as anchored and intentional rather than cramped. The layered texture adds visual interest that prevents the color from feeling flat or depressing. I'd use it confidently in rooms under 200 square feet.

How do I clean and maintain a jute-wool blend rug?

Vacuum weekly with a brush attachment—jute can unravel with beater bar attachment. Blot spills immediately with cool water and a cloth; don't scrub. Professional cleaning every 12-18 months extends life significantly. Avoid direct sunlight in one spot or the charcoal may fade unevenly. Rotate the rug quarterly for even wear.
