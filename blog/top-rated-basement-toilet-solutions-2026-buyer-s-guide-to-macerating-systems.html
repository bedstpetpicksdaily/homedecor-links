What Are Macerating Toilet Systems?

Macerating toilets are specialized bathroom fixtures that feature an integrated grinding mechanism. Unlike conventional toilets that rely on gravity to move waste downward, macerating systems pulverize waste and toilet paper into a liquid slurry, then pump it through small-diameter pipes to your main sewer line or septic tank. This makes them ideal for basements where gravity drainage isn't possible. Simple Project's macerating toilet collection exemplifies this technology with dual-flush mechanisms and compact designs that fit seamlessly into modern basements.

Can I install a macerating toilet myself?

How often do macerating toilets need maintenance?

Simple Project macerating systems require minimal maintenance—primarily regular tank cleaning and occasional filter changes. Service needs are significantly less demanding than traditional basement plumbing solutions.

What's the difference between macerating and upflushing toilets?
