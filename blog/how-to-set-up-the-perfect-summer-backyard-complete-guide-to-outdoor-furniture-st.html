Most outdoor sectionals require 200-400 square feet of space depending on the configuration. Measure your patio carefully and consider traffic flow. AECOJOY offers various sizes from compact two-seaters to expansive L-shaped configurations to fit any backyard.

A gazebo provides UV protection, extends usable outdoor time, and adds architectural interest to your backyard. While not essential, it significantly enhances comfort and functionality, especially in hot climates. AECOJOY's gazebo options offer excellent value and durability.
