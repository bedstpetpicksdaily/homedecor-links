This guide will help you select pillows that are both beautiful and genuinely functional for everyday living.

What to Look For

  • Removable, machine-washable covers with quality zippers: Look for covers that unzip completely from the insert, and verify they're safe for machine washing in cold water. Check the product description carefully—some brands claim "washable" but actually require hand-washing only, which defeats the purpose. Quality zippers won't jam or break after multiple uses, so look for heavy-duty metal or reinforced plastic zippers that are clearly labeled as durable.
  • Fabric that resists staining and fading: Performance fabrics like solution-dyed polyester, microfiber, and cotton blends are your best friends. These materials are specifically engineered to resist spills, fading from sunlight, and pilling from regular use. Avoid delicate fabrics like silk, linen blends, or loosely woven materials that look beautiful in the showroom but stain easily and deteriorate quickly with washing.
  • Neutral or pattern-based colors that hide imperfections: While white and cream pillows look stunning in magazines, they show every fingerprint and coffee splash. Choose charcoal, gray, navy, or patterns with darker tones that maintain their appearance between washes. If you love lighter colors, opt for patterns that incorporate whites with darker accent colors rather than solid light shades.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What fabric is best for washable throw pillows on a couch?

    Look for performance fabrics like microfiber, cotton blends, or polyester that are labeled machine-washable and fade-resistant. Avoid delicate fabrics like silk or linen unless you're willing to hand-wash them, and choose darker colors or patterns if your couch gets heavy use, as they hide stains better than light solids.

    Can you wash throw pillow covers without removing them?

    Most quality throw pillow covers have removable, zippered covers that should be washed separately from the insert for best results. If the cover is permanently attached, you'll need to wash the entire pillow on a gentle cycle in cold water, which can damage the insert over time—so removable covers are always the better choice.

    How often should I wash my couch throw pillows?

    Wash throw pillows every 2-4 weeks if you use your couch daily, or monthly for lighter use, depending on your household's activity level and pet/child exposure. Spot-clean stains immediately and fluff pillows weekly to extend the time between full washes and maintain their shape.

    What size throw pillow is best for a standard couch?

    For most couches, 18x18 inch and 20x20 inch pillows are standard and provide good visual balance without overwhelming seating space. If you have a large sectional or extra-deep sofa, 22x22 inches or larger works better, while smaller apartments benefit from 16x16 inch pillows to avoid a cluttered look.
