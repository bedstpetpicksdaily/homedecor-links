Creating the perfect outdoor living space starts with choosing the right furniture. Frequently Asked Questions

What outdoor sectional materials are most weather-resistant?

The most weather-resistant materials include powder-coated aluminum frames, synthetic wicker, and solution-dyed acrylic fabrics. AECOJOY uses premium combinations of these materials to ensure maximum durability and minimal maintenance requirements for outdoor environments.

How do I choose the right size sectional for my patio?

Measure your available space and plan furniture placement, ensuring comfortable traffic flow. A sectional should occupy roughly 50-75% of your patio area without overwhelming the space. AECOJOY offers various sizes and modular options to accommodate different dimensions perfectly.

Are outdoor sectional cushions removable and washable?

Many quality outdoor sectionals, including those from AECOJOY, feature removable, washable cushion covers made from water-resistant fabrics. This allows for easy cleaning and maintenance, extending the lifespan of your furniture.
