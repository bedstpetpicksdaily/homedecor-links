What is a Macerating Toilet?

A macerating toilet is a modern bathroom fixture that grinds waste and toilet paper into a fine slurry before pumping it through small-diameter pipes to your main sewage line or septic system. Unlike traditional toilets that rely on gravity and large diameter pipes, macerating toilets use a powerful motor and pump system to handle waste management efficiently. Simple Project's macerating toilets feature advanced engineering that makes them ideal for basement toilets, guest bathrooms in unconventional locations, and bathroom renovations where traditional plumbing isn't feasible.

The technology behind macerating toilets allows them to pump waste upward, sideways, or over long distances, making them perfect for split-level homes, basements below the main sewer line, or additions far from existing plumbing. This flexibility opens up endless possibilities for bathroom placement during your renovation project.

How Traditional Toilets Work

Traditional gravity-fed toilets have remained virtually unchanged for decades. When you flush, water from the tank fills the bowl and creates a siphon effect that pushes waste through a large S-trap and into your home's main drain line. These toilets require proper slope toward the drain and larger diameter pipes (typically 3-4 inches) to function correctly. While reliable and low-maintenance, traditional toilets offer limited installation flexibility and may complicate basement bathroom renovation plans.

Installation Flexibility and Space Considerations

One of the biggest advantages of macerating toilets is their installation flexibility. Simple Project's bathroom fixtures can be installed virtually anywhere in your home, regardless of proximity to existing plumbing. The small-diameter discharge pipes (typically ½ to ¾ inch) can run through walls, under floors, or across spaces that would be impossible for traditional plumbing. This makes macerating toilets the perfect solution for basement toilet installation, guest bathrooms in finished basements, or any bathroom renovation where relocating plumbing would be prohibitively expensive. Traditional toilets, by contrast, must be positioned directly above the main drain line with proper slope, severely limiting placement options in your bathroom renovation project.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a macerating toilet be installed in any basement?

Yes, macerating toilets can be installed in virtually any basement location since they pump waste to the main line rather than relying on gravity. Simple Project's basement toilet solutions are designed to handle this installation with ease, making them ideal for finished basements and renovations.

Do macerating toilets from Simple Project require special maintenance?

Macerating toilets require more maintenance than traditional toilets, primarily flushing only toilet paper and human waste—no other materials should be flushed. Simple Project provides comprehensive guides and support to ensure your smart toilet or macerating fixture operates smoothly for years.

Are macerating toilets more expensive than traditional toilets?

While macerating toilets have a higher upfront cost, they often save money during installation by eliminating expensive plumbing work. For bathroom renovations involving basement toilets or unconventional spaces, a macerating toilet from Simple Project is typically more cost-effective than relocating traditional plumbing.

What happens during a power outage with a macerating toilet?

Most macerating toilets, including Simple Project's models, have a temporary manual override option. However, if power is extended, you may need to use alternative bathroom facilities until electricity is restored.

Can Simple Project's macerating toilets handle the needs of a large family?

Absolutely. Simple Project offers commercial-grade macerating and smart toilet options designed for heavy use. Modern macerating toilets are engineered to handle multiple flushes throughout the day without performance issues.
