Frequently Asked Questions

How does the aged brass finish hold up in humid bathrooms or kitchens?

Schoolhouse Electric uses a sealed brass finish that resists patina-ing too aggressively in damp environments. It'll still develop subtle character, but won't corrode or turn green like unsealed brass. That said, direct steam (like above a shower) can accelerate tarnishing. If you're installing this over a range, ensure proper ventilation. The smoke glass handles humidity fine—the real maintenance is dusting.

What's the actual light output, and will one pendant be enough for a dining table?

This pendant works best as ambient or accent lighting paired with another fixture, not as a solo task light. One Karman over a standard dining table will create a moody, intimate atmosphere but won't provide practical work lighting. Designers typically use two or three Karmans clustered, or pair it with overhead recessed lights. If you need it to actually illuminate a task, pair it with directional lighting elsewhere.
