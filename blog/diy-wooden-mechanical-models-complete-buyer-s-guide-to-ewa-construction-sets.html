Do EWA wooden models require glue or special tools?

Most EWA Eco-Wood-Art models use precision laser-cut pieces that fit together without glue. You'll typically need only a screwdriver for metal hardware and possibly sandpaper for finishing touches. This makes assembly accessible and reduces complexity compared to traditional model kits.

Are EWA construction sets suitable for children?

EWA offers age-appropriate models ranging from beginner sets for ages 12+ to advanced models for adults. Younger children should be supervised due to small components. The STEM-focused educational value makes them excellent for developing problem-solving and spatial reasoning skills.
