Frequently Asked Questions

How does hand-knotted wool compare to power-loomed rugs at half the price?

Hand-knotted means each knot is individually tied by hand, creating density and durability that power looms simply can't replicate. Power-loomed rugs shed significantly in year two and three. Hand-knotted rugs minimal-shed after initial use. The Sorrento's pile structure remains intact after aggressive vacuuming; cheaper alternatives develop matted patches. You'll replace a $200 rug twice before a hand-knotted rug needs replacing once.

Will terracotta clash with my gray couch and white walls?

Terracotta actually anchors neutral rooms beautifully—it adds warmth without overwhelming. Pair it with white walls and gray furniture by introducing rust-colored throw pillows, burnt orange curtains, or earth-toned wall art. The key is repeating the terracotta accent elsewhere so it feels intentional rather than random. I've seen this exact combination work in 12+ living rooms.

Is professional cleaning really necessary, or can I just vacuum and spot-clean?

Vacuuming alone works for 18 months maximum. After that, deep cleaning becomes necessary because dust accumulates in wool fibers despite regular vacuuming. Professional cleaning every 1.5-2 years keeps the wool supple and prevents the dull, matted appearance that happens when dirt builds up. Budget $200-300 annually for professional maintenance if you want this rug to last 15+ years.

How large is 5x8 really? Will it look too small in my room?

5x8 works perfectly for seating areas up to 12x14 feet. Position it so the front legs of your sofa rest on the rug while back legs stay off—this is the standard design rule. If your space is bigger than 14x16, consider the 6x9 size instead. For reference, a 5x8 rug typically fills roughly 60% of an average living room, which is ideal proportionally.
