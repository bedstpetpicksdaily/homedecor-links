What Is a Photo Flipbook and Why It's Perfect for Summer?

A photo flipbook is a small, pocket-sized booklet filled with sequential photos that create an animated effect when you flip through the pages quickly. It's nostalgic, interactive, and far more memorable than a standard photo album or digital slideshow. Flipbooks have made a major comeback as personalized gifts because they combine the charm of analog with the convenience of digital creation.

Summer memories deserve more than a folder on your phone. With GiftLab's custom photo flipbooks, you can transform candid moments into a physical keepsake that friends and family will treasure for years. Whether it's a hilarious video clip converted into flipbook format or a sequence of photos from a road trip, flipbooks make memories come alive literally at your fingertips.
