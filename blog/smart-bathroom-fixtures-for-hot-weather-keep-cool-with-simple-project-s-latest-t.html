Yes, absolutely. Simple Project's macerating toilets are specifically designed for basement installations and challenging spaces. They include smart ventilation integration and superior moisture control, making them ideal for basements where temperature and humidity regulation are critical.

Can I control Simple Project smart bathroom fixtures remotely?

Yes, Simple Project's smart fixtures come with app-based controls that allow you to monitor and adjust your bathroom's temperature and humidity levels from anywhere in your home using your smartphone.
