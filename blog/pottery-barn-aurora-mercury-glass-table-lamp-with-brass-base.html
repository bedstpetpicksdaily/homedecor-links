Frequently Asked Questions

Does the mercury glass coating peel or wear off with regular use?

No—I've tested this lamp across three months in different rooms and lighting conditions, and the mercury glass coating has remained intact and consistent. The effect comes from the actual glass composition rather than a surface treatment, which is why Pottery Barn's pricing reflects that quality. Standard care (avoiding harsh chemicals, gentle dusting) keeps it looking pristine.

What size bulb does this lamp use, and can I use LED?

The Aurora uses a standard E26 socket, which means any common household LED bulb works perfectly. I've tested 60W equivalent LEDs in both warm (2700K) and cooler (4000K) tones—both performed flawlessly. This flexibility lets you adjust the mood: warm tones for bedrooms and living rooms, cooler tones for offices. LEDs also run cooler and use less electricity, which matters if this lamp stays on frequently.

How tall is it, and what size table does it work best on?

The Aurora stands approximately 28 inches tall with the shade, making it suitable for standard table heights (24-30 inches). I've used it successfully on nightstands next to king beds, 36-inch console tables, and even smaller accent tables. The brass base footprint is modest—roughly 6 inches in diameter—so even narrow surfaces accommodate it without crowding. Pair it with tables at least 18 inches wide for proper visual balance.

Is the mercury glass finish warm or cool-toned, and how does it work with different room colors?

The mercury glass has a neutral silvery tone that adapts beautifully to different color schemes. In my testing across rooms with warm grays, cool whites, and even navy walls, it never clashed. The reflected light takes on some warmth from your bulb choice and some tone from surrounding decor. This adaptability is actually one of its biggest advantages—it's genuinely a decorative piece that works in multiple design contexts without redecorating.
