Frequently Asked Questions

What file format and resolution do I need for personalized photo gifts?

GiftLab recommends high-resolution images (minimum 300 DPI) in common formats like JPG or PNG. The higher the resolution, the sharper and more professional your final product will appear. They provide file upload guidelines during the customization process to ensure your photos print beautifully.

How long does it take to receive personalized photo gifts from GiftLab?

Standard processing typically takes 5-7 business days, with expedited options available for rush orders. Summer orders are recommended 2-3 weeks in advance to avoid delays during peak season. Check GiftLab's website for current turnaround times and shipping estimates.

Can I customize multiple items with the same photo?

Yes! GiftLab allows you to use the same image across multiple products. This is perfect for creating coordinated gift sets—for example, matching custom mugs and photo flipbooks featuring the same summer memories.

Are GiftLab's personalized mugs microwave and dishwasher safe?

Most of GiftLab's custom mugs are microwave and dishwasher safe, though it's recommended to hand wash them to preserve image quality over time. Always check product specifications before purchasing, as some specialty mugs may have different care requirements.

What makes photo flipbooks different from digital photo albums?

Photo flipbooks are tangible, interactive products that don't require electricity or screens. They offer a nostalgic, tactile experience and are perfect for sharing memories in-person. They're also great for display on shelves and coffee tables as unique home décor pieces.
