Frequently Asked Questions

How does Loloi Homage compare to Ruggable or similar performance rugs?

Completely different categories. Ruggable and performance rugs use synthetic fibers optimized for stain resistance and easy cleaning—they're 30-50% cheaper upfront. But they'll show wear, flatten under furniture, and won't develop the patina hand-knotted wool does. Loloi Homage trades easy maintenance for longevity. If you prioritize cleanup convenience and have young kids or pets, performance rugs win. If you're furnishing a formal living room or prioritize 15-year lifespan, Loloi's wool construction wins.

Is the 9x12 size right for my living room?

9x12 works if your sofa measures roughly 8 feet or less and you're anchoring a seating arrangement. Front legs of chairs should land on the rug. Test the dimensions before buying: a 9x12 rug in a 12x14 room leaves awkward margins, while the same rug in a 14x18 room feels perfectly grounded. If your seating area is unusually small (under 12x14), consider 8x10 instead. Too-large rugs make spaces feel cramped; too-small rugs appear accidental.

Does hand-knotted wool justify the price over Loloi's power-loomed alternatives?

Yes, if you're thinking in 10-year timelines. Hand-knotted rugs maintain pile height, resist flattening under furniture, and remain visually interesting as they age. Loloi's power-loomed rugs cost 40-50% less but will show matting in high-traffic zones by year four or five. The Homage hand-knotted version is the choice for family rooms and primary living spaces you'll keep long-term. Power-loomed works fine for secondary bedrooms or spaces with lighter foot traffic.
