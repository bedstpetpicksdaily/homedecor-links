Frequently Asked Questions

Will this macramé wall hanging work in a modern minimalist space?

Yes, but with intention. The cream color and layered texture actually complement minimalist design when paired with clean-lined furniture and limited wall décor. I'd suggest hanging it alone rather than clustering it with other pieces. How do you clean macramé wall hangings without damaging them?

A soft-bristled brush (like an old toothbrush or makeup brush) works best. Gently brush from top to bottom, allowing dust to fall naturally. For deeper spots, a handheld vacuum on the lowest setting with a brush attachment works, though I prefer the brush method for added control. Avoid spraying anything directly on the macramé, as the cream fibers can stain or take forever to dry. I clean mine every two to three weeks depending on household dust levels.

Is this piece appropriate for a rental apartment where you can't use heavy anchors?

Completely. At 24 inches and made from lightweight macramé, this requires only a standard picture hanging nail—nothing more. It weighs less than most framed art. Even if your lease is strict about wall anchors, this piece is gentle on drywall and poses no risk of significant damage. The included hardware is basic and straightforward, making it perfect for renters who need to take things down without leaving holes.
