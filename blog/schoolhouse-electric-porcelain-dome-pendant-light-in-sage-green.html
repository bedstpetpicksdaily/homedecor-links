Frequently Asked Questions

What bulb type works best with this pendant light?

LED A19 or A21 bulbs deliver the best results—they generate minimal heat, fit standard sockets, and the sage green dome's warm undertones pair beautifully with 2700K color temperature LEDs. I tested both warm white (2700K) and soft white (3000K) in my installations, and the 2700K created more ambient, inviting light. Avoid higher wattage incandescent bulbs; the dome's enclosed design limits safe wattage to about 60W maximum.

How does sage green work in different room settings?

In my testing, sage green performed exceptionally well over kitchen islands with warm wood tones—it grounded the space without competing for visual attention. In bedrooms with cooler gray walls, it added unexpected warmth without clashing. The key is avoiding rooms already saturated with green undertones (like living rooms with olive upholstery). The dome works best when it's the room's single bold design statement rather than one of several competing accent colors.

Is installation difficult for someone without electrical experience?

Installation mirrors any standard pendant light: turn off power at the breaker, disconnect existing fixture (if replacing), connect wires using provided connectors, and secure the canopy. The instructions are clear, and the dome's weight (approximately 2-3 pounds) makes one-person installation feasible with a basic ladder. That said, if you're not comfortable working with ceiling wiring, hiring an electrician for $100-150 is money well spent and shouldn't feel like an extravagance given the fixture's price investment.

Will this pendant light look dated in three years?

Schoolhouse Electric designs pieces rooted in industrial and mid-century Modern aesthetics that have remained relevant for decades, not trend cycles. The sage green colorway is muted enough to avoid the 'Instagram aesthetic of 2026' trap. That said, bold colored fixtures by their nature are statement pieces—commitment-level purchases, not temporary decor. If you love the color today and your design preferences shift dramatically, you'll notice it more than if you'd chosen white or black.
