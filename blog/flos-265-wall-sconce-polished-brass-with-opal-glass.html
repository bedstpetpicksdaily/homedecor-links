Frequently Asked Questions

How many of these should I install for proper ambient lighting?

One Flos 265 works as accent or task lighting beside a mirror or artwork, but rarely as primary ambient light in an open room. For a 12×14 bedroom or living area, plan for two flanking a focal point, plus overhead or pendant fixtures. The opal glass diffusion is generous, but this fixture's strength is quality over quantity. Think of it as your room's jewelry, not its foundation lighting.

Will the polished brass tarnish or require special maintenance?

Polished brass will develop patina over time—this is actually desirable and part of its character. Unlike cheap brass plating that flakes or oxidizes unevenly, Flos uses solid brass that ages with dignity. Dust it monthly and polish lightly once or twice yearly if you prefer the bright finish. In dry climates, patina develops slowly. In humid environments, it accelerates, but remains beautiful rather than deteriorating.

Can this sconce work in a bathroom, and how do I protect it from moisture damage?

Yes, but with considerations. Brass handles moisture better than steel, and the opal glass won't rust, but the electrical components need space from direct spray. Mount it at least 2-3 feet from shower areas and ensure your bathroom has adequate ventilation. If you're placing it directly above a sink, you'll need to clean it weekly to prevent water spots from etching the glass. Bathroom applications shorten the interval between maintenance, so factor that into your decision.

How does the Flos 265 compare to other designer wall sconces in this price range?

In the luxury wall sconce category, you're choosing between Italian minimalism (Flos), Scandinavian restraint (Hay, Artemide), or contemporary maximalism (Tom Dixon). The 265 excels if you want understated elegance that won't date. It's quieter, more durable, and less precious-feeling than trendier pieces. If you want something that makes a bold statement, the 265 disappears—which is precisely why designers prefer it.
