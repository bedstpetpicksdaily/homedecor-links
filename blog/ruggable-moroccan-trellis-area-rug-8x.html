Frequently Asked Questions

Can you really put this rug in a standard washing machine?

Yes, absolutely. I use a standard top-load washer on the gentle cycle with cold water and regular laundry detergent. The rug's backing is specifically engineered to separate and reattach after washing. Don't use hot water or bleach, and air dry it completely before replacing it—I lay mine flat on the patio for 24 hours. This is genuinely easier than most people expect.

How does the Moroccan Trellis pattern look in different lighting?

The design holds up beautifully in both natural and artificial light. The geometric trellis doesn't look dated or trendy—it's classic enough that it'll work with your decor updates over several years. In my living room with afternoon sunlight from south-facing windows, the pattern adds depth without looking busy.

Is this rug worth the price compared to standard machine-washable rugs?

This is the honest answer: you're paying a premium for the specific backing technology and the ability to fully separate and rewash the rug. Cheaper machine-washable rugs exist, but they often deteriorate faster or don't clean as effectively. For an 8x10 that'll handle a decade of family life, the investment justifies itself through durability alone.
