What is a Macerating Toilet and Why Choose One?

A macerating toilet is a self-contained bathroom fixture that combines a toilet bowl with a built-in macerator unit. The macerator grinds solid waste into a slurry and automatically pumps it through small-diameter pipes to your home's main drainage system or septic tank. This revolutionary design means you can install a basement bathroom almost anywhere without extensive excavation or re-plumbing. Simple Project's macerating toilets offer premium engineering with quiet operation and reliable performance, making them ideal for homeowners who want professional results without the professional price tag.

Tools and Materials You'll Need

Before starting your installation, gather the essential tools: an adjustable wrench, screwdrivers (Phillips and flathead), a level, flexible waste hose (typically 3/4 inch), plumber's tape, and a caulking gun. You'll also need the macerating toilet unit itself—Simple Project provides everything you need in their bathroom renovation kits, including the toilet, macerator pump, and pre-sized connectors. Most installations require 120V electrical access nearby, so confirm your outlet placement before beginning work.

Step-by-Step Installation Process

Start by removing your existing toilet (if replacing) by shutting off the water supply and flushing to empty the bowl. Disconnect the supply line and unbolt the toilet from the floor flange. For new installations, prepare a level floor area with proper drainage access. Position your Simple Project macerating toilet on its mounting bolts, ensuring it sits level—use shims if necessary. Connect the water supply line to the fill valve, then attach the discharge hose to the macerator outlet, running it toward your main drain line or stack vent. Most Simple Project models feature intuitive quick-connect fittings that eliminate complicated plumbing work. Once hoses are secured with clamps and plumber's tape, connect the electrical cord to your nearby outlet. Run the system through a test cycle with water only before regular use.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

The most frequent installation error is improper hose routing—the discharge line should never have low spots where waste could collect. Always slope the hose slightly upward toward the main drain to prevent backflow. Additionally, don't skimp on securing hose clamps; loose connections cause leaks and reduce efficiency. Verify that your electrical outlet is properly grounded and consider installing a dedicated circuit for optimal performance. Simple Project's smart toilet models come with diagnostic features that alert you to installation issues, so take time to review all error codes during your initial startup.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do macerating toilets typically last?

Quality macerating toilets from Simple Project are engineered for 10-15 years of reliable service with proper maintenance. The macerator pump is the most critical component and typically lasts 7-10 years before requiring replacement.

Can I use regular toilet paper with a macerating toilet?

Yes, standard toilet paper works fine with macerating toilets. However, avoid flushing paper towels, feminine products, or other non-flushable items, as these can clog the macerator mechanism.

What happens during a power outage?

During a power outage, the toilet bowl functions normally for one use. However, the macerator won't operate, so you shouldn't use it multiple times before power is restored. Simple Project models often include battery backup options for added convenience.

Is installation really a DIY-friendly project?

Yes, with simple plumbing and electrical basics, most homeowners can install a Simple Project macerating toilet in 2-4 hours. The kit includes detailed instructions and pre-fitted connectors that simplify the process significantly.

How much does a macerating toilet cost compared to standard toilets?

Macerating toilets are more expensive upfront ($800-$2,000+), but they eliminate thousands in plumbing labor and excavation costs, making them cost-effective for basement bathroom installations.
