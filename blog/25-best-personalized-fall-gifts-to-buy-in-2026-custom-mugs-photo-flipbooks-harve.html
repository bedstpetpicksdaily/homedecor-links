Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I order personalized fall gifts?

Aim for at least 2–3 weeks before your occasion. Personalized items are made to order, so production plus shipping takes longer than standard retail. For Thanksgiving, order by early November; for December holidays, order by the first week of December to be safe.

What is a photo flipbook and who is it best for?

A photo flipbook converts a short video clip into a printed book of frames you flip through to replay the moment. It's ideal for weddings, proposals, first steps, graduations and pet videos — basically any 3–8 second clip with emotional weight. It's one of GiftLab's most popular novelty gifts.

What photo quality do I need for custom mugs and canvas prints?

Use the highest-resolution original file you have — ideally straight from your phone's camera roll rather than a screenshot or a social media download. Compressed or cropped images can look soft or pixelated when printed on larger surfaces like blankets and canvases.

Are personalized gifts a good idea for coworkers or hostesses?

Yes, as long as you keep the personalization light. A mug with a shared inside joke, a monogrammed kitchen towel or an engraved cutting board reads as thoughtful without being overly intimate — perfect for Friendsgiving hosts, teachers and office gift exchanges.
