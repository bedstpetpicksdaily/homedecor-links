Installation Tips for Maximum Impact

Once you've selected your large wall art from Big Wall Decor's collection, proper installation is crucial for achieving maximum impact. Frequently Asked Questions

What size large wall art should I choose for my living room?

For most living rooms, oversized canvas prints measuring 48x72 inches or larger work best, especially if you're creating a focal point above a sofa. Big Wall Decor offers various dimensions, so measure your wall space first and choose a piece proportional to your furniture.

How do I decide between abstract art and other styles?

Abstract art is incredibly versatile and works well in modern, contemporary, and minimalist spaces. If your home has a traditional style, consider framed art in classic designs. Big Wall Decor's collection includes options for every aesthetic preference.

Are Big Wall Decor's oversized canvas prints durable?

Yes, Big Wall Decor specializes in high-quality large wall art using premium materials that resist fading and maintain their appearance for years. Their products are built to last, making them an excellent investment for your home refresh.

Can I return large wall decor if it doesn't fit my space?

Big Wall Decor has a return policy for their products. Always check their specific guidelines before purchasing oversized canvas prints or statement wall pieces to ensure you're covered if the piece doesn't work with your décor.

What's the best way to hang large wall art safely?

Use appropriate hardware rated for the weight of your oversized canvas print or framed art. For heavier pieces, install into wall studs when possible. Big Wall Decor products come with installation instructions, but consulting a professional is recommended for very large pieces.
