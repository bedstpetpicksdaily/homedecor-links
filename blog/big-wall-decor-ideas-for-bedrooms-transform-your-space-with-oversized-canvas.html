Frequently Asked Questions

What size oversized canvas should I choose for my bedroom?

The ideal size depends on your wall space and bed size. For a queen or king bed, consider a canvas that's at least 60-80 inches wide. Big Wall Decor offers various sizes to fit different bedroom dimensions, so measure your wall first and choose accordingly.

How do I hang oversized canvas prints from Big Wall Decor?

Big Wall Decor's oversized canvas prints come gallery-wrapped and ready to hang with included hardware. Simply locate the studs in your wall or use appropriate wall anchors for drywall, then mount the hanging hardware and hang your artwork.

Can I use abstract art in a traditional bedroom design?

Absolutely! Abstract art is incredibly versatile. Big Wall Decor's collection includes pieces that range from subtle and understated to bold and colorful, making it easy to find abstract art that complements traditional, modern, or eclectic bedroom designs.

What's the difference between framed art and oversized canvas prints?

Framed art from Big Wall Decor provides a more formal, gallery-like appearance and offers additional protection, while oversized canvas prints offer a modern, casual aesthetic with easier installation. Both create stunning big wall decor—choose based on your preferred style.
