What to Look For

1. Linen Quality and Blend Composition

  • Look for 100% pure linen or linen blends (linen-cotton combinations offer durability)
  • Check GSM (grams per square meter) ratings—higher is generally more durable
  • Verify that the linen is pre-shrunk to avoid fit issues after washing
  • Pure linen will have visible texture; overly smooth "linen-look" covers are often synthetic blends

2. Set Composition and Cover Sizes

  • Confirm how many covers are included (typically 2, 4, or 6-piece sets)
  • Verify standard sizes: 18x18" for throw pillows, 20x20" for sofa pillows, 12x20" for lumbar covers
  • Check if covers come with or without pillow inserts (sets without inserts are usually cheaper)
  • Ensure sizing information is detailed—mismatched covers and pillows are the #1 customer complaint

3. Color Range and Neutrality

  • Choose from curated color palettes that work together (not random color assortments)
  • Neutral tones like beige, ivory, sage, and taupe are most versatile for seasonal updates
  • Check if the set includes coordinating colors that allow flexible arrangement options
  • Look for fade resistance ratings, especially if you have sunny windows

4. Care Instructions and Durability Features

  • Machine washable in cold water is essential for easy maintenance
  • Look for colorfast dyes that won't bleed during washing
  • Hidden zippers are preferable to exposed zippers for a polished look
  • Check return policies—reputable sellers offer 30-60 day returns in case covers don't meet expectations

Frequently Asked Questions

What size linen throw pillow covers should I buy for my couch?

The most common sizes for couch pillows are 16x16", 18x18", and 20x20", with 18x18" being the standard for most sofas. Measure your existing pillows or couch depth to determine the right fit—covers should fit snugly without puckering or sagging. If you're buying inserts separately, always size up slightly since linen shrinks about 3-5% after washing.

Do linen pillow covers shrink in the wash?

Yes, natural linen typically shrinks 3-5% on the first wash, so pre-washing before use is recommended to prevent future shrinkage. To minimize shrinkage, wash in cold water on a gentle cycle and air dry or tumble dry on low heat. High heat and hot water will cause additional shrinkage, so always follow manufacturer care instructions.

How many pillow covers do I need for a sectional or large sofa?

A typical three-cushion sofa works well with 4-6 throw pillows in varying sizes, while larger sectionals can accommodate 6-8 pillows depending on your style preference. A good rule of thumb is to use odd numbers (3, 5, or 7) and mix sizes like two 20x20", two 18x18", and one 14x14" for visual balance. Consider your couch color and existing décor when deciding on quantity and arrangement.

Are linen pillow cover sets pre-shrunk and what's the difference between linen and linen-blend?

Not all sets are pre-shrunk, so check the product description—quality brands often pre-shrink fabric to minimize future shrinkage. Pure linen is 100% natural and has a relaxed, textured look that softens over time, while linen-blend (typically linen mixed with cotton or polyester) is more wrinkle-resistant, durable, and easier to maintain. Choose pure linen for a natural aesthetic or linen-blend if you prioritize low-maintenance upkeep.
