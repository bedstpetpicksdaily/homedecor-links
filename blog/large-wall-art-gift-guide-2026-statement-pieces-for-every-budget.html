Frequently Asked Questions

What size large wall art should I gift?

Size depends on the room and wall space. For living rooms, 40x50 inches or larger makes a bold statement. Bedrooms work well with 30x40 inches to 40x50 inches. Big Wall Decor provides detailed size guides to help you choose appropriately.

Are oversized canvas prints from Big Wall Decor easy to hang?

Yes, Big Wall Decor's large wall art comes with professional mounting hardware and clear installation instructions. Most pieces can be hung by anyone with basic tools.

What's the difference between framed art and canvas prints?

Canvas prints offer a modern, gallery-style appearance and come as gallery wraps or with stretchers. Framed art includes traditional frames that add sophistication. Big Wall Decor offers both options—choose based on recipient preference and room style.

Can large wall art work in any room?

Absolutely. Big Wall Decor specializes in statement pieces for living rooms, bedrooms, offices, and entryways. Abstract art works nearly anywhere, while specific designs may suit particular spaces better.

How do I know what style to choose?

Consider the recipient's existing decor, color preferences, and personal style. Big Wall Decor's website features detailed images and customer photos showing pieces in various room settings, helping you visualize final results.
