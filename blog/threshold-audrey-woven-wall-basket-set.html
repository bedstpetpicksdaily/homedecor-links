Frequently Asked Questions

Do these baskets need wall anchors or can they hang on regular nails?

Use appropriate anchors based on your wall type. For drywall, toggle bolts or heavy-duty anchors rated for the basket's weight are essential—don't trust standard nails. Plaster walls need different anchors entirely. Check the product weight before purchasing and match your hardware accordingly. Undersizing anchors is the fastest way to watch a basket drop unexpectedly.

Will these work in a rental apartment?

They require wall holes for installation, so check your lease terms first. If you can't drill, you're out of luck with this product. There are adhesive-mounted storage options available as alternatives, though they lack the load capacity of anchored baskets. Better to explore those before committing to damage-prone installation methods.

How do I clean woven baskets without damaging the material?

Dry dusting with a soft brush or cloth is your safest bet. Avoid soaking or direct water exposure—spot-cleaning with a barely damp cloth works for minor stains. For stubborn dust, a soft vacuum brush attachment works better than wiping. Never machine wash or drench natural fiber baskets; you're inviting mold and structural breakdown.

Do these baskets work in kitchens with steam from cooking?

Not ideally. Kitchen steam and moisture create the exact conditions that warp natural fiber over time. If you want woven storage in a kitchen, position baskets far from the stove and away from direct steam patterns. Open shelving above a range is basically hostile territory for these. Consider moisture-resistant alternatives for high-humidity kitchen zones.

Can I paint or stain these to match a specific color?

Technically possible but not recommended by most brands, including Threshold. Painting woven baskets clogs the texture and changes why these products are attractive in the first place. If color matching is crucial to your design scheme, you're fighting the basket's natural strengths. Better to design around the neutral tone or choose painted storage alternatives from the start.
