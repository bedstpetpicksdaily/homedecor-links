Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is a macerating toilet and when would I need one?

A macerating toilet grinds waste and pumps it upward to your main drain line, making it ideal for basement bathrooms, half-baths under stairs, or any location where traditional gravity-fed plumbing isn't possible. Simple Project's macerating toilets are perfect renovation solutions for adding bathrooms without major plumbing restructuring.

Are smart toilets difficult to install and maintain?

Modern smart toilets from Simple Project are designed for relatively straightforward installation, though professional plumbing installation is recommended. Maintenance involves regular cleaning and checking electrical connections. They're no more complicated to maintain than traditional toilets once installed.

Can I mix and match fixtures from different brands in my bathroom renovation?

Yes, you can mix brands, but using coordinated fixtures from a single manufacturer like Simple Project ensures consistent style, quality, and compatibility. Their curated collections are designed to work together seamlessly for a polished, professional bathroom appearance.

How much can a bathroom renovation increase my home's value?

Bathroom renovations typically return 50-80% of their investment in home value, with modern fixtures and smart technology often appreciated by buyers. Upgrading to quality fixtures and smart solutions from Simple Project is a solid investment in your home's appeal and functionality.

Are smart toilets worth the extra cost?

Smart toilets offer long-term value through improved hygiene, water savings, comfort features, and durability. Many users find the enhanced experience and health benefits justify the investment, especially when choosing reliable brands like Simple Project that stand behind their products.
