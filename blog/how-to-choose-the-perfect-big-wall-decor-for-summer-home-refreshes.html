Assess Your Space and Wall Size

The first step in selecting the perfect big wall decor is understanding your space. Measure your walls and consider the room's proportions—oversized canvas prints and statement wall pieces should be appropriately scaled to avoid looking cramped or lost in the space. For larger walls, Big Wall Decor's collection of expansive pieces works beautifully as a dominant focal point. In smaller rooms, strategically placing a large art piece above a sofa or bed can create visual impact without overwhelming the area.

Consider Your Color Palette and Design Style

Summer refreshes often call for lighter, brighter spaces. Evaluate your current color scheme and think about what tones and hues will complement your furnishings and walls. Big Wall Decor's abstract art collection offers versatile options ranging from bold, energetic designs to soft, serene compositions. Whether you prefer vibrant colors that energize the space or neutral tones that provide sophistication, you'll find living room wall art and bedroom wall decor that matches your aesthetic perfectly. Consider how the artwork will interact with natural summer light streaming through your windows.

Mix and Match Framed Art and Canvas Prints

Abstract art pieces work exceptionally well when layered together, creating depth and dimension in your space. Big Wall Decor's selection allows you to mix different sizes and styles to achieve a curated, intentional look that feels both modern and inviting.

Frequently Asked Questions

What size wall art is best for a large living room?

For larger living rooms, oversized canvas prints measuring 40+ inches in width work best as statement wall pieces. Big Wall Decor offers extensive options in these dimensions that create dramatic focal points without overwhelming the space.

How do I choose between abstract art and other styles?

Abstract art from Big Wall Decor offers versatility and works with virtually any design aesthetic. Consider your existing décor—abstract pieces complement modern, contemporary, and transitional styles beautifully while adding visual interest.

Can I combine multiple pieces of big wall decor together?

Absolutely! Combining oversized canvas prints with framed art creates a curated gallery effect. Big Wall Decor's collections are designed to work together, allowing you to create dynamic, layered displays that enhance your entire room.

How do I ensure my large wall art is properly installed?

Big Wall Decor provides detailed installation guides with their pieces. For oversized statement wall pieces, professional installation is recommended to ensure safety and proper alignment on your walls.
