Frequently Asked Questions

What size wall art is best for different room dimensions?

For small to medium rooms (under 200 sq ft), 24x36 or 36x48 inch pieces work well. Larger rooms benefit from 48x72 inch or bigger statement wall pieces. Big Wall Decor's size guides help you select the perfect dimensions for your space.

How do I hang heavy oversized canvas prints safely?

Use appropriate wall anchors rated for your piece's weight, or hang on studs when possible. Big Wall Decor includes hanging hardware, and professional installation services are available in many areas for larger pieces.

Can abstract wall art work in any room?

Yes, abstract art is incredibly versatile. From living rooms to bedrooms to home offices, abstract pieces can complement virtually any decor style. Big Wall Decor offers abstracts in various color palettes to match your room's aesthetic.

Are Big Wall Decor's framed art pieces worth the investment?

Absolutely. Their framed art combines quality materials, professional printing, and elegant frames that add polish to any room. Summer deals make these premium pieces even more valuable.
