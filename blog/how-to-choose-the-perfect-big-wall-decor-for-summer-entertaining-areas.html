Understanding Your Space and Scale

Before selecting your big wall decor, measure your walls carefully and consider the room's overall dimensions. Oversized canvas prints work best when they don't overwhelm the space—typically, your artwork should cover 60-75% of the wall area above furniture. For summer entertaining areas with high ceilings, larger statement wall pieces from Big Wall Decor create dramatic focal points that draw the eye and encourage conversation. Consider the distance people will view the artwork from; pieces meant for intimate seating should feature details visible up close, while art for large entertaining spaces can showcase bolder, more abstract designs.

Choosing Colors and Styles That Complement Your Theme

Your wall art should harmonize with your existing color palette while adding visual interest. For summer entertaining, bright, energetic abstract art can invigorate a space, while cooler tones create a more relaxed, sophisticated atmosphere. Big Wall Decor offers extensive collections of living room wall art and bedroom wall decor in various styles—from vibrant contemporary pieces to serene minimalist designs. Think about whether you want your wall art to blend seamlessly with your décor or serve as a bold contrast that anchors the room's design.

Quality and Material Considerations

Investing in premium framed art and canvas prints ensures your big wall decor withstands the demands of entertaining areas. Look for fade-resistant inks, high-quality canvas materials, and sturdy framing that protects against moisture and temperature changes—especially important for spaces near outdoor entertaining areas. Big Wall Decor's collection features museum-quality oversized canvas prints and statement wall pieces crafted to maintain their beauty for years. The right materials prevent warping, fading, and damage that can occur in high-traffic entertaining spaces.

Creating a Cohesive Gallery Effect

Large entertaining spaces benefit from creating visual interest through multiple statement wall pieces or a single dramatic focal point. Big Wall Decor's range allows you to mix and match abstract art with framed pieces to create a curated gallery look. Consider arranging oversized canvas prints at varying heights to draw attention around the room, or commit to one showstopping piece as your entertaining area's centerpiece. This approach creates flow and guides guests' eyes throughout your space naturally.

Frequently Asked Questions

What size big wall decor should I choose for my entertaining room?

Measure your wall space and select artwork that covers 60-75% of the wall area above furniture. For rooms with 8-foot ceilings, 40-60 inch canvases work well, while rooms with higher ceilings can accommodate 72+ inch oversized canvas prints from Big Wall Decor.

Does abstract art work for all entertaining spaces?

Yes! Abstract art is versatile and complements various décor styles. It adds visual interest without being literal, making it perfect for entertaining areas where you want conversation to be the focus rather than specific imagery.

How do I care for large canvas prints in high-traffic entertaining areas?

Dust oversized canvas prints monthly with a soft cloth, keep them away from direct sunlight to prevent fading, and maintain consistent humidity levels. Big Wall Decor uses fade-resistant inks and quality materials designed to withstand the demands of busy entertaining spaces.

Can I use big wall decor in both indoor and outdoor entertaining areas?

While most canvas prints are best kept indoors, Big Wall Decor offers various options. For outdoor spaces, look for weather-resistant framed art or consider installing your statement wall pieces under covered patios and porches.

How do I choose between one large piece versus multiple smaller statement wall pieces?

One large oversized canvas print creates a dramatic focal point ideal for open entertaining areas, while multiple medium statement pieces work better for breaking up long walls or creating a gallery effect in larger spaces.
