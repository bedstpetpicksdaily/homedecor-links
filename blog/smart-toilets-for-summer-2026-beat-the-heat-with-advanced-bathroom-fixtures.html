Frequently Asked Questions

Do smart toilets really help with summer bathroom comfort?

Yes. Smart toilets with cooling bidet functions, temperature-controlled features, and advanced moisture management significantly improve bathroom comfort during summer heat. What are macerating toilets, and why are they useful for summer renovations?

Macerating toilets grind waste and transport it through smaller diameter pipes, eliminating the need for traditional plumbing layouts. This makes them ideal for basement bathrooms, powder rooms, and renovation projects where you want to add a toilet without extensive construction work.
