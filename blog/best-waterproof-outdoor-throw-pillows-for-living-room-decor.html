Creating a beautiful outdoor living room that can withstand the elements while maintaining its aesthetic appeal is a challenge many homeowners face. Waterproof throw pillows are the perfect solution to add comfort and style to your patio or deck without sacrificing durability. Whether you're dealing with unexpected rain, morning dew, or harsh UV rays, the right outdoor pillows will keep your space looking inviting year-round.

What to Look For

  • Genuine Waterproof Fabric: Look for pillows made from solution-dyed acrylic or high-performance polyester specifically designed for outdoor use. These materials are treated to repel water rather than simply resist it, which means water beads up and rolls off rather than soaking in. Avoid anything labeled merely "water-resistant" if you want true protection from pooling water.
  • UV-Resistant Construction: Outdoor pillows need to protect against fading from constant sun exposure. Quality waterproof outdoor pillows use fabrics treated to resist UV damage, ensuring your vibrant colors and patterns stay beautiful season after season without becoming washed out or discolored.
  • Mildew and Mold Prevention: The combination of moisture and outdoor conditions creates an ideal environment for mildew growth. Choose pillows with antimicrobial treatments or those made from materials that naturally resist mold and mildew, so your investment stays fresh-smelling and hygienic even in humid climates.

What to Avoid

  • Regular Indoor Throw Pillows: Even if they're temporarily placed outdoors, standard fabric pillows will absorb moisture, develop mildew, and fade quickly in sunlight. These are false economy—you'll replace them far more frequently than actual outdoor pillows, making them expensive and frustrating in the long run.
  • Cheap Polyester with Minimal Treatment: Not all outdoor-labeled pillows are created equal. Budget options that use thin, lightly-treated polyester may shed color, tear easily, or fail to truly repel water when tested in real conditions. Invest in established outdoor fabric brands rather than generic alternatives.
  • Pillows Without Drainage Solutions: Avoid covers that trap water inside or lack proper zipper design for drainage. Quality outdoor pillows either use completely waterproof construction or feature removable covers with underside vents that allow any trapped moisture to escape rather than accumulate.
