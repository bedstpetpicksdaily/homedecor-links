Frequently Asked Questions

What is a serger and why do I need one for quilting?

A serger is a specialized sewing machine that creates finished seams and edges using multiple threads and a cutting blade. For quilting, sergers are invaluable for binding quilt edges, finishing seams to prevent fraying, and adding decorative details. Baby Lock sergers make these tasks easier with features specifically designed for quilting fabrics.

Can beginners really use a serger without prior experience?

Yes, absolutely. Baby Lock sergers are specifically engineered for ease of use with color-coded threading systems, automatic tension settings, and comprehensive instruction guides. Many beginners successfully operate Baby Lock sergers right out of the box with minimal practice.

What's the difference between 2-thread, 3-thread, and 4-thread serging?

2-thread serging is lightweight and ideal for rolled hems. 3-thread is versatile for general seaming and decorative work. 4-thread provides the strongest, most durable seams and is often preferred for quilt binding. Baby Lock models offering multiple thread options give you maximum flexibility for different quilting techniques.
