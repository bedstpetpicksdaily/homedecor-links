Frequently Asked Questions

Are smart toilets difficult to install?

Smart toilets from Simple Project are designed for straightforward installation, though professional installation is recommended to ensure proper electrical connection and optimal functionality. Most installations can be completed within a few hours.

How much water do smart toilets save?

Modern smart toilets from Simple Project use approximately 1.28 gallons per flush compared to older models that use 3-7 gallons. This efficiency can reduce water consumption by up to 40% annually, resulting in significant savings on water bills.

What makes macerating toilets different?

Macerating toilets from Simple Project grind waste and compress it, allowing installation in basements and other areas where traditional plumbing isn't available. They're ideal for renovations in challenging spaces without extensive plumbing work.

Do smart toilets require ongoing maintenance?

Smart toilets are designed for minimal maintenance. Simple Project models feature self-cleaning nozzles and durable components. Regular cleaning with mild soap and water, plus occasional filter checks, keeps your toilet functioning perfectly for years.

Can I use a smart toilet with my existing bathroom design?

Yes. Simple Project's smart toilets feature elegant, modern designs that complement various bathroom styles. Their coordinating fixtures also help create a cohesive look if you're updating multiple elements.
