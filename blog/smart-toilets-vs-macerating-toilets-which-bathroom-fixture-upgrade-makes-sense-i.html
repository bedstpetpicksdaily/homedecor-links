Advanced Smart Toilets with Integrated Bidet Technology

Smart toilets from Simple Project feature automatic bidet functions, heated seats, air-drying capabilities, and customizable water pressure settings. They include energy-efficient flushing systems that reduce water consumption compared to standard toilets. These are standard-installation fixtures that work in any bathroom with existing plumbing.

Best for: Master bathrooms and main-floor renovations where you want to upgrade comfort and hygiene without moving plumbing lines.

Efficient Macerating Toilet Systems

Macerating toilets grind waste and paper into a fine slurry, enabling installation in basements, attics, and spaces far from main drain lines. They're designed for unconventional layouts where standard gravity-fed toilets won't work. Simple Project positions these as game-changers for basement bathroom renovations.

Best for: Basement installations, guest bathrooms in remote areas of the home, or any space where running new drain lines to the main stack is cost-prohibitive.

Space-Saving Corner Sinks and Compact Toilets

Designed for smaller bathrooms or layouts where square footage is limited. These fixtures let you install functional bathrooms in tight spaces, though they sacrifice some of the comfort features of full-size smart toilets.

Our Verdict

Choose a smart toilet if your goal is comfort and you have a standard bathroom layout. The heated seat, bidet, and air-drying features represent genuine lifestyle upgrades for master bathrooms and primary baths where you spend time daily. Installation is straightforward, and water savings offset some of the premium cost over time.

Choose a macerating toilet if you're solving a structural problem. Basement bathrooms, guest spaces in remote parts of the home, and unconventional layouts where standard plumbing won't reach are where macerating systems earn their cost. They're not comfort upgrades; they're enablers of bathrooms that wouldn't exist otherwise.

If budget is the primary constraint, neither option is essential. The sources don't provide specific sale pricing or comparison against standard toilet + faucet combinations. A contemporary vanity fixture with a standard toilet may deliver more renovated-feeling results per dollar spent than either premium option.

In practice, many homeowners use both approaches: a macerating toilet in a new basement bathroom paired with a smart toilet in the master bath upgrade. This combines the structural flexibility of macerating systems with the comfort benefits of smart technology where you'll actually use them most.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a macerating toilet and why would I need one?

A macerating toilet grinds waste and paper into a fine slurry before sending it through small-diameter lines to the main drain. You need one when building a bathroom in a basement, attic, or other location too far from existing plumbing lines to install a standard gravity-fed toilet. They're infrastructure solutions, not comfort upgrades.

Are Simple Project smart toilets water-efficient?

Yes—the sources specifically mention energy-efficient flushing systems that reduce water consumption compared to standard toilets, making them better for eco-minded homeowners. However, the electricity required for heated seats, bidets, and air-drying adds to operating costs.

Smart toilets in standard bathroom layouts can often be installed as DIY replacements if you're comfortable with basic plumbing.
