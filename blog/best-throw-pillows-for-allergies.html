If you suffer from allergies, you know how challenging it can be to create a cozy home environment without triggering symptoms. Throw pillows are a staple of comfortable interior design, but traditional options filled with down, feathers, or synthetic materials can harbor dust mites, pet dander, and allergens that compromise your health and comfort. Finding the right hypoallergenic throw pillows means you don't have to sacrifice style for wellness—you can have both.

What to Look For

  • Hypoallergenic Fill Materials: Opt for pillows filled with certified hypoallergenic materials like memory foam, polyester microfiber, or specially treated down alternatives. Look for certifications like OEKO-TEX or Allergy UK to ensure the fill has been tested and approved for allergy sufferers.
  • Tightly Woven Covers: The fabric covering your pillow is just as important as the fill. Choose covers made from densely woven cotton, microfiber, or performance fabrics that prevent dust mites and allergens from penetrating the material. A thread count of at least 300 is ideal for creating a barrier against microscopic irritants.
  • Washability and Maintenance: Select throw pillows with removable, machine-washable covers that can be cleaned frequently in hot water to eliminate allergens. Regular washing (ideally weekly) is one of the most effective ways to reduce allergy triggers, so easy-care fabrics are essential for this use case.

Our Top Pick

We recommend microfiber-filled throw pillows with a tight cotton blend cover as the best solution for allergy sufferers. This combination provides exceptional comfort while offering a naturally resistant barrier to dust mites and allergens. The microfiber fill is inherently hypoallergenic and won't break down over time like some natural fills, meaning your pillow maintains its protective qualities for years. The removable cover can be zipped off and washed weekly without concern, keeping allergens at bay while the pillow itself resists moisture and mold growth—both common allergy culprits.

Why This Works for This Situation

Microfiber fill is laboratory-tested and proven to repel dust mites, one of the most common indoor allergens. Unlike down or feather pillows that can develop small tears allowing fill to escape and accumulate allergens over time, microfiber maintains its integrity and density. The synthetic nature of microfiber also prevents the growth of mold and mildew, which are frequent triggers for those with respiratory sensitivities or asthma. When combined with a tightly woven cotton-blend cover, you create a complete barrier system that stops allergens before they reach your breathing space.

The removable cover design is crucial for allergy management because it allows you to wash the pillow cover in hot water on a weekly basis—the temperature needed to truly kill dust mites and remove allergen buildup. Unlike pillows with sewn-in covers that require dry cleaning or gentle washing, these pillows can withstand frequent, thorough cleaning without deteriorating. This washability factor alone can reduce allergy symptoms significantly, as regular cleaning removes accumulated dust, pet dander, and other microscopic irritants that traditional pillows trap over months and years.

What to Avoid

  • Down and Feather Pillows: While luxuriously soft, down and feather fills are notorious allergen traps. Feathers can develop small quills that break through fabric, and both materials create an ideal environment for dust mite colonies. Even "hypoallergenic down" is not truly allergy-friendly, as it still allows microscopic allergens to penetrate.
  • Low Thread Count Covers: Loosely woven fabrics with low thread counts (under 200) won't effectively block dust mites and allergens from reaching the fill. These covers may feel soft initially, but they fail at their primary purpose of creating a protective barrier for allergy sufferers.
