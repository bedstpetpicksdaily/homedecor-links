Throw pillows are one of the easiest ways to transform your couch and refresh your entire living room without a major investment. They tie together your color palette, add texture and comfort, and give you a simple way to update your décor with the seasons or whenever you want a change.

What to Look For

  • Size and proportion to your couch: A standard 18x18 inch pillow works for most couches, but measure your couch depth and cushion size first. Oversized pillows (20x20 or 22x22) work well for deep, spacious couches, while smaller 16x16 pillows suit apartment-sized furniture. You want pillows that look intentional, not like they're drowning in your seating or disappearing into it.
  • Fabric durability and feel: Since throw pillows live on your couch where they'll get frequent use and contact, choose performance fabrics like linen blends, cotton canvas, or microsuede that can handle daily wear. Avoid delicate fabrics like silk or velvet for high-traffic couches—save those for accent pillows in low-use spaces. Run your hand across samples in person if possible; you want a fabric that feels good against skin and invites lounging.
  • Color and pattern that complements your existing décor: Pull colors from your walls, rug, curtains, and other decorative accents you already have in place. A good rule is to choose two solid-colored pillows and one patterned pillow, or mix solids with subtle textures. Make sure your selections work with your wall art and overall design scheme, not against it.

Our Top Pick

We recommend a set of three 18x18 inch pillows in complementary solids and textures—perhaps a warm linen in your primary color, a textured boucle or linen-cotton blend in a complementary tone, and a subtle patterned pillow that ties together colors from your room. This combination gives you the flexibility to layer and create visual interest without overwhelming your couch. Look for pillows with hidden zippers so you can easily remove and wash covers, extending the life of your investment.

Why This Works for This Situation

Throw pillows are one of the most forgiving decorating decisions you can make because they're not permanent and they're relatively affordable to replace. When you choose pillows for your couch specifically, you're working with a piece of furniture that's likely a significant investment in your home, so you want to enhance—not compete with—that investment. The right pillows should make your couch feel more inviting and intentional, tying together the various elements of your room like wall art, rugs, curtains, and lamps into one cohesive design.

The beauty of using throw pillows to style your couch is that they give you an easy way to refresh your space without redesigning everything. If you swap out your wall art or add new curtains, you can often rotate your throw pillows to keep your room feeling fresh and coordinated. This is why choosing quality, versatile pillows in colors and patterns that complement your existing decorative accents—rather than fighting against them—matters so much.

What to Avoid

  • Too many pillows or mismatched sizes: While layering is great, cramming six pillows on a standard couch looks cluttered and makes it impossible to actually sit down. Stick with 2-4 pillows depending on couch size, and keep them in similar sizes so they look intentional rather than like a random collection.
  • Colors and patterns that clash with your room's design: If your walls are a warm neutral and your rug features cool grays and blues, don't choose throw pillows in warm oranges or hot pinks just because you like them individually. Your pillows should enhance your room's story, not start a new one. Check your existing wall art, curtains, lamps, and other decorative accents before purchasing.
