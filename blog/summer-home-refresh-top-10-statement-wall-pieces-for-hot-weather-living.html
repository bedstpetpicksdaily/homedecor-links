Frequently Asked Questions

What size statement wall art should I choose for my living room?

For most living rooms, oversized canvas prints between 40x60 and 60x90 inches work best as statement pieces. Big Wall Decor offers various sizes to fit different wall spaces and room dimensions. Measure your wall and consider the furniture arrangement before selecting your piece.

Are Big Wall Decor's canvas prints and framed art high quality?

Yes, Big Wall Decor specializes in premium oversized canvas prints and framed art made with high-quality materials designed to last for years. Their large wall art is printed on durable canvas and offers multiple framing options to suit your aesthetic preferences.

What type of abstract art works best for summer home décor?

Cool-toned abstract art with blues, greens, and whites creates a refreshing summer atmosphere, while warm neutrals with pops of color can energize a space. Big Wall Decor's living room wall art and bedroom wall decor collections feature both styles, so you can choose based on your preferred ambiance.

Can I use Big Wall Decor statement pieces in different rooms?

Absolutely! Big Wall Decor's versatile collection works in living rooms, bedrooms, home offices, and entryways. Their oversized canvas prints and framed art are designed to complement various interior styles, making them perfect for any space in your home.

How do I hang and display oversized wall art?

Most of Big Wall Decor's large wall art comes ready to hang with hardware included. Ensure your wall can support the weight, use proper anchors, and hang the piece at eye level (57-60 inches from floor to center). Consider professional installation for very large pieces.
