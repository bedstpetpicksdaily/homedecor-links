Frequently Asked Questions

Can you really put this in a regular household washing machine?

Yes, completely. Separate the rug from the non-slip pad, fold the rug loosely (don't compress it tightly), and wash on cold or warm water with mild detergent. I use a gentle cycle and hang-dry the pad while running the rug on low heat in the dryer for 20 minutes, then air-finishing. Full cycle takes about three hours total, and the rug emerges visibly cleaner. This is not dry-cleaning equivalency—it's actually superior for removing ground-in dirt and pet odors that dry cleaning doesn't fully address.

How does the Ivory hold up with visible dirt and stains compared to gray or charcoal shag?

Ivory shows dust and debris more obviously between washings than darker shades, which is honest to acknowledge. However, the washability mitigates this concern significantly. I vacuum weekly and wash quarterly, and visible dirt never accumulates enough to cause concern. For homes with significant outdoor activity or messy children, charcoal or gray might be psychologically easier, but Ivory's light warmth is worth the slight extra maintenance.

Is this noticeably different quality than other washable shag brands?

Ruggable occupies the premium tier of washable rugs without reaching designer price points. Compared to budget machine-washable alternatives I've tested, this rug's fiber density is noticeably denser, the pile feels more authentic to traditional shag texture, and the backing quality is superior. You do pay for that difference—roughly $100-$150 more than entry-level washable options—but the rug lasts longer and requires less aggressive maintenance to maintain appearance.
