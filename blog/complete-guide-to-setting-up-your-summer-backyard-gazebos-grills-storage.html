Frequently Asked Questions

What size gazebo do I need for my backyard?

Gazebo sizing depends on available space and intended use. For intimate gatherings, 8x8 or 10x10 feet works well. For larger entertaining areas, consider 12x12 or larger. AECOJOY offers multiple sizes and can help determine the perfect fit for your specific space and entertainment needs.

How much outdoor storage space do I really need?

Storage requirements depend on your entertaining style and climate. At minimum, plan for a 4x6 shed to store cushions, toys, and seasonal items. If you have pool equipment, gardening tools, or extensive entertaining supplies, larger 6x8 or 8x10 structures are more practical. AECOJOY's range of outdoor storage sheds accommodates various needs.
