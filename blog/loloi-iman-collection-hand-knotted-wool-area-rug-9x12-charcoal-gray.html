Frequently Asked Questions

How much does the Loloi Iman rug actually shed, and for how long?

Expect noticeable shedding for the first 3-6 months—this is normal with hand-knotted wool and isn't a manufacturing defect. Vacuum 2-3 times weekly during break-in, and the shedding tapers significantly. After 6 months, you'll see minimal additional shedding. Synthetic rugs don't shed, so if you have severe allergies or hatred of vacuuming, this matters.

Is hand-knotted wool worth the extra $300-400 compared to machine-made alternatives?

Yes, but only if you're keeping the rug for at least 10 years. Hand-knotted wool lasts 15-20+ years with normal use; machine-made polypropylene lasts 7-10 years. Do the math: a $600 hand-knotted rug costs roughly $30-40/year; a $250 machine-made rug costs $25-35/year. The cost-per-year difference is minimal if you keep it long-term. But if you replace rugs every 5-7 years, buy the cheaper option.

Does charcoal gray show dirt and stains easily?

Charcoal gray is actually one of the best colors for hiding dirt—it's dark enough to camouflage dust and pet hair but not so dark it looks oppressive. Light-colored rugs (cream, light gray, natural) show every speck. Dark reds or blues can show lint dramatically. Charcoal is genuinely practical for families. That said, any wool rug requires prompt spot-cleaning for wine, coffee, or pet accidents—wool absorbs liquids faster than synthetic.
