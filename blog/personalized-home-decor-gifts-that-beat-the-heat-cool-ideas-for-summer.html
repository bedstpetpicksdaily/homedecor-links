What makes personalized home decor gifts better than generic options?

Personalized gifts demonstrate thought and effort, creating emotional connections that generic items cannot achieve. They transform homes into spaces that reflect individual personalities and cherished memories, making them far more meaningful and memorable.

How long does it take to receive personalized gifts from GiftLab?

Production times vary depending on the item and customization complexity. Most personalized orders typically take 5-10 business days. We recommend ordering in advance, especially during peak summer season, to ensure timely delivery.

Can I return or modify a personalized gift order?

Since personalized items are custom-made to your specifications, return policies may differ from standard merchandise. Check GiftLab's specific return and modification policies during checkout, as most custom orders cannot be altered once production begins.

What types of photos work best for custom photo gifts?

High-resolution photos (minimum 300 DPI) with clear subjects produce the best results. Professional photos, candid moments, and colorful images typically display beautifully. GiftLab provides guidelines during the customization process to help you select or optimize your photos.

Are GiftLab's personalized gifts suitable for outdoor summer use?

Many GiftLab items are designed with durable, weather-resistant materials suitable for summer spaces. However, some delicate items may require indoor placement. Always check product descriptions to confirm whether items can withstand outdoor exposure to sun, moisture, and temperature changes.
