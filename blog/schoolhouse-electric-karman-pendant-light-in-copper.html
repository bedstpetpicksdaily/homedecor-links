Frequently Asked Questions

Will the copper finish tarnish or look dull after a year?

The Karman develops a natural patina over time, which many designers actually prefer—it looks intentional and vintage rather than brand-new and sterile. If you want to maintain a brighter copper finish, occasional polishing with a soft cloth keeps it looking fresh. The key is that it won't corrode or flake off; the tarnishing is cosmetic and part of the material's aging process. Most owners report they prefer how it looks after 6-12 months of natural patina development.

How much space do I need between the pendant and my kitchen island or dining table?

The Karman measures approximately 8-10 inches in diameter depending on the exact model, so you want at least 12-14 inches of clearance above head height for safety and sightline comfort. For a standard 36-inch-high island with 8-foot ceilings, hanging the fixture 30-36 inches above the surface is ideal. If you're unsure about your specific space, take measurements before ordering—this isn't adjustable after installation.

Is this better than cheaper brass pendant lights you see everywhere?

The Karman occupies a middle ground: it's more thoughtfully designed and durable than mass-market options ($30-80 pendants), but it's also not a $600+ artisan fixture. You're paying for design quality and material integrity without the designer premium. If you compare it directly to similarly priced brass or copper pendants from other quality makers, the Karman holds its own on durability and aesthetic longevity—the 4.3-star rating from 500+ reviews backs this up. The value proposition is solid if you're willing to invest in something that'll last through multiple décor rotations.
