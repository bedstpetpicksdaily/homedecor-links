Consider Your Bedroom's Color Palette

Before selecting any wall art, take a moment to analyze your bedroom's existing color scheme. Your new artwork should either complement your current palette or serve as a bold focal point that anchors the room's design. If your bedroom features neutral tones—whites, grays, and beiges—you have flexibility to choose vibrant oversized canvas prints that add personality. Conversely, if you already have colorful bedding or furniture, consider selecting art that picks up one or two colors from your existing décor to create visual harmony.

Big Wall Decor offers an extensive range of color options in their large wall art collection, making it easy to find pieces that seamlessly integrate with your bedroom's aesthetic. Their abstract art selections are particularly versatile, working with both contemporary and traditional bedroom designs.

Determine the Right Size and Placement

One common mistake homeowners make is choosing wall art that's too small for their space. Bedroom wall decor should have presence without overwhelming the room. A general rule of thumb: your artwork should take up between one-third and one-half of the wall space above furniture like dressers or nightstands. For a dramatic effect, consider statement wall pieces that span most of your wall—this is where Big Wall Decor's oversized canvas prints truly shine, creating an instant focal point that draws the eye.

Placement matters equally. Position artwork at eye level when seated, typically 57-60 inches from the floor to the center of the piece. This creates visual balance and ensures your wall art complements rather than dominates the room's layout.

Choose Art That Reflects Your Personal Style

Your bedroom wall art should tell your story. Whether you're drawn to minimalist abstract art, serene landscapes, modern geometric patterns, or inspirational quotes, Big Wall Decor's diverse collection ensures you'll find pieces that resonate with your aesthetic. Consider your lifestyle too—if you're someone who changes décor seasonally, you might prefer modular framed art that's easy to swap. If you're all-in on a particular style, a large statement piece from their oversized canvas print collection offers long-term visual interest.

Frequently Asked Questions

What size wall art should I choose for my bedroom?

A good rule is to select artwork that covers one-third to one-half of the wall space above furniture. For most bedrooms, oversized canvas prints ranging from 40x60 inches to 60x80 inches create significant impact without overwhelming the space. Big Wall Decor offers a variety of sizes to fit different wall dimensions.

How do I hang large wall art safely?

For oversized canvas prints and statement pieces, use appropriate wall anchors rated for the artwork's weight. Consider the wall type—drywall, plaster, or brick each require different installation methods. For valuable pieces from Big Wall Decor, professional installation is often recommended to ensure proper hanging and safety.

Can abstract art work in any bedroom style?

Absolutely! Abstract art is incredibly versatile and works well with contemporary, minimalist, traditional, and eclectic bedroom styles. The key is selecting abstract pieces that complement your color palette and personal aesthetic. Big Wall Decor's abstract art collection features options suitable for virtually any design preference.

Should bedroom wall art match my bedding?

Not necessarily. Your wall art can either complement your bedding by picking up accent colors, or it can contrast to create visual interest. The most important thing is that all elements work together harmoniously. Big Wall Decor's large wall art selection makes it easy to find pieces that coordinate with your existing décor.

How do I choose between canvas prints and framed art for my bedroom?

Canvas prints offer a modern, gallery-like aesthetic and work well for oversized pieces. Framed art provides a more traditional look and offers additional design flexibility through frame selection. Consider your bedroom style and wall space—Big Wall Decor offers both options, so you can find what works best for your space.
