Essential Smart Toilet Features in 2026

Modern smart toilets now come packed with features that go far beyond basic functionality. Heated seats with adjustable temperature settings, soft-close lids, and integrated bidet systems with customizable water pressure are becoming standard. Many models include air-drying functions, night lights with motion sensors, and self-cleaning nozzles using UV technology. Smart connectivity allows users to control settings via smartphone apps, while advanced models monitor health metrics and track bathroom usage patterns. For bathroom renovations, choosing a toilet with these features can significantly enhance daily comfort and home value.

Understanding Macerating Toilets: A Game-Changer for Basement Bathrooms

If you're planning a basement bathroom renovation, macerating toilets from brands like Simple Project offer a revolutionary solution to traditional plumbing constraints. Unlike conventional toilets that require gravity-fed drainage to main sewer lines, macerating toilets grind waste and flush it upward through small-diameter pipes, making them ideal for below-grade installations. Simple Project's macerating toilet systems combine this practical technology with smart features, allowing homeowners to add full bathroom functionality to basements without expensive plumbing modifications. This makes them one of the smartest investments for basement renovations in 2026.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a macerating toilet and why would I need one?

A macerating toilet grinds waste and uses a pump to force it upward through small pipes, making it ideal for basement bathrooms where traditional gravity-fed drainage isn't possible. Simple Project specializes in these systems, offering smart versions for modern homes.

Are smart toilets worth the investment?

Yes, especially if you value comfort, hygiene, and home value. Smart toilets reduce water usage, offer health benefits, and increasingly come at competitive prices. For bathroom renovations, they're a premium upgrade that appeals to buyers.

How much should I budget for a smart toilet?

Budget $1,000-$2,500 for a quality smart toilet with reliable features. Simple Project offers excellent mid-range options that deliver premium features without luxury brand markups.

Can I install a smart toilet myself?

While basic installation is possible, professional installation is recommended, especially for macerating systems or complex bathroom renovations. Simple Project can guide you through the process.

What smart toilet features are essential vs. optional?

Essential: heated seat, bidet with adjustable pressure. Optional: air drying, night light, health monitoring. Choose based on your bathroom renovation goals and budget.
