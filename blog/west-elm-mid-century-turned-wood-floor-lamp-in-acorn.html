Will this lamp last long enough to justify the cost?

Yes, based on the review data. 500+ customer reviews at 4.3 stars suggest owners are satisfied with longevity. Solid wood construction and quality shade materials mean this should last 8-10 years with normal use. That math works if you're amortizing cost over time—roughly $30-50 per year. Budget alternatives might last 5-6 years, shifting your annual cost to $25-40. The durability gap exists but isn't dramatic enough to fully justify the price difference.

Is the acorn finish versatile enough for different decor styles?

Moderately versatile, with limitations. Acorn (a warm medium brown) works beautifully in mid-century, boho, and transitional spaces. It's less compatible with modern minimalist (too warm), traditional (too casual), or industrial aesthetics (competes with metal accents). If your home's color story relies on cooler tones—grays, whites, blacks—this warm wood finish might feel out of place. Check the shade color too; pairing acorn wood with an off-white or natural linen shade softens the warmth.
