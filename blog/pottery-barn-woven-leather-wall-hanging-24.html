Frequently Asked Questions

How does this compare to cheaper woven leather wall hangings under $100?

Cheaper alternatives use thinner leather and looser weaves that sag slightly over time. The Pottery Barn version uses genuinely supple leather that holds its structure for years. You're paying for longevity—in this case, legitimately. However, if you rotate décor seasonally or plan to replace this in 3–5 years anyway, the cheaper option isn't a bad call. Just expect to rebuy it once.

Is 24 inches the right size for an average apartment?

Yes, for most spaces. At 24 inches, it works as a standalone focal point above a console table, or as part of a gallery wall arrangement. Anything smaller (12–18 inches) reads as decorative filler; anything larger (30+ inches) demands a dedicated wall. If your room has 8-foot ceilings, 24 inches is the maximum before it feels cramped. Measure your wall space first—don't guess.

Does the leather smell fade, and is it safe for bedrooms?

Genuine leather has a subtle natural smell that fades within 1–2 weeks with normal ventilation. Unlike synthetic woven wall pieces, you won't get chemical off-gassing or that plasticky odor that lingers. It's absolutely bedroom-safe and won't interfere with sleep quality. Air out your space normally, and you won't notice a thing.

What if I need to remove it later—does it damage walls?

The included hardware uses standard wall anchors that leave small holes you can easily patch with spackle. Removing it from drywall takes two minutes and leaves negligible damage. If you're renting, talk to your landlord first—most allow this type of installation, but policies vary.

Is July a good time to buy this, or should I wait for sales?

July is actually solid timing. Pottery Barn runs summer clearance sales mid-month, and wall décor pieces sometimes drop 15–25% off regular pricing. If you see this for under $160, that's a legitimately good deal. Black Friday (November) typically offers deeper discounts (20–30%), but if you're refreshing your space for fall entertaining, don't wait—grab it now if it's on sale.
