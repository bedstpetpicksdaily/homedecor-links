Photo flipbooks are a tactile, nostalgic alternative to digital photo albums—small bound booklets where sequential images create an animated effect when you flip through the pages quickly. They work well for anniversaries, weddings, baby milestones, travel adventures, proposals, or capturing a child's growth over time.

Unlike scrolling through photos on your phone, flipbooks offer an interactive, memorable experience that feels tangible and intentional. If you're considering creating one, the main decision is choosing a platform that matches your design comfort level, budget, and customization needs.

This guide walks you through the process of creating a custom photo flipbook, what to expect during production, and how to get the best results from your finished keepsake.

There's no strict minimum, but most flipbooks work best with at least 10–20 photos arranged in sequence. GiftLab allows flexibility in photo count, so you can create a shorter, more intimate flipbook or a longer narrative experience depending on your goal.

GiftLab accepts common digital image formats for photo uploads. Check their platform for specific technical specs (resolution, file size limits) before uploading to avoid compression or quality loss.
