Frequently Asked Questions

Does this rug work in bedrooms or just living rooms?

Absolutely works in bedrooms. The 5x8 size fits perfectly under most king beds with substantial overhang, and the muted rust tones pair naturally with both neutral and jewel-toned bedding. I tested placement under a bed in a guest room, and the rug's thickness (approximately 0.5 inches) provides nice underfoot cushioning without creating tripping hazards at the frame edge.

How much does this rug actually shed, and for how long?

Expect noticeable shedding for 2-3 weeks after arrival. Vacuum thoroughly every 2-3 days during this period. After the initial settling phase, shedding drops to minimal levels consistent with any quality wool rug. The dense hand-tufting means loose fibers are surface debris, not structural concerns. By week four, vacuuming once weekly handles any remaining loose fibers completely.

Is hand-tufted wool worth it over synthetic alternatives?

Yes, for long-term value. Hand-tufted wool costs more upfront but outlasts synthetic rugs by 8-10 years. Wool naturally resists staining, doesn't trap dust mites in fibers like polypropylene, and actually softens with age. If you're buying a rug for 10+ years of use, wool's per-year cost beats cheaper synthetics that need replacement within 3-5 years. For temporary decor in rental situations, synthetic makes financial sense.

Can you use this rug with pets and kids?

Yes, though manage expectations. Wool's natural resilience handles pet traffic well—fur doesn't embed like it does in synthetics. Spills require faster cleanup than with stain-treated synthetic rugs. Treat with a wool-safe rug protectant ($30-50) before pets arrive to add a protective layer. In my three-month test with two cats, the rug maintained appearance admirably without protective treatment, though that protective spray would have been smart insurance.
